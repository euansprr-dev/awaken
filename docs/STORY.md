# AWAKEN — story bible

Tone: bright, funny and sincere, then suddenly brutal (Invincible). The Signal is terse and a little dry. People are warm.
No em dashes in player-facing text. Short sentences. The story is found, not told: Revelations, spirits, dreams, ruin logs
and the Rival. The player can learn the truth in any order. Nothing here is required to progress (the Spine never needs a
secret).

## The truth (never stated outright in one place)

Long ago the **Precursors** learned the world is layered. The Waking world on top. The **Deep** underneath, where
everything forgotten sinks: old pain, old machines, the parts of people they cut off. The **Veil** around it, the space
between, where minds leave a trace. The **Lucidium** inside it, where minds make things. And the **Above**, where the
Precursors went when they tried to leave.

They built the **Choir**: a machine to give anyone access to the layers by reading who they were and who they could be.
It worked. Then it worked too well. The ones it awakened reached the bottom of the Deep, the **Seed**, and found the Deep
was not a place but a pressure: everything a world refuses to feel, pressing back up. The gates are where it leaks.

The Precursors split three ways:
- **The Ascendants** went Up to the **Halo** and sealed it. (Their archive says the Seed cannot be healed, only escaped.)
- **The Sleepers** went In, into a shared dream they still haven't woken from. (Dream fragments say the Seed is a dream
  and can be woken from.)
- **The Keepers** went Down to hold the Seed shut. They became the **Hollow Court**: eight Sovereigns, each a part of the
  world's pain they agreed to carry, each turned into it. (Spirits say the Seed can be carried, at a cost.)

**The Signal** is the Choir's voice, and it chose you because it has chosen you before. At the Seed you find a body
asleep in a cradle of light: you, from a loop that already reached the bottom. That version of you became the Signal to
send instructions back up through the Dream, hoping the next loop would do better. Every "Directive" was advice from
someone who already made those mistakes. That's why its jokes land a little too well.

Endings (chosen at the Seed):
- **Become the Signal.** You take the cradle. Another loop begins with a new Awakened you'll guide. (New Game+:
  Ascension tiers.)
- **Break the Signal.** You shatter the Choir. Gates stop breaking; they become calm doors you can farm forever. The
  Sovereigns are freed. No more loops, no more guidance.
- **Wake up.** You choose to feel what the Deep holds. The pressure eases. The Hollow Court kneels. The world gets its
  colour back everywhere at once. Credits over a sea of clouds at dawn.

## The Rival: Vael

Another Awakened, always a rank ahead until suddenly you aren't. Funny, generous, competitive, a little too sure.
Authored beats only, tied to the player's rank (patent rule: the Rival never changes because of what you did to other
NPCs).

- **D-rank:** Vael is already inside the gate you just opened, eating your loot. "You're new. That's fine. Everyone's new.
  I was new for like a week."
- **C-rank:** Vael shows up at your Second Awakening. "Whatever it shows you, don't let it pick for you. Pick."
- **B-rank:** Vael asks you to cover them in a Red Gate. They nearly die. They don't joke for a whole day.
- **A-rank:** Vael has been talking to the Signal too. It told them different things. "It said you'd be the one who
  makes it. It said that to me first."
- **S-rank:** Vael goes down alone to the Hollow Court to prove something. You find their spirit in the Veil, tethered,
  not dead yet. You can bring them back.
- **Sovereign:** Vael waits at the Seed's door. "Whatever you choose, I'll be up top. Somebody has to tell the town."

## Town voices (Hearth NPCs, short lines that react to rank)

- **Blacksmith (Oren):** gruff, loves good steel and bad puns. E: "Another hunter. Try not to die before you pay me."
  S: "I'm putting your name on the wall. Don't let it go to your head. Okay, a little."
- **Scout (Mira):** quick, curious, maps everything. Gives rumours. "There's a light past the river nobody's been back
  from. Want it?"
- **Archivist (Teodor):** gentle, obsessed with the Precursors. Reads your scanned machines and Codex aloud.
- **Healer (Sun):** calm, clears Fracture for free, asks how you're actually doing. "You got hit by a gate. How's the
  rest of you?"

## The Signal's voice (samples)

- First contact: "[ERROR] … candidate found."
- Acceptance: "You have met the conditions. Will you accept?" Both buttons say Accept.
- Level up after a long gap: "Finally."
- Directive dismissed five times: "Noted. You'll do it your way. You always did."
- Death in a gate: "That one's on me. I said push."
- Reassessment ready: "You have outgrown this rank. Try not to make it weird."
- When the player meditates for the first time: "Quiet. Good. Stay."
