# Feel session 1: the swing (about 10 minutes)

Plan decision 5: three short hands-on sessions (swing, glide, flight). This is the first one, for the rope. It also
lets you try the glide and flight if there's time. One question matters most: **does the swing feel alive, or stiff?**

## Start
1. `cd /Volumes/MEDIA/Awaken && tools/play.sh --pack` (build/awaken-v1).
2. Singleplayer → Create New World → Survival, **Allow Commands: On**, any seed.
3. In the world, type in chat, one at a time:
   - `/awaken admin awaken` (skips the prologue; choose any starter power on the screen that opens)
   - `/awaken ascent sandbox`: builds a launch tower where you stand, a lane of giant trees in front of it, and an
     updraft pillar to its side. It also gives you every tier: rope, leaper, wings, flight.

You're on the tower's deck, facing the lane.

## Keys
| | |
|---|---|
| **Left Alt** (hold) | Throw the rope and keep holding it. Aim a little above a tree trunk; it helps your aim. |
| Let go of Left Alt | Fly on with the swing's speed. Letting go near the top of the forward swing gives a bonus. |
| **Space** on the rope | Launch off it: up and faster. |
| **W** on the rope | Pump at the bottom of the swing (like a playground swing). A / D steer the arc, Shift reels in. |
| Space in the air | Double jump. A second Space opens the **wings**. |
| Sprint in the air | Air dash. Shift high up: dive slam. |
| Space while gliding | Take **flight**. Then W / Sprint go faster, Space climbs, Shift sinks. |
| Double-tap A or D in flight | Barrel roll. The gold line over the XP bar is flight's meter (Aether). The sun and updrafts refill it. |

## What to try
1. **Swing down the lane (5 min).** Jump off the deck and throw the rope at the first trunk. Pump with W through the
   bottom, and let go as you rise; before you land, throw it again at the next trunk. Try to cross the whole lane
   without touching the ground. Try Space (launch) instead of letting go.
2. **Glide (2 min).** From the deck: Space, Space (wings), then pitch down hard until the sonic boom, and pull up.
   Glide through the column of rising air over the pillar beside the tower.
3. **Flight (2 min).** Glide, Space to fly, Sprint to boost, double-tap A/D to roll, then stop pressing W to hover.
4. **Optional, the Tether Vow.** `/awaken ascent grant none`, then `/awaken ascent vow goto`. A real vault gives you
   the rope the way a new world will.

## Comfort
Mods → Awaken → Config → client: `cameraRoll` (0-7.5°, default 5), `speedFov` (0-1), `movementShake`,
`reducedMotion`. The file is `run-pack/config/awaken-feel-client.toml`.
