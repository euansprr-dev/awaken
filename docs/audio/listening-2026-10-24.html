The Sound: listening

AWAKEN · Awakened Pack stage 4 · 2026-10-24 · layered gate music: 14 themes, 56 new stems

About 16 minutes. For each gate theme, play the mix: a minute and ten seconds that walks through every level the way the game does, with the game's own fades. The line above it says what the theme is for: does it sound like that, rise with the danger and settle after? Mark it keep, remake or cut, and say why in the note when it isn't a keep. The strip under the player shows the level you're hearing; click one to jump to it.

The 56 layers under the cards are every new sound in this stage. A layer you leave unmarked takes its mix's verdict (shown dashed), so fourteen marks decide them all; open the layers only when one part bothers you, and mark that one on its own.

  • 0:00 calm (the bed fades in)
  • 0:12 a monster hunts you
  • 0:24 blows traded
  • 0:38 the Lord
  • 0:52 the Lord falls
  • 1:00 calm again

In the game: inside a gate the music becomes four stems played together. The bed while you explore; a tension layer when something hunts you or the gate's pressure rises; drums and bass in a fight; the theme's tune stated big for a Lord. It follows the Music slider, never plays over vanilla's music, rests after two and a half minutes of calm, and fades on the way out and on death. The System's voice, the UI, the music and the Ritual's voices stay dry under Sound Physics Remastered; everything else gets the room it's in (that part is heard in game, not here).

Your choices stay in this browser. When you're done, Export and send me the file (or the text).
