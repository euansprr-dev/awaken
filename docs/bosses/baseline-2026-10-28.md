# Bosses, the baseline before the rework (2026-10-28)

The numbers today's three kit bosses score on the R0 tools (SPEC §35.9), so the rework can be measured against them.
Made with `python3 tools/art/specs/kit_bodies.py motion DIR` and
`python3 tools/sim/combat_sim.py --kit --preset recommended-hp --cadence` (a parrying player, 400 seeds).

## Fight cadence (the sim)
The sim doesn't model walking, so "idle" is time the boss stands between moves.

| Boss | Threat | Recover | Idle | Show | Opened | Blows a minute | Still, median / p90 | Single-blow phrases |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ember Tyrant | 40% | 23% | 14% | 6% | 16% | 22.1 | 0.8 / 0.8 s | 80% |
| Unit Zero | 40% | 20% | 18% | 7% | 15% | 25.9 | 1.1 / 1.8 s | 68% |
| Mother of Spores | 41% | 25% | 16% | 7% | 12% | 23.6 | 0.8 / 0.8 s | 82% |

What it says: four blows in five are a lone blow followed by a long recovery and a stand. That is the hit-block loop
Euan felt. The rework's target is phrases of 3-5 blows with movement between them, and recovery under 15%.

## Motion (the meter)
Per move: kinks (abrupt speed changes, over 3° a frame), the worst one in degrees a frame, the held share (most posed
bones still), and still seconds (the whole body still for 0.15 s or more).

| Boss | Blows: kinks / worst | Roars and casts: held | Still spells | Strings: dip to rest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ember Tyrant | 11-29 / 12.2 (chop) | roar 40%, stoke 35%, meltdown 36% | roar 0.30 s, meltdown 0.48 s | 100% on all five |
| Unit Zero | 3-40 / 45.3 (zero point), 27.2 (lance) | deploy 29%, wake 27% | deploy 0.50 s | 100% on all four |
| Mother of Spores | 2-59 / 17.0 (lash) | unfurl 26%, rain 28%, birth 26% | bloom 0.47 s, lob x3 0.50 s | 100% on all five |

What it says:
- **Blows are not frozen** (held 0-11%, no still spells). Their fault is kinks: speed jumps where one stage clip hands
  to the next, worst at Unit Zero's Zero Point and lance and Mother's lash.
- **Roars, casts and transitions do hold** a pose for a quarter to two fifths of their time.
- **Every string drops all the way back to rest between blows** (dip 100%). That is the snap Euan saw.

The rework's targets: no kink over 4° a frame, held under 15% on any move, dips under 40% inside a phrase.
