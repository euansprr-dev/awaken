# Bosses R2, the motion kit: measured (2026-10-28)

The motion kit (SPEC §35.10) against the same bodies before it (commit 3488e8d5, the Living Forge already in place),
on the R0 meter. The meter now plays the game's way (one clip, links, the transition spring, the springs) and splits
kinks: **body kink** is the largest change of a body bone's speed outside the strike (wind-up, hold, follow-through,
recovery and every hand-over), in degrees a frame at 60 fps; springs (chain, tassets, veils, the halo) are counted
apart. **Strike** is the largest change of speed inside a strike, where a fast blow has to snap: reported, not a fault.
Before, the old meter's 9-12° "kinks" sat mostly at the stage hand-overs at the peak and contact, which now lie inside
one curve.

Made with `python3 tools/art/specs/kit_bodies.py motion DIR --no-gif` (before: the same on 3488e8d5's tools, with the
same strike split).

## Unit Zero and Mother of Spores (their own passes are R4 and R5)
- **Strings:** dip 100% → 46-48% (Unit Zero), 36-43% (Mother's lashes), 69% (Mother's lobs).
- **Body kink:** at most 4.6 on Unit Zero's cuts. Its step, spin and orbital have 6-9 (its rig's own keys); Mother's
  halo move has 7.1.
- **Ornaments:** Unit Zero's halo spins up and down fast (up to 34° a frame of spin change on the lance); counted
  with the springs.
- **Held poses:** fall on most casts (Mother's birth 26% → 2%, swoop 11% → 2%). Unit Zero's wake and deploy and
  Mother's rain and unfurl still hold 29-35%: their keys are long holds by design, for R4 and R5.

## What it says
- **Hand-overs are smooth.** Every body bone of the Forge stays under 4° a frame outside its strikes, in every move
  and string. There is one frame of 4.2 where the hammer links into the stomp.
- **Strings flow.** The next blow starts from where the last one ended. The worst fall back toward rest is 26-43% on
  the Forge, from 100%.
- **Roars and casts stopped freezing.**
- **Strikes snap harder** (Forge 12 → 16 at most). A strike now leaves its peak from almost still and reaches full
  speed inside its own ticks, then carries through contact instead of stopping dead. Its top speed is higher (the
  chop 1,561 → 1,962°/s). Whether that reads as weight or as a jerk is for Euan's eye in game.
- **The fight sim** (`--cadence`, 200 seeds): links trim the Forge's recovery share from 23% to 18-22%. Phrase
  lengths don't move yet; that is R3's fight scripts.

## After Euan's look (later on 2026-10-28)
Euan, on the preview: the slabs on its back glitched as it moved, and it hit "as if he had two hands instead of one
axe in one hand", with the axe going through the ground.
- **The back.** The carapace slabs' 6 Hz springs were unstable on whole-frame steps at 20 fps. They now step in slices
  of at most 1/120 s, in the game and in the Lab, and stay steady at 20, 30 and 60 fps.
- **One hand, one axe.** The chops are now a one-handed cleave and a one-handed sweep. Magma Fist and Eruption are the
  fist alone. The axe rests at 84° (it was 66°, buried 1.2 blocks at rest).
- **The ground.** No clip but the fall puts the axe more than 2 px into the ground, checked every tick (the build
  fails otherwise). It was as deep as 3.7 blocks in the cleave's follow-through.
