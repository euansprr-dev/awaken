# The combat sim with the move tables (2026-10-28)

`tools/sim/combat_sim.py` now fights a kit Lord as `FightRunner` does (SPEC §34.3): its fight script picks moves by
weight, `when` and range; each move winds up (a HEAVY is held 1 in 3), strikes with its capsules where they reach,
fires its ability under the move's own class, and recovers; phases enter through their transition move (shielded), and
a posture break ends the move and its string. `--override kit.on=0` fights the same Lord the old way (AbilityRunner and
ToldMelee), to compare. What the sim cannot know (where you stand) is ASSUMED in CONFIG `kit`: in reach 0.3-2.0 blocks
from its edge, behind it 15% of that time, out of reach 3-12; a kit flier 1 block out or 6 (its FlyHunt orbit).

Targets (docs/combat/PLAN.md §6.1): Tank wins 10% or less; Dodger 60%+ in 80-120 s; Parrier 80%+ in 80-120 s;
Expert 98%+ in 75 s or less with half its health left. `*` = misses a target. 400 seeds a cell, forged Longsword on
level.

## What the sim found in the game, now fixed (SPEC §34.3)

- **A move's ability struck under its own class.** The hammer told a HEAVY (guard it), but its Magma Fist landed
  PERILOUS, so a player who guarded as told was punished. Now an ability fires under its move's class.
- **A summon was always usable.** At 3-8 blocks only Deploy Coils reached, so Unit Zero replayed it (32 times a fight
  in the sim). Now a move whose ability summons, decoys or heals needs the same room the AbilityRunner checks.
- **It stopped walking 3.5 blocks out.** Unit Zero's cuts reach 1.6, so it stood idle in front of a long-reach player.
  Now it walks in to where its close moves reach.
- **A posture break didn't end a kit move.** Now it does, with its string, as it ends an ability's wind-up.
- **Mother still dived and hovered 2-5 blocks up**, so her lashes couldn't reach. Now a kit flier holds still for a move
  and orbits low between moves (1 block out, or 6), with no dives of its own.

## The kit's tuning (in the bosses' specs, `tools/art/specs/kit/bosses/`)

- **Capsules stay light when the ability is the blow.** Two moves dealt their damage twice: the hammer's fist (1.4)
  plus its ring (1.5), and the eruption's burst (1.8) plus its rain. Now the hammer is 0.7, the eruption 0.8 and Phase
  Step 0.85.
- **Summons come with phase 2**, as in GateBestiary: Deploy Coils and Birth are out of the first pools.
- **Middle range has its own moves.** Unit Zero's lance fires from 4 blocks in its first two phases, Mother's lob (from
  4) and swoop (from 5) need no `far`, and Spore Rain is weight 1 everywhere. Before, a signature filled the gap, and
  Mother cast Spore Rain 7 times a fight.
- **Spore Halo is a LIGHT**, like the bolts it throws.
- **Unit Zero's cuts:** sever_a and sever_b 1.75, sever_c 2.5, spin 1.5. Its told melee had dealt half again as much as
  the cuts first did, and a Tank won 95%.
- **Health under the curve:** the Tyrant 30.5 (the old recommendation was 30), Unit Zero 30 (was 32.5), Mother 22.5
  (was 22).

## Not covered

The sim has no positions, so the capsules' geometry is reduced to front and back reach. It doesn't punish the
recovery, and doesn't model line of sight. The feel of the fights, their tells and their timing are for Euan's look in
game (`/awaken bosses studio <id>`, then a real fight).
