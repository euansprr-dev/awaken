Carillon · Milestone 5 · concept for Euan

The Atlas

The world as a model on a table. Lift your wrist and the Holo lays out everything you've walked: real voxel terrain with depth and shadow, under clouds that hide only what you haven't seen yet. Carillon sits in the middle like a bronze-and-slate miniature, and every bell you climb blows the clouds off a district.

ATLAS · yaw 30° · tilt 52°

A browser sketch of the look and the moments, not the game's renderer. Drag to orbit and tilt, scroll to zoom. The game draws the same idea from real sampled columns, with real block colours, at a 2 ms budget.

Six moments

The table prints

Press M. The Holo's light spills off the wristband and the land rises out of the glass in a ring that starts under your feet and runs outward, column by column, like a hologram printing. Clouds roll in from the rim last. About 0.6 s, and it never waits on loading: the mesh is kept warm while the Atlas is shut.

Per-slice rise in the model matrix, delayed by distance. Costs nothing extra.

A living model

The table follows the real sky. By day there's a low west sun with cast shadows. At night the land goes blue and Carillon's windows and lamps light up as tiny warm points. Cloud shadows slide over the ground at the explored edge, and water shimmers.

A lit bit per column, from emissive top blocks. Glow points drawn in the same pass.

Clouds part as you walk

Everything within about 64 blocks of your path is known. Leave the Atlas open while you walk and you watch the blanket thin and lift over the ground you've just seen. Everything else stays under soft, sunlit cumulus, never a black fog of war.

Server bits, 1 per 16×16 cell. New cells fade their puffs out over a second.

Bells clear districts

Climb a district's bell tower and ring its bell (or just stand in the belfry), and the clouds blow off the whole district in a ring that spreads from the tower. The Choir spire's great bell clears all of Carillon. The city's towers become the reason to climb.

Bell positions from the city plan. District shapes from CityHooks.district.

The Registry reveal

At the Registry, Bram tells you the Association keeps a map of every runner. The Atlas opens on its own, high over the city, and your opening run draws itself across the rooftops as a glowing bronze thread, from the Roost to Choir Square, with the clouds already gone where you ran.

The server records the run's path (≤ 400 points). Plays once, then lives on as a faint thread.

The wrist glance

In the K menu, the Atlas is a small live puck: the land around you, tilted, turning with your facing, with your pin at the centre. It's cached as one texture and redrawn at most once a second, so it costs almost nothing. Click it and the full table opens.

UiCanvas.texture on the Holo panel, ≤ 0.3 ms.

Only what you know

Every marker passes one test: has this player explored that cell, or been told about it? Markers stand on the terrain, hidden when a ridge is in the way, and their labels fan out so they never pile up.

Places

Carillon's districts and landmarks in spaced small caps when you zoom in. Region names from the Living Wilds lettered across the land when you zoom out, the way old maps label a country.

Rumours

A rumour you've heard is an amber "?" hanging in the clouds over the region, because by definition you haven't seen it yet. Go there and the "?" becomes the thing.

Gates, lairs, your Hearth

Gate pillars you've seen are violet light columns in their rank's colour. Lairs, landmarks and your Hearth come through the existing MapMarks. Party members show as small lit pins; later, a shared pin you drop on the table.

Phases

Phase 1
building now
  • Server discovery: 1 bit per 16×16 cell per dimension, an attachment with schemaVersion. Clears about 64 blocks round your path. Bells clear districts and the spire clears the city. AtlasHooks.reveal and explored.
  • Sync to the owner in batches. Party sharing on request.
  • Client tile cache in awaken/atlas/<world>/<dim>/: versioned, deflated, ≤ 64 MB, oldest first, with a "Clear Atlas cache" button.
  • The renderer from spike S5, with its memory bounded: a mesh window round the view, coarser levels when zoomed out, and meshing on a worker thread.
  • The table view on M, in Holo glass, with markers from MapMarks.

Choices for you

I'm building on the recommendation for each of these. Each is cheap to change, so say if one is wrong.

Which key opens the table?
M, the key Xaero's used. The glance stays in the K menu.
How does a bell clear its district?
Ringing the bell or standing in the belfry both count. Ringing is the deed. Standing there means nobody misses it.
Should the Atlas pop open by itself at the Registry?
Yes, once, right after Bram's line about the Association's map, before the first Directive. If the opening is skipped, the reveal plays the first time you press M.
What happens inside a gate?
The Atlas is shut there ("The Atlas can't see inside a gate"). Gates are learned by walking and by Perception, as now.
What does the Nether show?
Phase 3: sample the cave floor below your height instead of the bedrock roof. Until then, the Nether is drawn only where you've walked, from the ground you stood on.
Should the table follow day and night?
Follow the real sky, so the city lights up at night. A setting can keep it always day.
What should the Gate Map tab become?
Rename it "Atlas": the glance on top and the gate list under it. This touches the gates module, so it goes through the orchestrator.
