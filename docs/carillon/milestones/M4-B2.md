## The honest read: "Carillon isn't flat any more"

Yes from the street and the roofs; only partly from the sky.
- **The street has levels.** Lanes on the Knoll climb in flights a house apart, and the old town's roofs climb to the
  tower: its top ring of houses stands 8 up with 4-5 storeys (`pair_roofs_bellwatch`). The square is an amphitheatre:
  three rings of andesite steps round the Bell Stone with the crowd in them (`b2_bowl`). The Terrace is a wall you climb:
  12 high, banded in andesite, a drainpipe every 12 and the parapet open over each (`b2_terrace_wall`); its stairs are
  real flights. The canal sits a level down with its towpath. The walls between levels (3,568 cell edges of 2+) give
  the backs of the blocks a stepped, built-up look.
- **From the sky the change is quieter than the word "flat" deserves.** Steps of 2 and a Terrace 4 higher are small
  against houses 9-25 high: `pair_sky_nw_low` shows the bowl and the Terrace's stair but not much of the Knoll. The bible's
  Knoll top (+8) is honest but modest; if Euan wants the old town to read as a hill from the Roost, two more rings (+10,
  +12) are a plan change, not code.
- **Short of the brief**: setbacks are only the Terrace's walk (no stepped upper storeys in the grammar; towers get
  stepped shoulders from `chain()` instead); of 20 crossings only the 7 walkways are visible until B3 hangs the 13 lines;
  the Garden's terraces are raised lawns behind villas that stay at street level (only lanes, not houses, front its
  terraces); the walkways are blockouts; no new running faces beside wide roof gaps beyond the walkways' flanks and the
  retaining walls. The fourth merchant tower found no plot. The tour world shows a lands tree growing in the gatehouse
  yard (worldgen order; check with `/awaken city scan`). B1's `bellwatch` street camera now looks into a jettied house.
- **Not verified in game**: walking the flights (`city_relief_steps` checks every step's geometry along the streets),
  and placing a hanging ladder by hand (the GameTest checks its survival rules, not the right-click).
