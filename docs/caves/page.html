The Living Deep

Your caves still read as vanilla because, under the Singing Deep's lodes and mouths, they still are: vanilla's cave shapes, bare grey rock, almost nothing on the floor, no light to see by and nothing alive. This plan gives the underground its own geography, eight cave regions with their own shapes, rock, growth, light, life and sound, and dresses every cave with an underground version of the Soft Land.

Caves right now, they just feel like vanilla still. Like I just went into a completely new world and everything, and it just felt very vanilla and boring. So we need to like plan an entirely new revamp to the caves and the cave generation system and the ambience to really make it immersive just as much as the overworld is. You, 29 Oct, after New World (9)

I read "no depth" two ways and the plan answers both: the caves have no layers of detail (nothing on the surfaces, no variety), and going deeper doesn't feel like going anywhere new.

Why they read as vanilla

R1

Every cave is vanilla's shape.

Tectonic's caves are vanilla's own cave noises (cheese, spaghetti, noodles, pillars) sealed 12 blocks under the ground. The Singing Deep left generation alone on purpose; its styles were decoration only.

R2

The walls are bare grey rock.

92% plain rock, 0.12% ours. The only dressing (cave styles) uses vanilla's lush-cave blocks, tries 20 spots a chunk, and does nothing under plains, meadows, hills, savanna, swamp or beaches.

R3

Nothing sits on the surfaces.

About 2% of cave floor carries anything, against 56% of the ground outside. The overworld has seams, slabs, pebbles and plants; caves have nothing like it.

R4

It's too dark to see what variety there is.

1.3 light points per 1,000 cave cells. Anything more than 10 blocks from your torch is black, so a cave's size and character never show.

R5

Going deeper only changes the tint.

The bands change the fog, the caption and the hum. Shapes, rock and life are the same at y 40 and y -40, apart from deepslate.

R6

Nothing small lives down there.

The 19 wildlife kinds (fireflies, birds, butterflies) are surface-only. Underground there are vanilla bats and the few Hollows.

R7

The air is one colour per band.

One fog colour and one room tone per band for the whole world, and no particles in the air except motes in your own light.

What already works and stays: the mouths and their routes, lodes on walls, the strike, the Pick, the Delver lamps, the sinkholes, mines, seeps and grottos, the breath of the Deep, held light. They made the trip down good. They can't carry the whole underground, because they're rare: about one authored place per 500 blocks of cave.

Three ways to fix it

A · Dress what's there

Keep every shape. Add a dense skin, our own blocks, underground life and air.

Gains: cheap, quick, low risk. Misses: the generation stays vanilla's, every cave the same shape. You named the generation first.

B · Cave regions (recommended)

The underground gets its own geography: eight Awakened cave regions, each a full kit of shape, rock, growth, light, life, air and mining. Every cave gets A's skin.

Gains: fixes all seven causes and keeps vanilla's connected network, Tectonic and the Singing Deep. Costs: the biggest art job yet; one cost unknown (shapes) proven first.

C · Our own generator

Switch off Tectonic's caves and generate every cave from Awaken's own functions.

Gains: total control of shape. Costs: re-tunes water, ores, ancient cities, lodes and mouths all at once; slowest world generation; highest risk for the same result.

B builds on A (the skin ships first and helps every cave before a region exists), so nothing is lost by choosing it. If the first phase's bench shows the new shapes cost too much, B keeps everything else and draws its shapes with features only.

The underground, mapped

A region is chosen by the land above it (which kinds can appear) and by its own noise field (which one does, and where none does). Caves under a forest lean to the Rootways, but a forest can sit over Pale Halls too. Edges are dithered, never a chunk line. Regions take about two thirds of the Shallows and the Middle Dark and half of the Deep; the rest stay plain caves, dressed, so a region feels like a place you enter.

Every cave, region or not

The underground Soft Land

  • Rubble half-steps where floor meets wall and on one-block risers, made of the rock they sit on.
  • Loose stones and scree fans; now and then bones.
  • Stalactites and stalagmites in every rock, where water drips.
  • Wall growth: lichen in the band's colour, mineral crust, wet streaks that catch light.
  • Puddles under drips that ripple when a drop lands; dusty webs in dry dead ends.

Light that shows a cave's size

  • Glowing life and minerals in regions, sparse, so dark patches stay and monsters still spawn.
  • Drawn light (no light blocks): glow-worm skies, rippling water light, glints, lava's glow on rock, seen to 48 blocks.
  • Daylight shafts at every opening the game knows near you, not only named mouths.
  • Plain caves stay dark. The contrast is the point; torches still matter.

Life

  • A client-only underlife like the surface wildlife: moths to your light, glow-worm threads, blind fish, crayfish, beetles, swifts, roosting bats that scatter, crickets.
  • Never saved or sent, no server cost, about 40 bodies at most.
  • The Hollows stay the fauna module's; no new hostile mob in this plan.

Air and sound

  • Each region's own fog colour, Lens look, water colour and sound bed, on hooks that already exist.
  • Our own particles: spores, warm dust, glitter, ash, mist over water, falling drips that splash.
  • Fewer at Decreased, none at Minimal.

Shapes

  • A wrapper inside Tectonic's caves adds each region's shapes (halls, fissures, terraces, lake caverns, columns) and removes noodles where a region wants calm.
  • No new openings to the sky. The way in stays the mouths and sinkholes, which already keep clear of roads, villages and the city.

Mining

  • The rock behind a region's wall is that rock too, so mining into the Ochre Deep is mining ochre.
  • Ores in region rock get their own textures (about eight), same drops.
  • Lodes lean to their region; the Hearth names it: "The old Delvers' iron is in the ochre caves under the ridge, 300 m north of your home."
  • The first time in each region: a caption, a Journal line and a Codex page.

What it costs

World generation

The rock and floors ride a pass that already runs on every block, so they're nearly free. The shapes are the one real cost: a cached region field per column and a little noise per corner. The first phase measures it before anything else is built: within 20% of plain Tectonic, the same line the Lands kept, or the shapes come from features instead.

Far Sight

Far Sight predicts the far ground from the terrain density only, never the caves, so its far land is unchanged and costs nothing more. A test checks the surface stays bit-identical. New rocks get their colour in City Sight's palette.

Frame time

Underlife up to 0.2 ms, drawn light 0.1 ms, particles inside the current budget, Lens looks free. Measured once on the Mac at the end; your 60 fps on Cinematic stays the line.

Existing worlds

Generation is new worlds only, behind its own data pack flag. New World (9) gets livelier air and life but not new shapes. To see the regions you start a new world, as after each terrain change.

Phases

P0Ground truth

The data pack and its flag, the region field, the shape wrapper with one shape, cave biomes injected by depth, the bench, the census on five seeds.

Go or stop: generation within 20%, surface untouched, structures intact.

P1Skin, light and life

The underground Soft Land on every cave, drawn light, the underlife, cave particles. A style sheet comes to you before textures.

Gate: your OK on the style sheet; floors dressed ≥ 30%.

P2Two regions, whole

The Rootways and the Ochre Deep with everything: shape, rock, ores, growth, light, life, sound, Lens look, discovery.

Gate: you play a new world. Does it still read as vanilla?

P3The other six

The remaining regions, and the shafts and chasms between bands.

Gate: census targets on five seeds.

P4Mining ties

Lodes by region, the Hearth's region line, Journal, Codex and Trails, each region's find; the pacing sim re-run.

About four to five builder-days at our pace (the Singing Deep's four phases took one day). The art is the biggest part: about 30 blocks, 8 ore textures, 10 small creatures, 10 particles and region sounds.
