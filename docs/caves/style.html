The cave corners are rendered from the real block models and textures, lit the way the game lights blocks (block light falling one level a block, the game's light curve at the Bright setting and its warm torch colour, the fixed shading on each face). It isn't the game: no smooth shadows in corners, no Lens, and the particles and creatures stand still. Each corner is shown twice: by your torch, and in flat light so you can see every piece.

1 · The skin: every cave

Every cave gets this, region or not, made of the rock it sits in: rubble heaped where the floor meets a wall (its high side against the wall), real 3D stalactites and stalagmites in four thicknesses (vanilla's are a flat cross), loose stones, lichen in the depth band's colour, white mineral crust, wet streaks under a dripping roof, a puddle under the drips that takes the cave's water colour, and a dusty web in a dry corner.

Shallows (stone). Green-grey lichen on the left wall with a patch of crust; the shelf on the right drips, so stalactites hang from it, stalagmites rise under them, and a puddle lies between. In a plain cave the water is vanilla's blue.

Middle Dark (tuff) and the Deep (deepslate). The same kit in each rock's own colours (the middle five shades of every piece are the game's own rock, so a heap reads as fallen from the wall beside it). The lichen turns gold in the Middle Dark and slate-blue in the Deep.

The formations taper smoothly over one to four blocks (tip, then a narrowing piece, a middle and a base, as pointed dripstone does) and meet into columns. Each is a few stacked boxes, so the torch catches its sides.

2 · The first two regions (P2)

These two come first, and you'll play them in a new world before the other six are made.

The Rootways (the Shallows under forests, taiga and jungle): brown rootstone threaded with old roots, green-grey water, spores and pollen drifting, and pale dusk moths that come to your torch. Root curtains hang through the roof, teal glowcaps grow in patches on the floor (a faint light you see down the gallery), and glow-worm threads hang in clusters, shining in the dark. You hear the old roots creak overhead.

The Ochre Deep (the Middle Dark under temperate land): banded red-orange rock in terraces, orange drip pools, warm dust in the air, rust beetles on the walls and floor. Rust crust and rust flowstone take the place of the plain caves' crust and streaks, and ember lichen glows faintly like a dying fire. You hear the rock ticking as it settles, a chorus of chime crickets off in the dark, and a low warm hum.

The two regions' own pieces, close up. The ores that live in a region wear its rock (coal and copper in rootstone, iron in ochre: dark hematite with iron's peach rim); any other ore keeps a pocket of the stone it grew in.

3 · All eight regions

Each region's colours (its rock from crevice to highlight, then its light), its rock, its particle and its creature. These were the directions you OK'd; section 5 shows the six P3 regions as built.

4 · The underlife

Small life that only your game draws (nothing saved, nothing sent, about 40 at most around you), from one sprite sheet, as the surface's butterflies and dragonflies are: moths' wings hinge and beat, beetles walk the walls, fish turn in pools, bats roost in clusters and scatter as you pass. Our own bodies, not vanilla's bat.

5 · The other six regions, as built (P3)

Each region now has its rock all the way into the walls, its ores in that rock, and pieces no other cave has. The rocks had their full pass since the sketches in section 3: the limestone's bedding wanders and breaks off (straight lines read as planks), the basalt shows the sides of columns (it read as bricks), and the Violet Deep's vein crosses the block on a slant (straight down, it lined up into stripes across a wall).

The Pale Halls (the Shallows under plains, savanna, meadows and beaches): wide flat-roofed halls of pale limestone. Every roof drips here, not only the wet ones, so formations are everywhere; rimstone pools lie in terraced fields on the floor, and cave pearls sit in the puddles under the drips. Glow-worm threads hang from the roof, and mist lies low over the still water.

The Windcleft (the Middle Dark under hills and mountains): tall cracks in blue-grey banded slate with mica that glints in your light. Scree is heaped along the walls (twice the plain caves' rubble), white rime frosts the walls where the wind comes through, and now and then an old Delvers' rope hangs down a crack, up to twelve blocks, never quite to the floor. You can climb it. Swifts nest in the cracks.

The Drowned Galleries (the Middle Dark under coasts and the flattest land): low lake caverns of damp green-grey siltstone. Silt banks build up along the water, old driftwood lies where a flood left it, pale cave weed grows on the lake floors, and a film of algae glows green at the waterline (a faint light round every lake). Crayfish pick along the bottom.

The Glimmer Hollows (the Middle Dark under deserts, the Glass Steppe and frozen land): round crystal rooms off narrow throats, in sandy glimmerstone with amber flecks. Crystal clusters grow from the floor, the walls and the roof, each glowing softly. Under hot and warm land they're amber; under frozen land (the Frost Spires) they're glacial crystals, a pale ice blue. Glass moths drift through the glittering dust.

The Black Vaults (the Deep, half of all land): huge halls of column basalt over the lava sea, their roofs up to 48 blocks high, held up by rock pillars. Grey ash lies in drifts on the floor and falls through the air, some of it still glowing; ash moths, their wing edges like cinders. Diamonds grow here, in the basalt. Where the lava sea below breaks through, it lights the hall orange; this picture is torch only.

The Violet Deep (the Deep, the other half): warped tunnels into vaulted chambers, in violet seepstone with Deepglass veins. Violet growths glow in patches on the floor, the chitin crawlers leave their cast plates lying about, and violet seep rises through the air. The Hollows' home ground.

6 · Root amber, the Rootways' find (P4)

Each region now has something of its own you can take home and set down, and a trail in the Journal that lists them: cave pearls from the Pale Halls, the Delvers' old rope from the Windcleft, driftwood from the Drowned Galleries, an amber or glacial crystal from the Glimmer Hollows, ember lichen from the Ochre Deep, a Hollow's shed shell from the Violet Deep. All of those are pieces you've already seen. The Rootways' find on the plan's page is root amber, and that one is new.

Root amber: resin an old root bled into a crack long ago, hardened in the dark into small honey-coloured nuggets. Light comes through it, so it's warm orange in the body with darker lines where the resin ran, a pale glint where a facet catches your lamp, and now and then a gnat or a seed caught inside. It lies in a few beads on the Rootways' floor, under the root curtains, rarely (about one curtain in twenty), the way cave pearls lie under drips. It drops itself and can be set down at home. The colours are kept away from gold's yellow so it never reads as a gold nugget.
