Arms: listening

AWAKEN · weapons and combat · 2026-10-25 · 9 scenes, 26 sounds

About five minutes. Every sound a fight makes in Arms, built from CC0 recordings (Kenney) and the kit's own synthesis, mastered to sit with vanilla's combat sounds. The checks already passed (loudness, true peak, a blow's transient in its first 6 ms, no clicks, variants that differ, no harshness): they catch a broken sound, not a dull one. That part is yours.

  1. The scenes first: each plays a moment of a fight at the game's own timing. Does it feel like steel, like weight, like the moment it is? Mark it keep, remake or cut, and say why in the note.
  2. Then any sound alone that bothered you in a scene: its variants play one after another (the game takes them in turn so a fight never repeats one sample).

Headphones or good speakers, at the volume you play at. Your choices stay in this browser; Export and send me the file (or the text).

01

In a fight

the sounds together, at the timing of the game's rows

The chain, in rhythmStrike 1, 2, 3 into a zombie, each press on its rhythm window: the swish climbs a semitone a step, the finisher lands heavy.
The chain, mashedThe same chain pressed early every time (buffered to the window, no rhythm): no climb. Should feel flatter, not wrong.
Deflect, then riposteA creature tells a light blow (8 ticks), the guard rises in time: the steel sings, then the riposte's thrust lands heavy.
A heavy on the glintThe strike key held: the draw creaks, the glint pings at its mark, released on it: the rising cut lands heavy.
Perilous: dodgedA boss readies a perilous blow (a shriek nothing else makes), a perfect dodge (the world's breath held), a Slipcut.
A heavy breaks the guardAn amber heavy tell, blocked instead of deflected: the guard is crushed aside and the blow gets through.
Break, then ExecutionThree blows on a golem (stone), its stance gives, the Execution: the biggest blow there is.
The ArtA heavy held through to full charge with the meter full: the draw, the glint passing, the Art let go, its wave reaching three foes in turn.
What the blade meetsOne cut into each body: flesh, armour, bone, wood, stone, a spirit, slime.
