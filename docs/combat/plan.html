Arms: every fight a duel you can read

Three verbs on the mouse and the sprint key. Deflects that ring and stagger, dodges that slow the world for a beat, hits with real weight. Sixteen ways to fight and about 290 truly 3D weapons, each with its own ability, many of them fused with your powers. Built on the Ascent's feel, the Holo and the Hands plan, so it reads as one game.

Plan only: no game code until you say yes. Full text: docs/combat/PLAN.md on branch pack/combat-plan. Labels: verified means read in the code or the 26.3 jars; proposed means designed, not built, nothing measured.

01What you asked for

A fight should feel like the movement already does: a different game. That means four things, and the plan treats each as a requirement.

Survive by skill

Parry, block and dodge on every melee weapon, a diamond sword included, so a boss is won by reading it.

Feel every hit

Hitting, being hit and blocking each have their own weight, sound, camera and reaction.

Weapons worth finding

Hundreds of 3D weapons with abilities, merged with the magic, found over time or hunted on purpose.

Simple to play, deep to master

Few keys, combos that flow, and a skill ceiling you climb for months.

"Magical", as something you can see

WordWhat a player sees or hears
A satisfying hitOn the same frame: both bodies freeze 45–220 ms by weight, three layers of sound (swish, the material struck, a sweetener), a camera kick along the swing, sparks of the right material, and the target recoiling in the blow's direction. A dagger and a maul feel different with your eyes closed.
A satisfying defenceA deflect rings, throws sparks off both blades and staggers the attacker. A block shudders the weapon in your hands. A perfect dodge slows the attacker for a beat.
IntuitiveAttack, guard, dodge. Everything else comes from context: how long you hold, when you press, where your body is.
A skill ceilingRhythm, deflect timing, perfect dodges, charge releases, cancels, strikes from a slide or a wall, Breaks, and powers woven into the blade.
"I didn't know this was possible"Firing the rope at a Lord, reeling in and landing an Execution. A panicked Heat cast after a deflect that sets the riposte ablaze, and the System naming the Weave you just found.

Not in this plan: directional combat (For Honor style), a stamina bar, gore, any change to the Ascent's motion, PvP balance, bows and crossbows (later). Must not break: momentum hits, gear procs, power hit-stop, damage numbers, the free-run, the Ritual's fight, the gate run's rules, the 461 GameTests.

02What fights are today

Melee is vanilla's click-and-wait swing with our multipliers on top. There is no parry, no block of our own and no dodge. We worked a diamond sword against Gate Lords with the real numbers from the code (Lord health ×(1+0.075·lv)×14, damage ×(1+0.04·lv)×1.6, your stats at 3 points a level, full diamond armour).

Where it breaks: not in the numbers but in the first unreadable swing. A ground Lord's basic melee is vanilla's: a hit every second in reach with no wind-up and no tell in the creature life layer. It is about 74% of the damage that kills you. The told abilities (14–58 tick wind-ups, marked ground) are the readable minority. A parry added to today's Lords would have nothing fair to read, so the plan changes the enemies too.

03Three ways to do it

A · repair

Tell, block, shake

Wind-ups on every boss swing, a shield-style block and parry on swords, melee hit-stop, a sound pass. About 4–6 hours.

Bosses become survivable. Swings, weapons and combos stay vanilla: not "a different game".

B · recommended

Arms, our own combat

A combat module with data-driven fighting styles, server-run swings, guard, deflect and dodge on every melee weapon, posture on enemies, one feel layer, generated 3D weapons and Weaves with the powers. Built inside Hands and the Player Animation Library.

Biggest scope (§14). The slice proves the feel on one weapon family before anything scales, and A's fixes ship inside it.

C · adopt

Epic Fight or Better Combat

Depend on a combat framework and theme around it.

Its feel, not ours. Better Combat is All Rights Reserved and fought our feel in the pack review; Epic Fight is GPL-3 with its own animation stack, stamina and no 26.3 release.

04Three verbs, no new keys

Left mouse

Strike

Tap: the weapon's chain of three or four strikes; keep tapping to continue.

Hold: a heavy; release on the glint for a perfect one. Held past full charge with Resonance full: the weapon's Art.

Right mouse

Guard

The first 4 ticks after the press deflect. Held: a block. Shields keep vanilla blocking and deflect too.

Sprint+ ← → ↓

Dodge

A 6-tick step-roll, 2.6 blocks, two charges. Sprint with forward still runs and free-runs. Vanilla and the Runner never use sprint with back or sideways.

Context

Everything else

Riposte after a deflect, Slipcut after a dodge, Execution on a Broken foe, Undercut from a slide, Plunge from a fall, Wall Cut, Reel Strike on the rope, Swoop from a glide, Weaves with a power.

Optional settings: a rebindable Dodge key (unbound) or double-tap a direction; an optional lock-on key (unbound). Soft focus needs no key: the creature you strike becomes your focus, and your strikes turn toward it by at most 15°.

06Reading a fight: every attack tells you how to answer it

Every enemy attack gets a class, and every class has its own look and sound, so a fight can be read by eye and by ear. Today's told abilities keep their wind-ups and gain a class; the basic melee gets a tell of at least 8 ticks.

Posture (enemies only)

Deflects, heavies, finishers and Undercuts fill a creature's posture: 20 for normal mobs, 40 for elites, 100 per phase for a Lord, draining after 2 s untouched. Full: BREAK. The creature sags for 2–2.5 s, its wind-up is interrupted, and your next heavy is an Execution. There is no stamina bar for you.

Resonance (yours)

One meter from rhythm finishers, deflects, perfect dodges and Breaks. Full, the blade glows and a heavy held past full charge releases the weapon's Art. It shows as a thin arc under the crosshair and nowhere else.

07The feel of a hit

One shared feel layer for melee and powers. The hit-stop that powers already have moves to a public API and gains the attacker's side (hands and camera hold), and the Feel Director becomes the one camera voice for fights, with one comfort setting set.

LayerOn every connecting hit
FreezeThe struck body holds its pose and trembles (exists for powers); your hands and the camera hold too (new).
SoundSwish by blade length, speed and rhythm step (each step a semitone up) · impact by weapon (edge, blunt, point) × target (flesh, armour, stone, wood, glass, ghost, slime) · a sweetener (crit glass, element, Break crack, deflect ring, guard clang, Execution boom). 3–4 seeded variants each, synthesised and mastered with audiokit.
CameraA kick along the swing's plane through the Feel Director; reduced motion halves it; the shoulder view gets a combat pose, a little closer, leaning toward your focus.
ReactionThe creature life layer takes the blow's direction and strength, so a dagger flicks a body and a maul folds it.
LightA ribbon trail lying in the blade's plane (a new two-edge shape sampled every frame), sparks of the struck material, the DEFLECT / BREAK / PERFECT glyphs beside the damage numbers.
Getting hitA Holo shard at the screen edge toward the attacker, the camera jolting away from the blow, our own hurt sounds by damage kind; under 30% health a slow heartbeat and a cooled, desaturated edge (a Lens look).

09Weapons and magic: Weaves

For 12 ticks after a deflect, a perfect dodge, a Break or a rhythm finisher, a power you cast fuses with the blade. Four openings times nine families make 36 Weaves, each costing 30% less energy. You find them by doing: the first time, the System names it and the Codex adds it, and undiscovered ones are never listed.

Attunement at the Forge

Bind a weapon to a Force (Heat, Charge, Gravity, Spirit, Frost) with essence and a Core Shard. Its abilities take that element, and its hits prime your next power, the way the Gatewrought set already primes your next strike.

Arts

Every fighting style has a default Art; Lord-forged and legendary weapons have their own. Kill the Gearwright Colossus and its maul does the Piston Slam, for you now.

10The weapons: sixteen ways to fight

A fighting style (an archetype) owns a moveset, a timing table, a reach, a guard, a sound kit and a trail. Every weapon of that style shares its animations and adds its own model, materials, abilities and sometimes an Art. The Longsword is the slice.

About 48 abilities (Marks) combine a trigger (hit, finisher, deflect, perfect dodge, Break, kill, guard, perfect heavy) with an effect (an element, posture, healing, energy, a short buff). Mastery per style, from use, adds a fourth and fifth strike or a charged deflect to the same inputs. Vanilla swords, axes, tridents and maces get the three verbs and a plain moveset; a diamond sword counts as an E/D weapon.

11Making 290 of them, to one standard

One line of Python per weapon. Generators build everything else, checks reject what falls short, and you review a contact sheet and a listening page per batch of about 40.

13Before and after

A · Your case: a diamond sword against the D Gearwright Colossus

Today (from the code)

It swings every second with no warning. You trade hits and die in about 6 s, at 11% of its health. To win you must avoid about 89% of its basic swings, with no tool to do it.

Proposed

Its arm draws back for 10 ticks with a servo whine and a glint (LIGHT). You press right mouse as the fist falls: a ring, sparks, a 100 ms freeze, its posture jumps. Riposte. The Piston Slam flares amber: deflect or sidestep. Overload Arc flashes red: dodge through it and the Colossus slows for a beat. Five deflects in: BREAK. Hold left mouse: Execution. Target: about 110 s for a steady player, about 60% health left.

B · An ordinary fight in the Wilds: three skeletons and a zombie

Today

Click, wait for the cooldown, click. Vanilla sounds. Numbers float up.

Proposed

Tap in rhythm: three strikes, the swish rising each step, a short freeze and a crack of bone on every hit. An arrow is deflected off the blade. The zombie lunges; sprint-left, Slipcut. Three inputs the whole time.

C · A hard case: a crowded gate floor in a pressure wave

Today

Hits come from everywhere and none can be answered.

Proposed

Your focus stays on the one you struck; the arc hits everything in it, once each. Two attacks inside one deflect window are both deflected. A red grab from behind can't be deflected; its sound tells you to dodge. Freezes are capped so a sweep through five bodies never stalls.

D · Something missing: a 150 ms server, no animation library, Hands not built yet

Risk if we ignored it

Deflects land late and feel random; others see vanilla arms; the first person swings vanilla.

Proposed

Deflect grace makes a press timed to your screen count. Others see keyframed arm poses (no library needed). First person uses the slice's interim redraw. A swing the server refuses fades out instead of snapping.

E · Discovery: a Weave you didn't know existed

Today

Powers and the sword never meet, apart from one gear set.

Proposed

You deflect an imp and, panicking, cast Heat: the riposte comes out on fire and rings the ground. "WEAVE · Ember Riposte" types in; the Codex's Weaves page goes from 3 / ?? to 4 / ??. Later a Wayfarer hints at "what frost does after a perfect step".

15Your decisions

  1. Invulnerability on the dodge. The SPEC says "no invincibility frames anywhere". Allow 5 evade ticks on the dodge only, and the perfect dodge? Recommended: yes. Without it the dodge only moves you, which is weaker and less fair.
  2. The dodge input. Sprint with back, left, right or no direction, on the ground. Recommended: no new key, and nothing uses it today. Or a dedicated key (Mouse 4?), or double-tapping a direction.
  3. Guard and deflect on right mouse with any melee weapon. Recommended: yes.
  4. Hands grows to weapons. Its plan keeps attacking vanilla; Arms weapons would swing through Hands in first person, and its first rig would be built before the slice. Recommended: yes.
  5. Vanilla swords: the three verbs only (recommended), or also our own generated 3D models for vanilla swords and axes.
  6. The Player Animation Library: stays optional, with keyframed arm poses for players without it (recommended), or becomes required.
  7. Weapon levels: found and forged weapons carry a level that scales like the powers, so they keep pace with Lords; vanilla weapons stay E/D. Recommended: yes.
  8. Weapons found in a gate: a Lord-forged weapon goes straight into your hands on the kill; the rest stay Satchel loot. Recommended.
  9. Order: S0 and S1 first, with your feel session as the go or no-go before anything scales. Recommended.
