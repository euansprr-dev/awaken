# Arms combat sim, 2026-10-26

`tools/sim/combat_sim.py` answers PLAN §6.1's question: with Arms as built (SPEC §25: S0, S1), how do the four player
profiles fare against every Gate Lord at its home rank? 400 seeded fights a cell: 15 Lords × 4 profiles, plus the
"diamond sword, Parrier, at D" row and hits to kill normal creatures (25,600 fights). The as-built run takes about 10 s on
4 worker processes. The slowest preset takes about 110 s, because its fights are longer. Results are deterministic for a
seed, and `--jobs` only changes the speed.

```
python3 tools/sim/combat_sim.py                          # as built, 400 seeds, the table below
python3 tools/sim/combat_sim.py --item-level on          # melee x (1 + 0.075 x item level), the PowerMath mirror
python3 tools/sim/combat_sim.py --preset fixes           # the three bugs below, as if fixed
python3 tools/sim/combat_sim.py --preset recommended-hp  # every recommended change
python3 tools/sim/combat_sim.py --lord bell_warden --seeds 100 --override lord.extra_health=1.85 --json out.json
```

Nothing here was applied to Java or JSON. The changes are recommendations, and each one can be re-run with a flag.

## Headline

**As built, only the Tank target and the normal-mob target hold.**

| Target (PLAN §6.1) | As built | Fixes only | All recommended changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tank wins ≤ 10% | **PASS** 15/15 (dies in 4–21 s) | PASS 15/15 | PASS 15/15 |
| Dodger wins ≥ 60%, TTK 80–120 s | **FAIL** 0/15 (mean win 5%) | FAIL 0/15 (8%) | 11/15 (96%); the 4 misses have TTK 77–79 s |
| Parrier wins ≥ 80%, TTK 80–120 s | **FAIL** 0/15 (mean win 6%) | FAIL 1/15 (24%) | 12/15 (92%); misses: Hive Regent, Mother, Root Saint |
| Expert wins ≥ 98%, TTK ≤ 75 s, ≥ 50% health left | **FAIL** 3/15 (mean win 53%) | FAIL 5/15 (89%) | 13/15 (100% wins); Mother and Glass Serpent TTK 77–79 s |
| Diamond sword, Parrier, D: wins ≥ 70%, TTK ≤ 140 s | **FAIL** 0/4 | FAIL 1/4 | PASS 4/4 |
| Normal mobs: 3–6 hits on level | **PASS** (median 4 at every rank) | PASS | PASS |

Why it fails, biggest cause first:

1. **Blows land in a different class from the one they were told as (a bug).** Bolts that carry a Force (Spirit,
   Heat, Frost, Charge, Gravity, Void, Dream) are told as LIGHT but land as **NONE**. A guard can't deflect or block them,
   and the dodge's evade ticks don't cover them. Fang bites land as NONE too, and go through armour. Auras and clouds are
   told as PERILOUS but land as LIGHT. As built, Void Bolts are the Expert's second most common killer and Echo Bolts are
   in its top five. Fixing this alone lifts the Expert's mean win from 53% to 77%.
2. **One Lord blow takes a large share of an on-level player's health.** After armour and VIT, a told LIGHT melee takes
   29.5% at E, 21% at D, 16% at C, 15% at B and 12% at A. A Dodger lets 30% of blows through, which means a 20–40% hit
   about every 5–7 s, and nothing survives 80–120 s of that. Fitted to the targets, a told LIGHT should take about 6%.
   Because the share falls as rank rises, the cut has to depend on level: one flat cut that suits D and C leaves E
   unwinnable and lets the Tank beat the A Lord (86% wins at ×0.35).
3. **Fights are short on the ground and long in the air.** Ground Lords die in 40–60 s (the targets want 80–120 s). Fliers
   orbit out of reach and take 85–110 s, even though their health is the same.
4. **Skill barely speeds up the kill.** An Expert kills only 7–11% faster than a Parrier on the same Lord. So "Expert
   ≤ 75 s" and "Parrier ≥ 80 s" can both hold only if the Parrier's median is about 80–84 s.
5. **At E, vanilla's flat parts dominate.** A burn deals 1 HP per second, poison 1 per 25 ticks, and the fang a flat 6
   through armour, and none of these scale with level. An E player has 22 HP. Once the bugs are fixed and the damage
   curve is in, burning is still 62% of what the Ember Tyrant deals to a Parrier.
6. **A Lord's summons are normal creatures at its level.** The Lord's damage cut doesn't touch them, and they deal 28–42%
   of the damage in the Bell Warden, Mother of Spores, Sleeper and Hive Regent fights.

## How the sim works

The sim runs tick by tick at 20 ticks a second. The Lord runs the game's own state machines:

- `AbilityRunner`: a pick every 4 ticks, per-ability cooldowns plus 0–19 ticks, a 14-tick global cooldown, and 8 extra
  wind-up ticks on a heavy-flagged ability.
- `MobGoals.ToldMelee`: a 10-tick tell, a HEAVY haymaker every third swing, and a pause while the body is held.
- `FlyHunt` for fliers: the told dive.
- Phases with their 30-tick roar, posture with lazy drain, Breaks, the Mirror Knight's Perfect Parry, Haunted phasing,
  Frenzy, Royal Jelly, auras, clouds, fangs, and summons.

Each profile plans its answer to each told blow when the tell starts. The guard, the dodge and the evade ticks are then
resolved mechanically by Arms' rules (`ArmsRules.guard`, `evades`, the lockout and the guard break). The offence runs the
real chain decision (`ArmsRules.tap`), and each hit goes through the real damage stack: weapon, STR, crit, motion and
rhythm, then the Lord's armour. Hits on the player go through Veil Flicker, VIT, the set wards, the block, vanilla's
10-tick i-frames and vanilla armour, and natural regeneration runs off the food bar.

The game's numbers are **parsed from the sources at start**, so the sim follows them when they change. The other numbers
are assumptions, listed below.

## Item level on

`--item-level on` multiplies melee by (1 + 0.075 × item level), with the item level set to the Lord's content level.
That mirrors `PowerMath.levelScale` (`PowerMath.java:42-44`), stacked on top of STR. It **double-counts**:

- **Normal mobs FAIL.** Median hits to kill: E 3, D 2, C 2, B 1, A 1 (0–15% of creatures in 3–6 from D up).
- Lords die in 13–28 s from C up. The Tank wins 72% at C (Abbot) and 98% at A (Herald), which fails the Tank target at
  3 Lords.
- Dodger and Parrier still meet 0/15: TTK is far below 80 s, and E/D still kill them. Expert meets 8/15. Diamond meets 2/4.

Read: on-level melee doesn't need an item level to keep pace (see the paragraph above). If PLAN §7.7's item level ships,
normalise it so an on-level weapon is ×1: ×(1 + 0.075·(item level − the tier's content level)). That way a found weapon
above its tier pays 7.5% a level, and the 3–6-hit rule holds.

## Bugs the sim found

1. **Bolts with a Force, fang bites, auras and clouds land in a different class from the one they were told as.**
   - `AwBolt.onHitEntity` calls `Abilities.hurtBy` with `source(forceKey, direct = the bolt, causing = the mob)`
     (`AwBolt.java:108`, `Abilities.java:64-65`). No AWAKEN damage type carries a vanilla tag, so `Strikes.of` sees
     neither a projectile nor the attacker's own blow and returns **NONE** (`Strikes.java:64-74`).
   - `ArmsServer.onIncomingDamage` then returns before the dodge's evade check (`ArmsServer.java:374`), and
     `onShieldBlock` passes it straight through.
   - Affected: Echo Bolts, Lantern Bolts, Void Bolts, Frost Hymn, Spark Halo, Zero Lance's charge bolts, and the summons'
     bolts (Ember Imp, Frost Wisp, Coil Unit): every BoltKind with a Force. All are told as LIGHT, yet none can be
     deflected, blocked or evaded by i-frames.
   - The vanilla fang bite is indirect magic: it lands as NONE, through armour, for a flat 6.
   - Aura pulses (`AwakenMob.java:536`) and hazard strikes (`Hazards.java:117`) land outside `Strikes.deliver` as the
     caster's own blow, so they count as LIGHT even though they were told as PERILOUS.
   - Effect of fixing it: Expert mean win 53% → 77%, Parrier 6% → 18%.
2. **An ability's burn and effect land even when the blow was evaded or deflected.** `Abilities.hurtBy` ignites (FIRE,
   3 s) and adds the effect after `hurtServer`, whatever `hurtServer` returned (`Abilities.java:75-80`). A dodged Echo
   Bolt still applies Weakness (−4 attack: an E player's 5.5 drops to 1.5), and a deflected Magma Fist still burns.
3. **A broken Lord starts its next ability within 4 ticks.**
   - `AwakenMob.stagger` interrupts the current wind-up (`AwakenMob.java:313-320`), but the runner keeps ticking
     (`AwakenMob.java:434`) and its idle branch has no stagger check (`AbilityRunner.java:87-88`).
   - The interrupted ability's cooldown is only set when an ability completes (`AbilityRunner.java:192`), so the same
     ability can start again at once, inside the 40-tick Break.
   - Effect of fixing bugs 2 and 3 on top of bug 1: Expert mean win 77% → 89%.
4. (Minor) The Mirror Knight's Perfect Parry counter is an untold blow: it calls `doHurtTarget` outside
   `Strikes.deliver` (`AwakenMob.java:573`). That breaks SPEC §25's "every boss blow is told for at least 8 ticks".
5. (Dead rule) The rhythm-3 finisher's +12 Resonance (`ArmsRules.java:22`, `ArmsServer.java:205`) can never trigger.
   The chain starts at Cadence 0 (`ArmsRules.java:58`), the 3-strike chain's finisher reaches only Cadence 2, and mastery
   is passed as 0 (`ArmsServer.java:169`). Cadence 3 needs strike 4.
6. (Design) **The Art loses damage.** A 22-tick hold plus a 20-tick move deals 3.0 motion, against about 5.5 for the chain
   in the same time; the 12% cap never comes close. With the other recommended changes in place, the Expert's mean TTK is
   81.7 s using the Art and 77.2 s never using it (200 seeds). At motion 6 the Art breaks even.
7. (Design) **A diamond sword out-hits the D forged Longsword** (7 against 6.5). The gear module pins the C Hunter blade
   to diamond (`GearTests.java:293-296`), so PLAN §4.6's "a diamond sword is an E/D-tier weapon" doesn't hold. At D, a
   diamond-sword Parrier kills about 7% faster than one with the Beastbound Longsword.

## What the sim can't tell you

- **Where bodies stand.** Reach uptime, the share of blows that hit an undefended player, and what a dodge carries you out
  of are all assumed. The flier numbers (step 5) matter most: a tour that logs time-in-reach per Lord would replace them.
- **Feel, latency and readability.** The profiles' success rates are inputs, not results. Whether a 10-tick tell is
  readable at 60% is a question for a playtest.
- **Other boss fights.** Descent guardians, Sovereigns and nightmares share `roleDamage(BOSS)`. Step 3 is written
  Lord-only on purpose; check them separately.
- **Summons.** The minion model (cleave odds, detours) is the roughest part. Summons' share of damage is reported per cell
  in `--json` (`taken_share`).
