Where the items went

I read every path loot takes through a gate and reproduced the three big ones in GameTests (gates_spoils_repro_*). All three pass on today's build, which means the loss really happens. On the way I found that a test player in creative never sees it: creative pockets simply delete what doesn't fit.

#WhenWhat happens todayAfter this plan
1Coming out with full pockets (Rest Shrine, the Lord's exit, a collapsed gate, a relog)most likely yoursreproduced What doesn't fit is thrown at your feet at the gate mouth and despawns in 5 minutes. The only warning is one row in a 7-second card, shown while the gate's other cards are up. If the land at the mouth wasn't loaded, the payout lands up to 5 seconds after you arrive. A Lord's hoard goes past the 27-slot limit, so boss clears often bring out 30 or more stacks. Held for you, taken from E, with no limit and no expiry.
2Walking over loot with a full Satchelreproduced Refused. It stays on the gate's floor and is erased with the gate. The only cue is a toast, "Your Satchel is full." The Satchel never refuses.
3A cache, an elite, or a twist's reward (beating your echo, Stealth Halls, the lost spirit, the Hoarder) with a full Satchelreproduced Spills on the floor, then as row 2. The cache's toast still says "items into your Satchel". Goes in, always.
4Gifts handed straight to your pockets inside a gate: a world's keepsake and set piece, a Lord's weapon, a pattern's refund, the first bell's handbell and frame, the Heart-clapper With full pockets they drop on the floor, the Satchel picks them up, and they're lost if you die later (or left behind if the Satchel is full). A keepsake comes once ever, so that loss is permanent. Bell items will travel this same path once the bells session lands them. Held for you (safe even if you die). Bells and the band never go into the Satchel.
5Monster drops that fall into the void, lava, or a corner you never reach (sky islands, chasms) Never picked up; gone with the gate. Into the killer's Satchel the moment it dies; you still see them fly to you.
6Rare: a claim whose id clashes; gear handed back on respawn with full pockets The first puts loot back into a run that's ending (gone); the second throws gear at your feet. Both go to Held for you.

Kept on purpose: dying (Hunter loses the Satchel, Story keeps half), and walking back out through floor 1's door, which asks you first.

How the best games keep overflow

  • Monster Hunter: what your pouch can't hold goes to your item box.
  • Elden Ring: anything past your carry limit goes to your storage chest.
  • Lost Ark, Genshin Impact: rewards that don't fit arrive by mail.
  • Destiny 2: the Postmaster holds overflow, but deletes the oldest once it fills. That deletion is what players hate.
  • Solo Leveling, where Awaken comes from: the System's inventory holds everything, and the hunter takes things out when needed.

What that means here

Overflow is kept, it's shown, and it has no hidden limit. "Full" is a moment you see, never a silent loss. The Signal holding what your pockets can't is the same idea as the System's inventory, which is why it sits right in your E screen and not in a chest at home.

Decisions for you

1

The Satchel never refuses Recommended

No more 27-slot cap. Everything the gate gives you goes in, and the panel shows it a page at a time.

The other way: keep 27 as "the bag is full, go home" pressure with a clear full moment. Then loot you leave behind is the one thing that can still be lost.

2

What doesn't fit your pockets is held for you Recommended

Taken from the E screen anywhere outside a gate. No limit, no expiry, and you can't put things in, so it isn't a free chest.

The other way: send it to your Hearth's chests (the original plan's line). That needs a Hearth and a trip home, and mixes it into your chests.

3

The Satchel panel is look-only Recommended

You see every stack and tooltip but can't move them, because taking loot into your pockets would dodge the death stake.

The other way: let you take from it, and the stake goes away.

4

Monster drops fly into the killer's Satchel Recommended

Each drop pops out of the monster, hangs for about half a second, then streaks into you with Minecraft's own pickup flight. The void and lava can't take it, and you never hunt for drops.

The other way: drops stay on the ground for you to walk over, and what falls out of reach stays lost.

How I'll check it

  • One GameTest per rule: the Satchel never refuses; held for you (nothing on the ground, a take works, it survives a save and a death); gifts never fall; drops fly in; bells skip the Satchel.
  • The three repro tests turn round into those rules, and the old "a full Satchel refuses" check is updated.
  • The full suite on CI.
  • One CI tour at your MacBook's pixels and GUI scale: E in a gate (Hunter and Story lines), over 30 stacks, the recipe book open, and outside with stacks held. I look at every shot before handing back.

What stays as it is

  • The death rule and the retreat rule.
  • Bells code (the bells session's) and boss fights (the boss-fights session's). One listener in the gates module catches gifts for every module, so neither needs a change.
  • The holo inventory itself: the panel draws beside it from the gates module.
  • No new items or blocks. One small item tag.

About half a day of work at our pace, in one stage. Rollback is one revert: saves only gain the new held list.
