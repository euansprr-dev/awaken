# The roster: every gate monster that needs a body

Part of `PLAN.md`. Proposed, not built. 79 monsters in 15 themes, in the order players meet the themes (rank E first).
The id never changes; the name changes only where the creature's idea did. Each line is a first design, bold on
purpose: one idea, an outline you could cut out of black paper, nothing like the Minecraft mob it wears today. Each
theme also says what its room looks like and so what its monsters are painted in, and what its one light is.

Roles: **swarm** (several, weak), **bruiser** (walks up and hits), **shooter**, **flier**, **elite**, **Warden**.
"Then" is what you do about it.

Kinds of skeleton the engine already walks (a kind shares a clip helper and a profile to start from, never a
performance): **strider** (two legs, arms may plant), **runner** (four legs), **crawler** (six or more), **floater**
(flies or hangs in the air), **slider** (no legs, a base on the ground), **coiler** (segments).
