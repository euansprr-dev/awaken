# The Run: what you do inside a gate (planning brief, 10 Nov 2026)

Euan said "Yes, plan it" on 10 Nov. Follow `/Volumes/MEDIA/Awaken/.claude/PLANNING-PROCESS.md`, end at an approval page, build
nothing until he approves. State of the gates work is in `docs/gates-as-worlds/round-two/STATUS.md`.

## His words (10 Nov, three messages)

- "should each gate be so massive as well? Or can we also have a mix where some are very massive and expansive the way we've been
  building here, and then some are more interior, much faster to build, but still has the depth and everything that looks good...
  how can we speed this up even more? Because it's still too slow in my opinion."
- "we're gonna have to think as well of mob spawning and how you actually level up in gates... right now I don't see any mobs or
  anything. Where's the atmosphere in terms of custom mobs... herds of mobs and things you need to fight, because it can't only
  be the boss... where things spawn on the map and how you actually level up inside of a gate and how you can find loot and
  actually make it AAA level in a way that's genuinely fun... when you go in a gate, you have an awesome time and then you come
  out with, first of all, a challenging experience, but then also a lot of cool loot and things to do after your gate."
- "All of these sound like it's just one room... Why are we replacing the ones that have different tunnels and dungeons and caves?
  I feel like we're just turning all gates into a massive open world that has no mobs or anything. And that's not the point. Gates
  are still supposed to be something where you level up a ton, where you have to go from one point to the next. You have to go and
  fight the boss. You go up, you go down. Maybe there's some movement, stuff where you need to wall run. Maybe there's some places
  where there are traps... very interactive, very fun. What we're building so far is already great. These open world massive
  things are super awesome... But we also still need to have some dungeons, kind of a dungeon crawler experience. That is what a
  gate is at the end of the day. It is a place to fight a lot of different mobs, and you go to three different herds, and you have
  these different things you need to do."
- Earlier verdicts that bind this (9 Nov): the old gates felt like "linear hallways", "a maze", "go back on your steps"; "when I
  go in a Gate, I want to gain a lot of experience and actually massively level up"; never pre-announce a boss; no allies; the
  System is the only guide; plain speech.

## The brief

- Job: step into a gate and play a run: move through it point to point, fight several different packs, get past traps and
  movement stretches, beat wardens and the Lord, level up a lot, carry loot home that matters afterwards.
- Pain: the new gates are places to look at with no designed fights; the old ones had fights but felt like hallways and mazes.
- Wanted: a mix of forms and lengths (open lands and dungeon crawls, short to great), each a designed run; faster to build.
- Proof he agreed to: one dungeon gate he can play start to finish (three packs, a trap stretch, a wall-run stretch, a warden,
  the Lord, loot in the Satchel on the way out).
- Unknowns to audit: what the old gates' fights, keys, caches, modifiers and twists really do; which custom mobs exist per theme;
  which movement a player has at each rank; whether any traps exist; how XP and loot are paid today; what a Realm spawns today.
