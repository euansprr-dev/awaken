Gates You Play

You said the new gates are turning into a massive open world with no mobs, and that a gate is a place where you go from point to point, up and down, fight a lot of different mobs, get past traps and movement, beat the boss and level up a ton. This is the plan for that. Nothing here is built. It waits for your yes.

What I recommend

Make every gate a run of legs, and build it mostly from what the old gates already have. The old gates hold nearly everything you described: packs that wait and wake, rooms that seal, a gauntlet with presses and a wall run, loot, pressure. The new gates use almost none of it. So the first job is connecting, then designing each gate's route in its sheet.

Proof for you to play: The Hour Crypt turned into a full run, and one short dungeon crawl built new. About three to four hours on the clock with three builders, at today's measured pace.

Part 2

What "good" means

StrongYou are fighting within a minute and a half of arriving. You can see the next pack before it sees you. Three wardens, each with its own kind of pack, then the Lord. You go up and down, cross at least one movement stretch and one trap stretch, never walk the same way twice unless a shortcut opened, and never walk a full minute with nothing to do. You leave two or three levels higher with loot you want to use.
WeakA beautiful place with monsters sprinkled on it.
NeverA boss announced ahead. A monster appearing out of thin air beside you. A dead end with nothing in it. A stretch that needs a move you don't have yet. Getting stuck.

Levelling and loot along the way

  • XP arrives all the way through, not in a lump. As a starting split, to be tuned in the pacing simulator: packs 45%, wardens 25%, the Lord 20%, hidden rooms 10%. At E rank that should be two to three levels for a player at the gate's level, from the pay you already approved.
  • Every warden drops something you keep: a part or a pattern that feeds the forge, the Armoury or the Hearth, which are already built. A hidden room in every gate holds the best find.
  • Pressure is on. Stand still for four minutes and the gate starts sending waves.

The mix

Form and length are separate. Form is what the run crosses: a crawl (tunnels, rooms and caves, up and down), a land (open ground with camps and one great thing), a climb, a deep, a sky. Length is how many legs.

LengthLegsTo playShare I'd build
Shortabout 58 to 12 min3 in 10
Standard8 to 1015 to 25 minabout half
Great14 or more40 min and up1 in 6

Crawls should be the quickest to build, because their legs come from parts (a tunnel, a stair, a cave, a trap room, an arena) that a sheet places by name. I expect 15 to 20 minutes for a standard crawl once the parts exist. That is an estimate; the proof measures it.

What the check gains

The bench already proves nothing floats and every site is reached, in under three seconds. For a run it also proves: every movement leg can be done with the moves an E-rank player has, every fight space is big enough for its pack, no leg is without a job, the first fight is within 90 seconds of the Mouth, no walk between legs is longer than a minute, there is at least one loop, and a crawl changes height by 20 blocks or more.
