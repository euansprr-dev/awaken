The idea

Make caves the place metal lives, and make getting there a story.

Not to scale. Each depth gets its own air, light and sound; the first iron trip lives in the Shallows.

Caves that call you

A new mouth about every 200 m: hill arches, Delver adits, collapsed dolines, clefts. They breathe mist, a cold draught sounds near them, a cairn marks the way. The Hearth tells you where, with a "Mark it on my map" button.

Metal lives in the caves

Lodes open onto cave walls and glint in your light. Vanilla's scattered iron, copper, coal and gold drop to 35% in new land. One shallow iron lode covers Tier II.

The strike

Stone chips that tumble and settle, short spark streaks off metal, a breath of dust, new cracks, a real dig motion, recorded sounds. On ores, a seam you can aim for: a true strike.

The Delver's Pick

Pulled from the stone in your first adit. It wakes verse by verse through what you do underground. Magic that changes how you explore; never more than three blocks at once.

The air below

Short-range coloured air in Lens, motes in your light, shafts of daylight at openings, and every few minutes the Deep breathes toward the way out.

Delver lamps

Strike an old lamp and light floods the cavern and stays: named, on your map, safe from spawns.

The dark bites back

The Hollows in every cave by depth, with plates and levels. Your strikes are noise; your light keeps them wary.

Cave styles

Caves take a look from the land above: roots under forests, crystals you can mine under the crystal lands, amber under deserts, ice under snow.
