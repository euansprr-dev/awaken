# Fauna: the Awakened layer's creatures (world module)

Plan v2 sections 4.1 (the reveal) and 4.2 (creatures native to the overworld). Before this the mod had no overworld
spawns at all; now twelve creatures live in three biome families, spawned by vanilla's natural spawner under their
own category, and the world visibly changes the moment you awaken.

Code: `world/fauna/**` (spawning, sightings, reveal, beasts, Now, commands, tests) and, in the mobs module,
`mobs/def/FaunaBestiary.java` (the defs), `mobs/entity/FaunaMob.java`, `NamedBeast.java`, `FaunaGoals.java`,
`FaunaHooks.java` (the bridge), `mobs/client/model/FaunaModels.java` (five new bodies). Data:
`data/awaken/neoforge/biome_modifier/fauna_*.json`, loot tables, lang in `src/main/merge/fauna/`. Everything but the
Java is generated by `tools/art/specs/fauna.py`.

## Spawning rules (`FaunaRules`)

Vanilla's natural spawner does the work: the biome modifiers add the creatures to their biomes' spawn lists under the
`awaken:fauna` category, and `FaunaRules.check` is every creature's spawn placement predicate (`ON_GROUND`,
`MOTION_BLOCKING_NO_LEAVES`). In order, cheapest first:

1. the `awaken:fauna_spawning` game rule (on by default; the "fauna setting"), the overworld only;
2. the ground (`NATURAL`: grass, dirt, podzol, moss, snow, sand, terracotta, goat stone; never planks or bricks;
   scarabs need sand or terracotta) and the surface (at or above the leafless heightmap; hunters may be underground);
3. the time of day by the overworld clock (day 23500-11800, dusk-night 11800-23500, day-dusk 23500-13500), or for
   hunters vanilla's monster light rule (`Monster.isDarkEnoughToSpawn`: block light 0, dark sky) and never in Peaceful;
4. the nearest player within 128 blocks must have awakened; before that only ambient creatures, at 35% per candidate,
   at most 3 near the player (the "faint hints");
5. nothing but ambient creatures within 80 blocks of any Hearth anchor (`HearthHooks.nearest`, covers the tier 5
   radius);
6. role caps near that player: ambient 8, neutral 5, hunter 5, beast 1 (counted from the census, never the level);
7. named beasts: player level 3+, at least 40 blocks from the player, the beast registry's 30 minute world cooldown,
   no other beast within 384 blocks, at most 12 living beasts remembered.

Content level: a step below the nearest player's level, capped at their rank's gate content level (beasts at the
player's level, cap +2), so the overworld stays gentler than the gates you can enter.

## Caps and timings (performance)

- Category `awaken:fauna` (`MobCategory.AWAKEN_FAUNA`, `META-INF/enumextensions.json`): 16 per player, friendly,
  despawns like monsters (instant beyond 128 blocks, randomly beyond 32). It never takes a slot from vanilla's
  categories. Persistent named beasts don't count against it.
- Spawn predicate: O(players + census) per candidate, only while the category is under its cap. The census is a list
  kept by the creatures themselves as they join and leave a level (a few dozen entries per player at most).
- Sightings: every 10 ticks per player, a pass over the census; at most 2 line-of-sight rays per pass, only for kinds
  the player hasn't met; nothing once all twelve are known. One sighting toast per 3 seconds.
- Talents: a moth probes 12 blocks below it every 2 s until it finds ore (8 tries); a sprite looks at 20 columns every
  2 s while idle and off its 30-45 s cooldown; finches probe 16 blocks for a campfire every 2 s and check their flock
  every 3-5 s; a scarab checks for a nearby player every 5 ticks; hunters rally their pack every 2 s while hunting;
  beasts report their position every 2 s. Paths are recomputed at most every 10 ticks; fliers fly straight.
- No per-tick entity scans anywhere. Client: one profile lookup per tick for the faint-hint check, a 1.8 s overlay.

## The reveal (`Reveal`)

On `Progression.onAwakened` (players with a real client only: fake and GameTest players are skipped) the reveal is
marked pending and plays as soon as the player stands in the overworld (the story's Accept teleports home in the same
call; also on a later login or dimension change). It sends a pulse to the client (a signal-blue vignette and one
outward ring, 1.8 s; vanilla's Distortion Effects slider is the reduced-motion switch: no ring below 50%, nothing at
0), plays an amethyst resonance, sends three waves of light motes rising out of the ground around the player (only
that player sees them), and spawns 4 of the biome's current ambient creatures 5-12 blocks in front of them. Worlds
awakened before this existed get the reveal once on the next login.

## Sightings, the Codex, the Now tracker

The first time an awakened player sees a kind (within 24 blocks, within about 60 degrees of the crosshair, line of
sight) `Progression.discover(player, "creature/<id>", 0.1)` pays a tenth of a level (a beast 0.25) and a toast says
"New creature · Lumen Moth / Circles above buried ore at night · +XP". The Codex gains a Creatures column
(`world/fauna/client/CreatureCodex`) built from the synced discovery counters; a named beast is threaded to its
family. The Now tracker shows one line for a named beast within 160 blocks that the player has seen, or that is
within 96 blocks (sensed): "The Old Antler · 120 m NE" (priority 40 from level 3, 22 before), with a compass target.
A new beast also announces itself to the nearest awakened player with a toast.

## Named beasts (`NamedBeast`, `BeastRegistry`)

Persistent, Warden-sized (role WARDEN: x5 health), a territory of 40-48 blocks around where they spawned. A player
within 14 blocks with line of sight gets a warning (a snort, a flash, an action-bar line) every 6 s; one who stays
within 7 blocks for 2 s, or strikes it, gets the fight. Three phases from the def (new abilities, a line, a bar colour);
the bar shows only while it fights. It gives up on a target that leaves the territory, heals and resets when left
alone. On a player kill: the loot table (essence by level, its part, vanilla materials), a 45% Power Core
(`PowerCoreHooks.roll` at the rank of its content level), `awaken:beasts_felled`, a first-kill discovery
(`beast_felled/<id>`, 0.5 level), a best moment. The registry (`awaken:fauna_beasts`, overworld saved data) keeps id,
kind, dimension, last position; entries go on death or discard.

## Commands

- `/awaken fauna`: creatures you have met.
- Admin: `/awaken fauna census` (role counts near you, loaded count, game rule, time), `goto <greenwild|sunscorch|
  frostwild>` (nearest biome of the family, found from the live biome spawns), `reveal`, `summon <id> [level]`,
  `lineup` (all twelve in a row, NoAI, tagged `awaken_lineup`), `beasts [reset]`, `why <id>` (would this creature
  spawn where you stand, and if not the rule that says no: "wrong time of day", "inside a Hearth radius"...).
- `/gamerule awaken:fauna_spawning false` turns the layer off without touching saves.

## Tests (`FaunaTests`)

`fauna_category_and_bestiary`, `fauna_biome_modifiers_decode` (every modifier through the registry, the plains, desert,
snowy taiga and dripstone spawn lists, no fauna in vanilla's categories), `fauna_spawn_rules_matrix` (awakening, hints,
Hearth, caps, beasts, windows, ground), `fauna_beast_registry`, and isolated: `fauna_spawn_rules_in_world` (real
light in sealed rooms, day/night, the Hearth check, the game rule, the role cap with real creatures),
`fauna_natural_spawning_end_to_end` (vanilla's `NaturalSpawner` places creatures through the biome modifier and the
rules), `fauna_hunters_keep_the_light_rule`, `fauna_sighting_discovery` (in view, behind a wall, behind you),
`fauna_reveal_on_awakening`, `fauna_named_beast_drops`. The mobs module's bestiary tests cover the defs too (loot
tables, eggs, bosses list). In-world tests build on a platform 65 blocks above their origin (isolated batches reuse
the same spot and other tests leave structures there), wait for the light engine before judging light, and use a
plain survival `ServerPlayer` (the helper's mock player always answers "creative", which hunters ignore).

## Art

`python3 tools/art/build_all.py fauna` writes textures, `python3 tools/art/specs/fauna.py content` the JSON, and
`python3 tools/art/specs/fauna.py preview <dir>` renders every creature on its real model (three day views, one night
view with the glow layer) plus a size line-up next to a player-height bar. Use the preview before shipping a creature.

Autopilot: `tools/autopilot/fauna.txt` (hints, reveal, line-up by day and night, each family by day and night, a beast,
the Codex).

## Known gaps

- Not seen in a real client yet (builders don't run one): poses and the reveal overlay are checked only in the
  offline preview and by the autopilot script.
- Named beasts spawn naturally (rare, gated by the registry), not placed by world generation; "on the Gate Map" is
  for the gates module.
- No mounting or taming yet; no custom sounds (voices borrow vanilla sounds).

## SPEC-worthy decisions (for the integrator)

1. New spawn category `awaken:fauna` (16 per player) via NeoForge enum extension; `enumExtensions` in mods.toml.
2. Role caps per player: ambient 8 (3 before awakening, 35% per candidate), neutral 5, hunter 5, beast 1.
3. Hunters: vanilla monster spawn light rule; hostile only while the player stands in block light < 8 and is not
   under an open daytime sky; they melt away after 10 s of open daylight.
4. Nothing but ambient creatures within 80 blocks of a Hearth anchor.
5. Content level: player level - 1 capped at the rank's gate content level (beasts: player level, cap +2).
6. Named beasts: level 3+, 40+ blocks away, 30 min world cooldown, 384 block spacing, 12 max; 45% Power Core;
   first kill pays 0.5 level; first sighting 0.1 level (beasts 0.25).
7. The reveal plays only for players with a real client, in the overworld; old awakened saves get it once.
8. Game rule `awaken:fauna_spawning` is the fauna setting (rollback switch).

# P2: the rest of the land, and lairs for the named beasts

Plan v2 P2 ("the living world"). Six more biome families (22 creatures) so that every land biome of the overworld has
its own creatures, and a lair placed by world generation for each of the nine named beasts (P1's three included).
The P1 rules hold for every new creature unchanged: the `awaken:fauna` category and its caps, the awakened gating, the
exact Hearth exclusion (`HearthHooks.inside`), sightings and discovery XP, the `awaken:fauna_spawning` game rule.

Code: `mobs/def/WildBestiary.java` (the defs), `mobs/entity/WildTalents.java` and `WildGoals.java` (the new talents),
`mobs/client/model/WildModels.java` (four new bodies), `FaunaMob` (talent hooks, frog keyframes, wall climbing,
running on water), `world/fauna/Lairs.java`, `LairRegistry.java`, `LairCommands.java`, `FaunaItems.java`,
`world/fauna/lair/**` (structure, piece, builder, styles), tests in `world/fauna/WildTests.java` and
`mobs/entity/WildTalentTests.java`. Everything else (textures, loot, biome modifiers, lair structures, structure sets,
biome tags, lang) is generated by `tools/art/specs/fauna2.py`.

## Lairs

Each named beast has a lair, a small structure placed by world generation: `awaken:lair/<beast>` (structure type
`awaken:fauna_lair`, one code-built piece; `data/awaken/worldgen/structure/lair/`), its own structure set
(`.../structure_set/lair/`, random spread) and biome tag (`#awaken:has_lair/<beast>`, the family's biomes); all nine in
`#awaken:fauna_lairs` (`/locate structure #awaken:fauna_lairs`).

| beast | lair | spacing | look |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Briar Sow | the Bramble Wallow | 112 chunks (1,792 blocks) | a mud wallow with a puddle, churned edges, fallen mossy logs, scarred stumps, a ring of berry bushes |
| The Dune Wyrm | the Glass Hollow | 112 | a sand hollow with fused-glass patches, a wyrm's spine and ribcage in bone, broken sandstone pillars (red in the badlands) |
| The Old Antler | the Antler Grove | 112 | six pale spruce "antler posts" with tines and soul lanterns round a mossy altar (snowy where it's cold) |
| The Mire Mother | the Toad Throne | 96 (1,536) | a mud mound crowned with moss and froglights amid lily-padded pools, mangrove roots, fireflies |
| The Plumed Coil | the Serpent Shrine | 96 | an overgrown stone-brick platform, vine-hung broken pillars, a leafy nest, a carved serpent post, bamboo |
| The Weaver Queen | the Silk Hollow | 96 | a hollow trunk full of silk, four dead trunks strung with sagging cobweb strands, bones and a skull (pale oak in the pale garden) |
| The Empress Moth | the Petal Bower | 96 | four cherry trunks under a leaf dome, hanging lanterns and spore blossoms, a carpet of petals, azaleas |
| The Stonehorn | the Ram's Cairn | 96 | a stone ledge with a cairn, standing stones and boulders (snow and packed ice on the cold peaks) |
| The Mycelord | the Spore Ring | 40 (640) | a fairy ring of eight toadstools with shroomlights round a mycelium clearing |

Placement (`LairStructure.findSite`): the candidate chunk and up to 11 more spots within six chunks of it; the cheap
biome test first (near sea level, or at the surface for the peaks), then dry ground (shallow bog up to 2 deep for the
Mire Mother, built at the water line), a slope limit across the floor (4-7 blocks, 16 for the Stonehorn), the floor
at the mean height. The floor disc is flattened by the beardifier (NeoForge `PieceBeardifierModifier`, BEARD_THIN on
the floor only) and built of mud, gravel, stone, moss or sand so trees don't grow through it; the ring outside follows
the terrain column by column. Rare by design: about one candidate per 1,500-2,500 blocks of world, and only where the
biome and ground fit (on a test overworld, 28 of 264 candidate chunks became lairs). The Mycelord's set is denser (640
blocks) because mushroom islands are rare; the Stonehorn's lairs are the rarest (steep ground).

Run time (`Lairs`): a lair is learnt when its chunk loads (`ChunkEvent.Load` reads the chunk's structure starts, no
searching) into `awaken:fauna_lairs` (overworld saved data: start chunk, beast, den, resident beast, when it fell).
Every two seconds, for each overworld player within 96 blocks of a loaded lair: if its beast isn't home, the beast
appears at the den under the named-beast rules (the game rule, not Peaceful, the player awakened and level 3+, not
inside a Hearth radius, no other beast within 128 blocks of the player) with the den as its territory; standing within
20 blocks of the den finds the lair (discovery `lair/<beast>`, 0.15 level, a toast, remembered as found). When its
beast falls the lair stays quiet for an hour of play (`Lairs.RESPAWN`), then a new one waits there. Lairs are empty
before awakening: the world is still vanilla then.

Hearing of a lair (the Now tracker and the Codex): the first time a level-3+ player sights any creature of a family
(or its beast, or a beast turns on them), or on reaching level 3 for each family already met, a rumour: the nearest lair
of that family's beast within 3,000 blocks (the world's registry first, else one structure lookup over a few candidate
chunks, one rumour a tick at most; a miss isn't retried for 10 minutes). Toast "Heard of · The Bramble Wallow / The
Briar Sow's lair · 1.2 km NE"; the Now tracker then shows "Lair of the Briar Sow · 1.2 km NE" (priority 30, a compass
target) until the player has stood in it, unless its beast fell recently. The Codex entry of a beast says "You have
heard where its lair is" or "You have found its lair" (counters `awaken:lair_heard/<beast>`,
`awaken:discovered/lair/<beast>`). Known lairs live in the player's `awaken:fauna_state` (schema 2, at most 24).

## Commands (admin)

- `/awaken fauna goto <family>` takes the new families (`mire`, `canopy`, `duskwood`, `blossom`, `crag`, `spore`).
- `/awaken fauna lineup [all|p1|p2|<family>]`.
- `/awaken fauna lair list | goto <beast> | place <beast> | hear <beast> | why | reset`: lairs the world knows; the
  nearest lair of a beast (or one built in front of you if none is within reach); build one in front of you; a rumour;
  would the nearest lair wake its beast for you, and if not why not; every lair waits for a new beast again.

## Tests

`fauna_p2_bestiary`, `fauna_p2_biome_modifiers` (every P2 modifier through the registry, the biomes' spawn lists, the
handover), `fauna_lair_structures` (structures, sets, tags through the registry; spacing; biomes are the family's),
`fauna_lair_placement_rules` (the real overworld noise generator and biome source for a fixed seed: every lair the
placement finds is in its beast's biome, on dry or shallow ground, flat enough, within reach of its start chunk; some
exist, and they are rare), `fauna_state_codec_keeps_lairs`, and isolated: `fauna_p2_families_spawn` (vanilla's spawner
in each family's biome by day and by night with the real clock set: the family's creatures and nothing else, each in

attempts by day and night: no role passes its cap), `fauna_lair_styles_build` (every lair style and look built: the den
is standing room with headroom; writes `run/fauna_lairs/*.json` for `fauna2.py lairs`), `fauna_lair_beast_waits` (a

queues one, a found lair leaves the tracker), `fauna_p2_talents_day` and `fauna_p2_talents_dark` (each talent called

the dark, the owl's glowing mark, a planted mushroom; and every P2 creature spawns with its goals). P1's counts were
updated (34 creatures). In-world tests put the shared test spot back as they found it (the wallow's water once ran into
P1's patch).

## Art

`python3 tools/art/build_all.py fauna2` writes the textures (skin and glow for 22 creatures, eggs, six parts, twelve
materials), `python3 tools/art/specs/fauna2.py content` the JSON, `python3 tools/art/specs/fauna2.py preview <dir>`
renders every P2 creature on its real model (three day views, a night view with the glow layer), a size line-up and the
icons, and `python3 tools/art/specs/fauna2.py lairs <dir>` draws the lairs the GameTest built. Honest notes from the
previews: the lizards, frogs, panther, coil, snails, moths, kite, goats and mushrooms read well; the Hollow Owl is a
big parrot body and reads as an owl mostly by its colours and ear tufts; the Bogwick's lantern half is a block (the bee
body is a box). P1's `fauna.py content` now merges its lang into the shared file instead of replacing it.

Autopilot: `tools/autopilot/fauna2.txt` (awaken, first power, level 3, every family lined up by day and by night, all
22 for scale, each family in its biome by day and night with the census, a rumour on the tracker, a lair visited with
its beast home, two more lair styles built in front of the camera, the Codex).

## Known gaps (P2)

- Not seen in a real client yet: the new bodies, frog keyframes and lairs are checked in the offline previews and by
  the autopilot script only.
- The Gate Map can't show lairs: that is the gates module's map (request below). The Now tracker and the Codex do.
- Talent world changes respect the Hearth radius but have no extra off switch beyond the fauna game rule.
- The Stonehorn's lairs are rare (steep ground); the roaming Stonehorn fills in.

## SPEC-worthy decisions (P2, for the integrator)

1. Six more families: the Mire, the Canopy, Duskwood, Blossomwood, the Crags, Sporewild (22 creatures; see the table).
   Greenwild hands over the dark forest, cherry grove, flower and birch forests; Sunscorch keeps savanna and badlands.
2. Every named beast has a lair placed by world generation: one structure set each, random spread 112 chunks (P1's
   three) or 96 (P2's), 40 for the Mycelord; only where the biome and ground fit.
3. A lair's beast appears when an awakened level-3+ player is within 96 blocks of the loaded lair, under the
   named-beast rules; a felled lair beast returns after an hour of play. Lair beasts count for beast spacing, not for
   the roaming cooldown or ceiling.
4. Roaming named beasts now spawn at weight 1 (was 3).
5. Rumours: first sighting of a family's creature at level 3+ (or reaching level 3) tells of that family's nearest lair
   within 3,000 blocks; finding a lair (within 20 blocks of its den) pays 0.15 level once per beast kind.
6. Twelve family materials `<family>_<thing>` (list above) for Hunter gear.
7. Talents that change blocks (lichen, flowers, small and giant mushrooms) never act inside a Hearth radius.
