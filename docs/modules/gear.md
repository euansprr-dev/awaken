# Hunter gear (gear module)

Plan v2 §4.5: the start is no longer "go find iron". Hunter gear is crafted from what the awakened world drops
(creature materials, essence, beast trophies, Gate Lord parts), in tiers by rank, and sits beside vanilla gear rather
than replacing it. E stays hide and bone. Since "Between the Gates" W2 (SPEC §28.3), every piece from D up also needs its
tier's metal from the deep: hide from the hunt, metal from the deep, light from the gates.

Code: `src/main/java/dev/awaken/gear/**` (`HunterTier`, `Piece`, `GearItems`, `HunterGearItem`, `SetBonus`,
`GearCombat`, `GearNow`, `GearCommands`, `GearState`, `Silhouette`, `GearTests`) and `gear/client/**` (`GearClient`,
`HunterSense`, `Silhouettes`).
Wired by one line each in `Awaken.java` and `AwakenClient.java`. Everything else (textures, item models and
definitions, equipment assets, recipes, tags, level-road steps, lang) is generated by `tools/art/specs/gear.py`.

## The tiers

Six tiers are built (E to S), seven pieces each: four armour pieces, a melee **blade**, a power **focus** and a
**mattock** (one head for stone, wood and earth). E to B sit on a vanilla equivalent; A and S go past netherite. Every
tier adds a twist through its set bonus, and the late two change your silhouette.

| Tier | Set | Vanilla twin | Armour (H/C/L/B), toughness | Blade | Focus | Mattock mines like |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E | **Wildhide** (Hood, Vest, Leggings, Boots, Fang, Charm, Mattock) | stone sword, copper armour | 2/4/3/1 = 10, 0 | 5 dmg, 1.8/s | +6% power dmg | stone pick, speed 4.5 |
| D | **Beastbound** (Helm, Hauberk, Greaves, Boots, Cleaver, Totem, Mattock) | iron | 2/6/5/2 = 15, 0.5 | 6 dmg, 1.7/s | +9% | iron pick, 6.0 |
| C | **Gatewrought** (Helm, Cuirass, Greaves, Sabatons, Sabre, Lens, Mattock) | diamond | 3/8/6/3 = 20, 2.0 | 7 dmg, 1.7/s | +12% | diamond pick, 7.5 |
| B | **Veilwoven** (Cowl, Mantle, Leggings, Treads, Edge, Censer, Mattock) | netherite | 3/8/7/3 = 21, 2.5, kb 0.05 | 8 dmg, 1.7/s | +15% | netherite pick, 8.5 |
| A | **Lordscale** (Greathelm, Warplate, Tassets, Talons, Warblade, Orb, Mattock) | past netherite | 3/9/7/3 = 22, 3.0, kb 0.1 | 9 dmg, 1.7/s | +18% | netherite pick, 9.5 |
| S | **Sovereign Weave** (Crown, Regalia, Legwraps, Striders, Oathblade, Sceptre, Mattock) | past netherite | 4/9/7/4 = 24, 3.5, kb 0.15 | 10 dmg, 1.8/s | +22% | netherite pick, 11 |

Blades hit exactly like the vanilla sword of their twin but swing a little faster (they're hunters' knives); armour
durability multipliers 13/18/33/35/40/48 (Gatewrought's raised to diamond's with its armour, W2, so a diamond spent on
it beats a diamond chestplate; Veilwoven and Lordscale a point of armour up to keep the ladder climbing); blades 220/500/1400/1900/2300/2800; foci 180/400/1100/1600/2000/2500;
mattocks 240/520/1450/1950/2350/2850; melee of foci 3/4/5/6/7/8 and mattocks 3/4/5/6/7/8 (with the base 1). A and S
equip with netherite's sound; S stays under the armour attribute's cap of 30.
The mattock (from D up it needs its tier's metal, like every piece) mines like its vanilla twin: a D mattock mines
diamonds. Ore Sense, the strike's feel and the heavy strike treat it as a pick (`#awaken:mining_tools`).
The focus is a club in melee (3/4/5/6 damage, slow) and raises power damage while held in **either** hand (the best
focus counts, they don't stack); on a boss the raised hit still respects powers' 20%-of-health cap.

### Set bonuses (the twist)

Two pieces of a tier give its half bonus, four the full one. Tiers mix freely with each other and with vanilla armour
(two Wildhide + two Beastbound give both half bonuses).

| Set | Tied to | 2 pieces | 4 pieces |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wildhide | Perception, sneaking | sneak 15% faster (`sneaking_speed` +0.15) | +0.30, and **Hunter's Sense**: while sneaking, living creatures within 12 + PER/2 blocks (max 32) are outlined through walls, amber for animals and the awakened creatures, crimson for monsters (client only) |
| Beastbound | Strength, Vitality | +1 heart | +2 hearts, and **Cornered**: below half health, +15% melee damage (+1% per 2 STR above 5, max +30%) and +0.3 knockback resistance |
| Gatewrought | the Five Forces | Force damage taken -15% | -25%, and **Force-primed strikes**: a power hit primes your next melee hit within 4 s: +35%, and it carries the Force (Heat burns 3 s, Frost slows 2 s, Charge +15% more, Gravity lifts, Spirit heals you 15% of the hit) |
| Veilwoven | the Veil, Spirit | Spirit damage taken -30%; meditating mends 1 HP every 2 s | **Veil Flicker**: below 40% health a hit can pass through you (10% + 1% per SPI above 5, max 35%; once per 8 s; never against `bypasses_invulnerability`) |
| Lordscale | the Lords' Forces | Gravity and Charge damage taken -20% (the Sky Leviathan's and the Herald's) | **Overflow**: every fifth power hit of one Force (Kinetic counts as its own) bursts round the target: half the hit to every foe within 4 blocks, with the Force's touch (Heat burns 2 s, Frost slows 2 s, Charge +15%, Gravity lifts, Spirit mends you 10% of it, at most 4 HP; Kinetic knocks back). Bosses take at most 20% of their health from it; players, Echoes, spirits, townsfolk and tamed animals are never caught |
| Sovereign Weave | the Hollow Court, the self | **Unbowed**: dying in a gate below your rank leaves no Fracture (a Fracture from before stays) | **Sovereign's Measure**: your highest stat counts half again: its points above 5 add half again to its attribute bonus (STR attack, AGI speed and swing under progression's caps, VIT health), powers hit +0.75% a point above 5, and Hunter's Sense, Cornered and Veil Flicker read the raised value |

Attribute parts (`SetBonus.refresh`) are re-applied every 10 ticks per player (staggered by entity id); the max-health
modifiers are permanent so a relog never clamps your health under them, the rest are transient. Unbowed listens to
player deaths at HIGHEST (remember the Fracture) and LOWEST (after the gates' handler at HIGH: if that death
Fractured you and the monster that killed or last hurt you belonged to a gate below your rank, `GateHooks.get` gives
its rank, the Fracture is taken back). Combat parts are one
`LivingIncomingDamageEvent` listener at LOW priority (`GearCombat`), so a hit someone else cancelled never spends a
primed strike.

### The Forge

Every piece is temperable at the Hearth Forge and by Oren (armour is `Equippable`, everything else carries attack
damage): tempering adds its own modifiers on top of the tier's stats, tempered pieces still count for set bonuses, and
a tempered **focus** also gains +2% power damage per temper level (read from the `awaken:temper` component by id, so
gear never depends on hearth classes).

## Crafting and unlocks

All 28 recipes are shaped crafting-table recipes (`data/awaken/recipe/<item>.json`, category equipment, grouped per
piece so the book shows one button per piece cycling through the tiers). They unlock in the recipe book the first time
you hold any ingredient (progression's rule) and by the level road. No Hunter's Bench: the first set has to be makeable
in the first hour with no new block, and one familiar place for all tiers keeps the recipe book the guide. The Forge
could take the higher tiers later (see Requests).

Ingredients use hook tags, so new creature families join by adding a tag file to their own merge folder
(`src/main/merge/<module>/data/awaken/tags/item/hunter_hide_d.json` with `{"values": ["awaken:their_hide"]}`):

| Tag | E | D | C | B |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hunter_hide_<t>` (armour bulk) | leather, rabbit hide, phantom membrane | leather, turtle scute, armadillo scute, phantom membrane | turtle scute, armadillo scute, phantom membrane | turtle scute, armadillo scute, shulker shell |
| `hunter_core_<t>` (the signature part) | bone, flint, quartz | the named beasts' Briar Tusk, Wyrm Scale, Antler Tine; the E-rank Gate Lords' parts | the D-rank Gate Lords' parts | the C- and B-rank Gate Lords' parts |
| `hunter_bind_<t>` (bindings, fittings; also repairs) | string, feather, moss block | copper, iron or gold ingot | gold ingot, emerald, amethyst shard | echo shard, dream residue, ender pearl |

| Tag | A (Lordscale) | S (Sovereign Weave) |
|---|---|---|
| `hunter_hide_<t>` | Precursor alloy, netherite scrap, shulker shell | starmetal ingot (W2; unused by S's recipes, which take it as `M`) |
| `hunter_core_<t>` | the B and A Lords' parts (Leviathan Fin, Sky Pearl, Herald's Halo, Abyssal Glass) and the deep guardians' (Furnace Engine Core, Drowned Moon Scale, Architect's Key) | the Herald's parts, the Architect's Key, and the Hollow Court's when you get there (Regent's Shadow, the eight Sovereigns' hearts and crowns, the Seed Shard) |
| `hunter_bind_<t>` | diamond, wind charge, Precursor circuit | echo shard, dream residue |
| `tier_metal_<t>` (W2, the `M` slot) | netherite scrap | starmetal ingot |

A's chest, blade and focus take a Lord's Heart; S's helmet, chest, blade, focus and mattock do. Every A and S piece is
craftable at its road level from the rank below's content (A at B-rank from the Sky Leviathan, the Foundry and the
Inverted Sea; S at A-rank from the Herald and the Dreaming), never from something you can't reach yet.

**The deep's metal (W2, SPEC §28.3).** From D up every recipe has an `M` slot that takes only its tier's metal,
`#awaken:tier_metal_<t>` (D iron ingot, C diamond, B Deepglass shard, A netherite scrap, S starmetal ingot): 2 for a
helmet, 3 a chest, 2 legs, 2 boots, 1 blade, 1 focus, 2 mattock, about 13 a tier. Every focus from D up takes gold (`G`:
1 at D, 2 from C; gold is the power's metal), every Veilwoven piece an amethyst shard (`Y`). The hook tags keep their
other options for the other slots; `hunter_hide_s` is starmetal now. E is unchanged. Forged Arms weapons from D up take
the metal in their bind cell.

Every recipe also takes essence shards (E 1 each; D 1-2; C/B 1-3) and C/B chests and foci a Lord's Heart. The whole
E kit is 15 hide, 7 essence, a few bones or flint, sinew and two sticks: each biome family can make it from its own
first-hour drops (Greenwild: Gloomstalker leather and string, Mossback moss, flint; Sunscorch: Dune Ray membrane,
Gloomstalker string, Glass Scarab quartz; Frostwild: Frost Stag leather, Wisp Hound bone, Frostfinch feathers). Other
modules' items in the tags are optional entries: a rename never breaks loading, and `gear_registry_and_hooks` flags it.

### Now tracker

One low-priority line (25, an idea) when the player holds what the next tier of a piece needs, doesn't own it yet, and
it would beat what they wear (armour value) or carry (melee damage, mining speed): "You can craft a Wildhide Vest".
Highest tier first, then blade, chest, legs, helmet, boots, focus, mattock. Worked out at most once a second per player.

## How to try it

- `/awaken gear`: the sets you wear, which bonuses are on, your focus.
- Admin: `/awaken gear kit <E|D|C|B|A|S>` wears a whole tier (blade in the main hand, focus in the off hand, mattock in the
  pack); `/awaken gear lineup [clear]` puts every tier on mannequins six blocks ahead (blade and focus on the first of
  each pair, the mattock on the second).
- Survival: awaken, hunt at night, hold leather: the Wildhide recipes appear in the book.
- Autopilot: `tools/autopilot/gear.txt` (awakening, first power, the line-up from the front, close-ups, the backs, each
  tier held in first person by day and the Veilwoven Edge at night, `/awaken gear` in chat).

## Art

`python3 tools/art/build_all.py gear` (42 icons, glow layers for the C, B, A and S blade and focus, 6 sets x 2 armour
layers, 2 silhouette textures, the Power Core Shard), `python3 tools/art/specs/gear.py content`,
`python3 tools/art/specs/gear.py preview DIR` (the icon sheet; `gear_figures.png` every set on a 3D figure, three-quarter
front and back; `gear_late.png` A and S large from the front, three-quarter, side and back; the raw layers and the
silhouette textures). The figures are drawn by a small renderer in gear.py with ModelPart's own cube, UV, mirror and
pose maths, so the preview shows what the client builds. Ramps: E warm hide, bone, cream sinew and an amber bead; D a darker beast
pelt under tusk plates with bronze and the green D gem; C gate slate, brass and signal-blue runes; B Veil violet,
mirror silver and spirit glow; A the Lords' black scale (4 x 3 scallops with a lit lip), molten gold only at the rims, the
Sky Leviathan's pearl in the visor slit and the sternum, the amber A gem; S a royal void-violet twill, gold filigree,
ivory at the shoulders, crimson linings and jewels. The rank colour is always the gem. Hoods (E, B, S) leave the face
open; D a nasal guard; C a visor slit; A a closed greathelm with a pearl slit. The A and S icons are hand-laid 16 px
grids (`grid()`), the rest procedural.

### Silhouettes (A and S)

The late sets change the armour's shape, not only its paint. `tools/art/specs/gear.py` lists extra cubes per set
(`SILHOUETTE`), packs their faces into a 128 x 64 texture whose top-left 64 x 32 is the normal layer
(`textures/entity/equipment/silhouette/<set>.png`) and writes the parts to `assets/awaken/gear_silhouettes/<set>.json`.
The client (`gear.client.Silhouettes`, an `IClientItemExtensions` on the A and S helmet, chest and boots) builds
vanilla's armour mesh for the slot plus those parts, bakes it at 128 x 64 and swaps the texture to the silhouette one;
leggings keep the vanilla model. If the file is missing or wrong it logs once and draws the plain armour with the normal
layer, so nothing ever renders with mismatched UVs.

|---|---|---|---|
| Lordscale | two horns sweeping out and up from the temples (two segments, gold tips), a gold crest from brow to nape | three-plate pauldrons with a hanging lower plate on each shoulder, three gold-tipped spines down the back | three gold claws on each toe, a heel spur |
| Sovereign Weave | a gold crown round the hood: band, a tall front spire and four more, crimson-tipped | a standing collar fanned behind the head (gold outside, crimson lining, wider than the hood so it frames the face), a short cape with a gold border and the Sovereign's mark that clears the legs, disc pauldrons with gold fringe | gold fins at the ankles |

## Known gaps

- Not seen in a real client yet (builders don't run one): icons, layers and the A/S silhouettes are judged on the
  preview sheets (the 3D figures use the client's own cube maths), Hunter's Sense only by code review; the autopilot
  scripts (`gear.txt`, `requests.txt`) show the rest to the integrator.
- The cape and pauldrons are rigid (they move with the body and arms, no cloth sway); a baby zombie wearing A or S
  armour gets the plain model.
- Sovereign's Measure lifts every power hit by its highest stat's share, whichever stat that power scales with
  (powers don't expose which), and doesn't reach energy regen.
- Hunter's Sense can't be shown by the autopilot (it can't hold sneak).
- Tempering cost is the Forge's (iron, gold, diamonds); a tempered piece crafted into a new tier starts fresh.
- Repair tags are unchanged by W2 (the binds, not the metals).
