# Onboarding and progression (plan v2, P1 "the first hour", and the level road to 50)

Built in `progression/**` (not `progression/client`), `story/**`, `powers/Starter*` + `powers/net/StarterPayloads` +
`powers/client/Starter*`, data under `data/awaken/awaken/{directive,drill,level_road}`, `tools/content/progression_content.py`,
`tools/sim/pacing_sim.py`, `tools/autopilot/onboarding.txt`.

## What changed for the player

| Moment | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Prologue (Bell Crypt) | kills and floors paid 0 XP | everything earned before awakening is **banked** (`Profile.bankedXp`) and paid on awakening, Satchel claims included |
| Accept | "[Y]/[N]" window; "The gate is still open. Finish what you started." | choice card **[Y] Accept · [N] Accept anyway**; toast "Accepted. / The gate will wait. It never breaks."; waking is worth at least **level 2** (more if the prologue went well) |
| First power | none | **three cards** (one per family) from Haymaker, Shockwave, Charged Shot, Beam, Spirit Palm, Shadow Step; click or press 1/2/3; learned and put on a key |
| Truths | 3 offered at minute 3 | first offered at **level 5** (a calm toast); players awakened before v2 keep theirs and get no second set |
| Directives / drills | asked for powers, Dash, meditation, Veil | every one has `requires`; never offered when unmet; first-hour pool doable at level 2 with one power |
| Home XP | flat cap 1.5% of a level per minute | **diminishing returns per source per ~10 min**, tapered for out-levelled content; banked before awakening |
| Levels | +3 points only | +3 points and a **level-road unlock** (toast), 1–12 every level plus 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50 |
| Recipe book | mod recipes never appeared | a mod recipe unlocks the first time you hold one of its ingredients; the road adds some on its own |
| "What now?" | nothing | Now lines: Choose your first power · K (85), Reassessment ready (72), Bell Crypt · floor N (70, with its position), Spend N points · K (55), Level 6: Hearth Anchor (15) |

| 11 | Meditation | recipe meditation_mat, flag `awaken:meditation` |

| 50 | The Hollow Court stirs | flag `awaken:hollow_court`, two cores |

Dash sits at 3, not 2: Accept already puts you at level 2, so level 2's step lands beside the first-power cards; at 3
(~30 min) Dash is its own moment. The first hour then has something new about every 10–15 minutes (power at ~8 min,
Dash ~28, a core ~40, Truths ~47).

Rules: one JSON file per step; **the file name is the unlock id** saved in the profile (`Profile.unlocks`), so never
rename one. Steps are granted on every level reached and on awakening; on login, any step at or below your level that
the profile lacks is granted with one summary toast ("N unlocks caught up"). An id the table no longer has is kept
and logged, never revoked. A `power` grant never levels up a power you already own. Flags are counters
`awaken:road/<name>`, readable as `Progression.hasFlag(player, "awaken:<name>")` (server and client) or as a
`{"type":"flag","flag":"awaken:road/<name>"}` condition. Other modules may gate features on them; nothing is gated by
this module except Directive/drill offers.

## Requirements (`progression/Requirements`)

`"requires": [...]` on Directives and drills. Tokens: `power:<id>`, `dash`, `movement`, `active`, `forces`,
`family:<family>` (answered by the powers module), `unlock:<flag>`, `level:N`, `hearth`, `counter:<id>`. Unknown tokens
count as unmet and are logged once (a GameTest checks every token in the data is well formed).

## XP rework (`progression/XpBudget`, `Progression.worldXp`, `ProgressionModule.homeXp`)

Each repeatable source (`kill`, `ore`, `build`, or any module's own key through `Progression.worldXp(player, key,
fractionOfLevel)`) keeps a tally of recent grants that decays with a 10-minute time constant. Below its budget it pays
in full; above it every extra bit pays `budget / tally` (exactly `budget · ln(after/before)`), so one activity levels off
at about one budget per 10 minutes and mixed play is never cut. Budgets per 10 min: kill 0.15, ore 0.06, build 0.03,
anything else 0.25 of a level. World kills, ores and blocks are tapered by `1 / (1 + max(0, level − 1 − contentLevel)/10)`
(vanilla mobs are content level 0; AWAKEN mobs use their `MobRole` content level). Discoveries (`Progression.discover`)
are first-time only and not budgeted. Regimen pays a tenth of a level (was a quarter: a morning comes every 20 minutes).

Gate floor shares (`Rank.share`, used by `Curve.floorReward`): E .28 (was .81), D .16 (.39), C .065 (.25), B .22 (.23),
A .60 (.18), S .55 (.12), Sovereign .50 (.12). C is the long middle; from B on, gates pay more per level so the climb
from 50 to 135 takes ~45 hours.

## Simulator results (`python3 tools/sim/pacing_sim.py`, 300 runs per player type, seed 42; `tools/sim/pacing_results.json`)

Targets (balanced player): level 5 in 45–60 min, 10 in 3–4 h, 25 in 15–20 h, 50 in 55–60 h, 135 in 95–105 h; level
3–4 at 30 min, 5–6 at 60 min. **All pass.**

| Player | Lv at 30 / 60 min | Lv 5 | Lv 10 | Lv 25 | Lv 50 | Lv 70 | Lv 135 | deaths / 10 gates |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| balanced | 3 / 5 | 47 min | 3.6 h | 17.0 h | 56.0 h | 73 h | 101 h | 1.7 |
| careful | 2 / 5 | 50 min | 4.0 h | 19.5 h | 63.1 h | 85 h | 120 h | 1.0 |
| greedy | 3 / 5 | 46 min | 3.3 h | 17.1 h | 62.1 h | 78 h | 104 h | 6.7 |
| builder | 2 / 5 | 49 min | 3.7 h | 17.5 h | 57.0 h | 76 h | 105 h | 1.9 |

First power at minute 8 for everyone. A mob farm (60 kills every 10 minutes, nothing else) pays 0.8 levels/h at level
3, 0.5 at 10, 0.28 at 25, 0.16 at 50. Push-EV on a C gate still peaks at floor 4 (the extract-or-push choice holds).

What the sim models: the prologue bank (1–6 kills) and the level-2 floor; overworld minutes with night kills, ores,
building, first sightings and anomalies through the same budget maths as the game; one Regimen, Directive and dream
chance per play-day; rank trials; gate runs with travel (3–8 min after the Bell Crypt) and the v1 death model.
Assumed for other modules (their builders should match or tell me): a first sighting pays ~0.12 of a level, ~10 in the
first hour, ~140 over the game; an anomaly every ~9 outdoor minutes, a rift tear paying `0.3 × Curve.floorReward(rank, 1)`
(use `Progression.XpSource.ANOMALY`); dreams ~0.1 level on 35% of play-days.

**Endgame pacing (resolved 2026-10-23):** level 135 used to land at ~145 h. B now pays .22 and A/S/Sovereign .60/.55/.50
(C drops to .065 to keep level 50 at ~56 h), so the balanced player reaches 135 at ~101 h, matching the ~100-hour story.
The Seed's level requirement is unchanged.

## How to try it

- Fresh survival world: the full flow (`tools/autopilot/onboarding.txt` scripts it, with screenshots).
- `/awaken admin story prologue` opens the Bell Crypt now; `/awaken admin story enter` walks in; `/damage @s 1000`
  inside is the scripted death.
- `/awaken powers starter` shows the cards again; `/awaken powers starter 2` picks the second;
  `/awaken admin powers starter reset` clears the choice (powers you own stay).
- `/awaken admin levels 5` walks the road; `/awaken why` lists every unlock, bank payout and first-power roll.

## Known gaps

- The cards have no 3-second preview (plan §4.1); hovering a card shows its full description instead.
- Truth rewards are shown by the Status screen (another builder); the Truth toast only points there.
- "Bell Crypt · floor N" counts floors cleared while you were inside the Bell Crypt; for a save from before v2 it starts
  at floor 1.

## SPEC-worthy decisions (for the integrator)

1. §2: `Rank.share` E .28, D .16, C .065, B .22, A .60, S .55, Sovereign .50; Regimen = 0.1 × xpToNext(L) + 1 point.
2. §4.3 replaced: home XP = kill 4% × min(2, hp/20), ore 1%, block 0.05% of a level, each through diminishing returns
   per source (tally with a 10-min time constant; full below budget, then budget/tally; budgets kill .15, ore .06,
   build .03, other .25 per 10 min), tapered 1/(1 + levels above content level / 10). Discoveries are uncapped.
3. §4.1: Profile schema 2 adds `banked_xp` and `unlocks` (defaults 0 / empty). XP before awakening is banked.
4. §4.5: Truths are first offered at level 5 (level-road flag `awaken:truths`), then at each rank-up as before.
5. §4.8/4.9: Directives and drills have `requires`; unmet ones are never offered. Directive cards are choice cards
   ("Take it on" / "Let it pass").
6. New §4.11 Level road: data `level_road/*.json` {level, title, text, grants[power|recipe|item|power_core|flag], quiet};
   ids saved, login backfill, flags `awaken:road/<name>`.
7. §6: the first power is a choice of three E-tier actives from different families (`powers/Starter.POOL`), saved in
   attachment `awaken:first_power` {offer, chosen}; v1 saves that already own an attack power are marked chosen.
8. §10: Accept tops the player up to level 2 (`Prologue.WAKE_LEVEL`); the Accept card is a choice card.
9. Mod recipes unlock when an ingredient is first held (checked every 40 ticks per player against a cached map).
