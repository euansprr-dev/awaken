The short answer

Your idea is half built already. The rope comes from the D rank fight and flight comes from the A rank fight. Those two can't be missed.

The two rungs between them are the problem. The leap waits on a count of 400 that the game never shows. Wings wait on a sky platform that nothing points at. Neither has a row on the Tasks page. The top rung arrives after the story is over. And every unlock, when it comes, is a title and one line of text.

So I recommend one rule for the whole ladder:

A rank fight opens a rung. A place gives it. The way out of the place is the move.

Most of the places are already in the game: the vault that pulls you up its pylon, the course with a gap no jump can cross, the sky platform with a windway off its pier. Today new worlds can't reach the first of those at all.

What the audit found

  1. Two rungs already come from rank fights and can't be missed: the rope at D, flight at A.
  2. The two between them wait on things the game never shows. The leap on a hidden count. Wings on a platform nobody points at. The movement code puts no row on the Tasks page at all.
  3. The leap's gate is offered once, by one toast, and is tracked nowhere.
  4. A higher rung hands you the lower ones in silence. A sky core gives the leap without its course. The Sky Warden's roof can give wings, the leap and flight in one go, mid-fight, to someone who has never glided. That is why nothing is truly impossible today. It is also why nothing gets taught.
  5. Three built things can't happen in a new world any more. The vault's rope scene, the Sky Leviathan's trial, and the first vault's promise.
  6. The K menu's page about the ladder is wrong in two of five rows, and so are the level road at 73 and the Wings trail.
  7. The top rung is given after the story ends.
  8. Every unlock is a title and one line. Six seconds of rising light, then words such as "In the air: jump again · sprint to dash · sneak high up to slam." Nothing asks you to do the move. Three of the titles are riddles by your own plain-speech rule.
  9. The moment can be buried. In today's tour of the vault, the rope's words were drawn underneath the vault's own discovery card, and neither could be read. The vault's scene does not hold other messages back.

Never stuck

If…Then
You never go to the placeThe row stays for good. The next rank fight hands the rung down as it does today, and its lesson is owed to you the next time you are free. No more silent hand-me-downs.
The place that lit up is gone or farAny place of that kind will do. The spawn vault is in every world, 240 to 360 blocks from where you started.
There is no spire near youThe second way opens the leap: enough rope. With the new sling that is a few minutes of swinging, not 200 hidden throws.
You fall during a lessonCaught as a soft landing. It costs a climb, never your pack.
You log out halfwayThe lesson plays again. A rung given is never taken back.
You fly or build up to a core or a crownRefused, as today. It answers only to the rope.

Three ways to do it

A · Repair in place

Keep every rule. Add a Tasks row for the leap and for wings, track the leap's gate, fix the stale texts.

3 to 5 hours. The hidden count, the silent hand-me-downs and the title-and-a-line all stay.

B · One road · recommended

A rank fight opens a rung, a place gives it, the way out is the move. Built from the fights, the vault, the course, the platform and the windways that exist.

16 to 24 hours. It is your own sentence, and it gives the world's places a reason again.

C · All inside the rank fights

D the rope, C the leap, B wings, A flight, Sovereign the form, each handed over mid-fight.

Nothing can be missed and nothing is found. Wings move from 10 hours to 24, and every move is taught while a boss hits you.
