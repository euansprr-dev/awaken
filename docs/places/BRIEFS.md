# Places: the briefs (written before any code, 10 Nov 2026)

Euan, 10 Nov 2026, on the 144 small finds: "this is not at all what I was asking... I meant things that you discover, like caves,
or special buildings... actual places... a really cool swamp thing with an awesome atmosphere, unique places that actually stand
out when you discover them." Then: "Build them all. Code all first, then tour multiple at a time... Really build yourself full
briefs for every single structure beforehand... the atmosphere, the blocks, everything that's needed from A to Z."

Thirty places. Each is somewhere you walk into: 36 to 90 blocks across, built for its land, with its own air (fog, light,
particles, sound) that fades in as you come close and is at full strength inside. Each has a silhouette or a sign you can see
from a distance, a way in, two or three things to do with your hands, a reward, and a name in the Journal the moment you
arrive. The stone people (the Stilled) are who lived there. No fights. Nothing automated. Rewards are per player.

## Rules every place is built to

1. **Ground first.** The place levels its own ground and blends the edge back into the land over eight blocks. Buildings stand
   on a foot course that goes down into the ground. Nothing sits on a visible pad.
2. **No flat faces.** A wall has a foot course, a body of two mixed materials, and a top that is either a real eave or a ruined,
   ragged edge. Long walls get buttresses or pillars every four or five blocks. Windows are holes with sills or panes.
3. **Roofs are roofs.** Pitched with stairs and an overhang, or domed, or fallen in on purpose with the beams showing.
4. **Aged.** Moss low and on the north, cracks, vines on shaded faces, rubble at the foot of anything broken, leaves on roofs.
5. **Lit on purpose.** Every place has a planned light: lanterns, candles, embers, glow in water or leaves. Never bare torches in
   a row. Hidden light blocks only to lift a dark interior to where you can read it.
6. **Its own air.** One fog colour and distance, one particle, one or two vanilla recordings on a loop. Stated in each brief.
7. **A way in and a heart.** You can see where to enter. Inside there is one room or spot that is the point of the place.
8. **Water never leaks.** Any water is held by blocks on every side and below. Falling water lands in a held pool.
9. **Safe.** No drop over six blocks without water or a rail. Nothing that traps you.
10. **Not near another of its kind**, never in quiet land more than rarely, never on a structure or road.

What a brief's "You do" means in the engine: a short chain of the hand verbs already built (light, ring, turn, carry, pour,
pull, read, break, reach). Finishing pays loot and marks the place done; arriving already puts it in your Journal.

### 1. The Charcoal Burners' Clearing (`charcoal_clearing`) · forest, dark forest · rank E · 44 across

- **You get:** rank E, big.

## How they are placed

One place at most in a cell 448 blocks on a side, six times in ten where the land is lively, almost never where it is quiet,
never two of a kind within three cells. Rank by distance as everything else in the game. A cell whose ground cannot be levelled
within six blocks stays empty. The 144 small finds are no longer placed by the world or listed in the Journal.

## How they are checked

Tests over every place at once (it builds, nothing floats, nothing past its reach, its steps can be done by a scripted player and
it pays once). Then tours on GitHub's runners, six at a time, four pictures a place: from afar, from above, at the door, inside.
