Hands: the body you feel in first person

A real grip on every pipe, with a slide down it. A first-person animation for every move of The Ascent. Hands that do the work at the crafting table, the forge and the door. Everything is built on the clips, curves and springs we already use, so nothing reads as an add-on.

Plan only: no game code until you say yes. Full text: docs/anims/PLAN.md on branch pack/fp-anims.

Third person is in good shape. First person is mostly empty: five separate listeners each nudge the whole hand stack, and the pipe has no animation in either view.

02Pipes: the grip

A new Runner state, Grip, on every climb line: drainpipes, trellises, hoist ropes, hanging ladders and ladders. Walk or jump into one, or fall past it holding forward, and the body snaps to it. Both hands go round the pipe and the knees clamp it.

Input on a pipeWhat happens
NothingHold still. The head looks round freely and the body stays put.
Forward / free-runClimb at 0.2 blocks a tick, or 0.3 with the free-run. At the top, the eave's lip is caught as a hang, then you climb out (as today).
Shift held → releasedSlide, speeding up to 0.65; release to brake and hold. At the ground the body stands out of a crouch, with no fall damage.
Jump + backKick off the wall.
Jump + left / rightSwing out sideways and catch a pipe or ledge within 3 blocks, or drop clear.
Jump aloneLunge up about 1.2 blocks, at most once every 10 ticks.
Rope keyFire the rope straight from the pipe.

How do you drop off?

Recommended

A · Shift down, Jump off

Hold Shift to slide. Jump + back kicks you off. Ride the slide to the ground to step off.

On a ledge, Jump + back already kicks off and Shift already takes you down. No timing to learn, and you can't drop by accident while you slide and brake in short bursts.

Your idea

B · Double-tap Shift

Hold Shift to slide; tap twice within 6 ticks to open your hands and fall.

Easy to learn, but braking in short bursts (slide, brake, slide) reads as a double-tap and drops you mid-wall.

C · Hold Back

Hold Back for 4 ticks: you lean out and let go.

No clash with the slide, but it's a new gesture, and Back means something different on a ledge.

Engineering: vanilla caps ladder descent at 0.15 blocks a tick. Our climb blocks override NeoForge's isLadder(state, level, pos, entity) while the body grips, so the grip owns the motion on client and server with no mixin. Vanilla's own ladder gets the grip and every animation, but its slide stays at 0.15. The server checks the grip like a hang. Third-person clips (body_pipe, _hold, _slide, _kick, _side_l/_r, _hop, _land) run at the climb's speed through the library's SpeedModifier.

03The first-person system: Hands

One owner for everything drawn in first person, as the Feel Director is the one owner of the camera. The five listeners that exist today become inputs to it, so nothing stacks.

5
Camera beats: stay with the Feel Director and BodyCamera; Hands asks them for a beat.
4
Procedural: the hands trail fast turns of the view on a critically damped spring, with breath, grip tremble, landing impacts and speed sway.
3
Action: one-shot clips: mantle, ledge-up, vault, landing, kick-off, every interaction and every cast.
2
Stance: the body's ongoing state: grip, hang, wall-run, rope, wings, flight.
1
Vanilla: held item, swing, equip. Untouched when nothing plays.
RenderHandEvent · arms and items
One listener at the highest priority. It cancels vanilla's hands when a layer is active, then draws your arms (skin and sleeves, as the casts already do) and any held prop or item through the item renderer.
SubmitCustomGeometryEvent · your legs
Your knees on the pipe, legs out in a slide, feet on the wall in a hang. They are drawn in the world with real depth, and only during moves. It steps aside if a full-body first-person mod is installed.
ComputeCameraAngles · ComputeFov
As today. The hand pass keeps its own FOV, so arms never stretch with the speed FOV.
No mixins
Every hook above checked in the 26.3 sources. The library's own first-person mode stays off: the library is optional, and its first-person mode runs on mixins.
  • Same pipeline as the third-person clips. First-person poses are written in Python (fp_moves.py, fp_interact.py) in the same figure terms and easings, with a preview strip from the eye. They share one timing table with the third-person clips.
  • Smooth across views. Both clips start from the same event on the same clock, so flipping between first person and the shoulder view mid-move stays in step.
  • Input never waits. The action happens on the click and the animation catches up. Any input cuts a touch.
  • Comfort. One switch each for moves, touches and stations, plus one per station. Reduced motion halves every amplitude.

05Cinematic interactions

What Cinematic Interactions is. A Fabric mod for workstations. You click a station, the camera leans into a framed view, arms in your own skin rise in, and the crafting table becomes a real 3×3 surface where your hands lay the ingredients. It has hammer strikes at the anvil and page turns at the lectern. It has no NeoForge build for 26.3, does nothing for doors or chests, and runs on about 26 mixins that clash with Shoulder Surfing. So we build our own version, in two families.
Family A

Station scenes

The view leans in on the opening's camera-shot system. Our screen replaces the vanilla one on the same menu, so every item moves by normal menu clicks and the server stays in charge. Arms are drawn in the world; you point at the surface and click. Lean-in 0.6 s, a tap 0.2 s. Moving, jumping, E or Esc leaves. Sneak + right-click always gives the plain screen.

Family B

Touches

Quick hand moments in the flow of play, on actions that already happened. They last at most 0.3 s and never hold anything up, and any input cuts them.

What we took from the research: the lean-in and the work surface, the escape hatch to the plain screen, short taps, one switch per station, motion in code with springs. What we left out: its 1.2 s lean-in (ours is 0.6 s), the auto-walk, and the mixins. Players complain about slow storage trips, camera motion they didn't cause, and wind-ups that feel like lag; the rules above answer each one.

07What you decide

  1. Dropping off a pipe: A, Shift slides and Jump + back kicks off, or B (double-tap Shift), or C (hold Back).
  2. No keys on a pipe: hold still, or keep vanilla's slow drift.
  3. Vanilla ladders get the grip too: yes (their slide stays at vanilla's slower speed).
  4. See your own legs in first person during moves: yes, moves only.
  5. Station scenes in full, with the crafting table as a real 3×3 surface: yes, in P5, with sneak + right-click for the plain screen. Chests stay quick touches.
  6. Settings: one switch each for moves, touches and stations, plus one per station, all on by default.
  7. Order: P1 first. It fixes the pipe and builds the rig everything else stands on.
