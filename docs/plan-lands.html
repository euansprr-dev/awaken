Awaken · Terrain plan · 24 Oct 2026 · for approval

Awakened Lands

A world you remember: landscapes with real scale, biomes that belong to Awaken's story, and forests that move. Built on Tectonic and our own layer on top, tuned for your M4.

The short version. Keep Tectonic for the shape of the land (MIT: we can tune or patch anything). On top, Awaken adds its own biomes, trees and landmarks, plus living foliage: wind, falling leaves, ground cover and a look for each biome. Terralith is out: its licence bans use in any AI project, and that includes me tweaking or testing it.

The biggest hidden job: today every Awaken system (creatures, lairs, ruins, gates, rifts, spirits, the cottage's wood) finds its place by vanilla biome names. A new biome would be empty of Awaken until those move to one shared biome table. That migration is the first real build step.

What today's test showed: Tectonic on its own changes the map, not the feeling. On the same seed, the land near spawn is still forest, lakes and plain with the same trees. Its scale shows at mountain ranges, far from your door. Identity and life are the missing pieces, and only Awaken can add those.

Performance: the darkest forest we measured under Tectonic already runs at 114 fps (8.7 ms a frame), right on your 110 fps line. So the richest forests have no spare budget: new trees and foliage have to pay for themselves, through leaf culling and fewer, larger leaf shapes.

1 · Understanding and success contract

What you need, and how we'll know

You find the overworld boring and stale: the land is low and repetitive, the trees are the same few shapes, and nothing moves. You want terrain that feels like a new game, made for Awaken rather than borrowed, with trees and leaves that feel cinematic, and still fast. You suggested a great terrain mod, customised, or our own.

The job

Make the Awakened Pack's overworld the most memorable terrain you've played: scale, identity and life, designed around Awaken's creatures, ruins, gates and story.

What's wrong today

Low relief, one forest blob everywhere, identical trees, still leaves, no landmarks. Awaken adds no terrain at all: its content sits on vanilla land.

Your ideas, to test, not assume

A great terrain mod (Tectonic + Terralith), customised to your goals; maybe our own generator; cinematic trees and leaves; keep the frame rate.

Must keep

Every Awaken system works in every biome. Your existing worlds never change. Swift look stays at your frame-rate budget. Mixin-free Awaken. Licences we can actually honour.

2 · Verified audit and diagnosis

What exists, and why it feels boring

Reproduced today: the same seed, vanilla against Tectonic

A disposable diagnostic tour in its own worktree, seed 777, 2880×1800, performance mods on, Lens off. Each tile is where awaken fauna goto landed for one creature family. That command picks the nearest place the family can spawn, not the biome's heart, so several tiles land in similar places. That itself is evidence of how samey the land near spawn is, and it's why the plan includes a proper "visit biome" tool.

Vanilla, 40 blocks up: low relief, one forest blob, the same canopy everywhere.
Tectonic, same seed and commands: a different map (lakes, a bare stone plateau, a snowy plain), but still flat near spawn, the same trees, and the same green carpet.
Tectonic at eye level: the same vanilla forests. Tectonic shapes the land; it never touches trees, ground cover or light.
Where Tectonic shines: a mountain range at sunset (from the pack work earlier today). Real scale, but vanilla biomes and vanilla trees.

4 · Alternatives

Three ways, and the recommendation

A · Tune and dress

Tectonic tuned to your taste, every vanilla biome restyled (colours, fog, ground cover, tree decorations), wind and falling leaves, a look per biome. No new biomes. About 1–1.5 weeks.

Loses on identity: still vanilla's biome list, so it can't feel like Awaken's world. It's the first layer of B, not the whole answer.

B · Awakened Lands RECOMMENDED

A, plus Awaken's own biomes born from the story, a tree library with true giants (as structures), landmark-scale features (rifts, spires, buried colossi), and every Awaken system wired to one biome table. 5–8 weeks in stages; a playable slice in about two.

Keeps Tectonic's tuned shapes; spends our effort where only we can: identity, life and integration.

C · Our own generator

Replace Tectonic with Awaken's own noise router and continents.

Loses: weeks of noise tuning to reach what Tectonic already does, every worldgen bug ours, and slower far-terrain generation if we get it wrong. Its best idea (story-shaped land) lives in B as Lithostitched wraps of Tectonic's density functions.

What would change this: if you see a tuned Tectonic world in stage 0 and say the shapes are already right, B shrinks toward A plus a few hero biomes. If even tuned Tectonic feels wrong in shape, we fork Tectonic's density functions (MIT) rather than start over.

5 · Behaviour and architecture

How Awakened Lands works

Shape

Tectonic, pinned, with an Awakened preset (vertical scale, continents, rivers, ridges) chosen in the terrain studio, at normal height unless the studio shows peaks feel capped. Story-scale land features as Lithostitched density wraps: rift canyons, shattered highlands. No fork unless the studio demands it.

Biomes

Vanilla biomes stay, restyled. Awakened biomes join through Lithostitched's biome injectors (already installed) at climate points Tectonic already produces, with TerraBlender as the fallback. You choose how much of the map they take (decision 2). Ground rules follow the surface, not fixed heights, so they hold on Tectonic's tall peaks.

One biome table

A single source of truth, biome → family, spirit hint, lair and ruin rights, cottage palette, music, Lens look, generated into every tag Awaken reads. Replaces today's scattered exact-id lists, so any biome, ours or vanilla, gets the full Awaken world.

Trees

A tree library: data trees from vanilla's placers plus a few Awaken trunk and canopy shapes, with Awaken logs and leaves (tagged #logs and #leaves so gates and stars still clear them), canopies within about 12 blocks of the trunk. Giant hero trees are structures, placed and checked like our lairs, built from the gate painter's tree work. No ticking trees or growth simulation.

Ground

Leaf litter, moss, bushes, fallen logs, rocks and flowers by biome through decorators and features. Cheap, and the biggest change at eye level.

Life

Wind: direction, strength and gusts from world time and seed, so every player sees the same wind with no network traffic. It drives falling leaves (our particle pool, coloured per tree, capped), petals in meadows, dust in steppes, and leaf rustle in the ambience. Leaves look fuller through texture art and silhouette, never extra leaf layers: every see-through leaf pixel is costly on Apple GPUs.

Look and sound

Biome colours, fog and particles; a Lens look per biome that eases as you walk; music and ambience beds per biome.

Distance

Far terrain shows ground blocks, logs and leaves only, so every Awakened biome gets a ground block and canopy shape you can recognise from a mountain top. Hero trees and landmarks are big enough to read there. Every feature has a cost ceiling and is safe to run off the main thread, because Distant Horizons runs it thousands of times.

Contracts and invariants

World creation: a new world gets the pinned Tectonic preset and Awakened Lands version, recorded in the world. Existing worlds are never changed: a world without the record keeps its terrain, and a newer version only affects chunks that haven't been generated yet, with a warning.

Every biome has: a family, spirit hint, lair and ruin rights, cottage palette, ambience, music and Lens look, all from the one table. The census test fails if any biome lacks any of them.

Cost ceilings: any feature ≤ 0.5 ms per chunk on average, hero-tree structures ≤ 2 ms each, measured in a pack-on worldgen bench. Chunk generation and a 64-chunk Distant Horizons build stay within 20% of plain Tectonic.

Client life: leaf particles from a fixed pool (no allocation per frame), capped at about 150 at once and scaled down by the frame budget; wind needs no network traffic; everything degrades to plain vanilla visuals if switched off.

6 · Concrete demonstrations

The same moments, today and proposed

"Proposed" describes behaviour that doesn't exist yet.

Today: leaving spawn

  1. You walk out of a low oak and birch forest.
  2. The next ten minutes are the same forest, a river, a lump of a hill.
  3. You follow a creature or a gate hint because the land gives you nothing.

Proposed

  1. Spawn sits on rolling hills; the Shattered Highlands rise behind, their fault lines glowing at dusk.
  2. An Elderwood canopy towers to the east; leaves stream past on the wind.
  3. You walk toward the glow. The world chose your direction.

Today: your complaint, flying over the land

  1. One green carpet in every direction.
  2. Trees all the same height; nothing breaks the skyline.

Proposed

  1. Distinct palettes by region: the white glass of a steppe, the blue glow of the Lumen Forest, grey moor fog.
  2. Giant trees and spires break the skyline and still read on the far horizon.

Hard case: the densest forest at 4K

  1. Giant trees, ground cover, 150 leaf particles, far terrain.
  2. Risk: leaves are expensive on Apple GPUs.

Proposed

  1. Today the darkest Tectonic forest already sits at 8.7 ms, so this case decides the design.
  2. New trees use fewer, larger leaf shapes and thicker art instead of more leaf blocks; MoreCulling hides leaf faces you can't see.
  3. The budget scaler trims particles and ground decoration first.
  4. Measured in the forest scene before anything merges.

Failure: a worldgen mod breaks on an update

  1. The pack-on worldgen test fails before you ever see it.

Proposed

  1. The pack pins the last good version.
  2. Worlds already made keep their biomes: worldgen is recorded per world.

7 · Evaluation and delivery

How we prove it, in stages

The terrain studio

Five fixed seeds; a spawn vista, an 8-point transect every 250 blocks, each biome's heart, and a forest interior at noon and golden hour. Contact sheets go to you as blind pairs: vanilla, Tectonic, Awakened Lands. Needs two small dev commands: terrain visit <biome> and terrain survey.

What we measure

Distinct biomes per km; relief per 512 blocks; your blind picks (subjective, judged by you, never by me); the census of Awaken content per biome; forest p50 and p99; chunk generation ms; Distant Horizons radius-64 build time. All existing GameTests keep passing.
