Power Levels

Every level-up should change the power itself: how it looks, what it does, and at levels 3 and 5 how your body casts it and how it sounds. Level 5 is the power's Awakened form. Here is the audit, the design, a ladder for all 63 powers, and 12 decisions for you.

"Let's say the beam ... levels up from the simple beam to around the beam there's an extra like spinny spiral ... It has to genuinely look insane." (you, 28 Oct)

63powers, each with its own ladder
4named steps per power (levels 2 to 5)
~3 hto the proof you look at in game
10–14 hof builder time for all of it

What I need from you: a yes, or changes, on the 12 decisions at the bottom. Each has my recommendation. The ladders are drafts: change any name or step you like.

What a level does today

  • Only numbers change. Each level adds 12% damage and takes 4% off the cooldown. Nothing a power does changes at any level.
  • 23 powers look exactly the same at level 5 as at level 1, the Beam among them. The other 40 draw some parts 10% bigger. No power's animation, sound or icon changes.
  • The level-up message says "+12% damage, -4% cooldown" for every power, including Wall, which does no damage.
  • Using a Power Core plays vanilla sparkles (Minecraft's dust and end-rod particles).
  • Wall Stride, Water Run and Danger Sense can never level through use. Mastery only counts hits and fights, and they do neither.

The good news is that the level already reaches every player nearby on each effect, so others can see your level's look with no new network traffic. Nothing draws from it yet.

The Beam, level by level

Your example, worked through. Each step adds a layer and a change in play, and the look tells you what changed. These sketches show the idea; the real thing is drawn by the game's own effects engine (ribbons, rings and glow, never vanilla particles).

Level 1 · today

Beam

A three-layer beam: wide faint glow, orange body, white core. Rings ride down it.

Level 2

Helix

A bright ribbon spirals round the beam and spins; embers peel off it.

Held on one foe, it builds up to 20% more damage over 1.5 s, and the spiral tightens as it builds.

Level 3

Lance

A second ribbon turns the other way. A flare bursts at every body it passes through.

Pierces the first foe and hits a second for 60%. New: the off hand grips the wrist; a deep hum under the beam.

Level 4

Corona

Where it lands, a molten pool spreads and a ring of light turns round the end.

The pool burns foes standing in it for 2 s.

Level 5 · Awakened

Sunspear

Born from a ring of runes at your palm. Three ribbons, a white-gold core.

Letting go sends a last pulse down the beam that bursts for 20% of what it dealt. A new stance, and its own sound.

The sketches turn slowly. They're drawn on the page, not taken from the game.

Every step keeps the ones before it and works with every variant. No step changes what a key does or where a move goes (the Dash bug from 27 Oct can't come back: its test runs at every level). Movement powers never get air jumps, gliding or flight from a step; the Ascent keeps those.

Strength, frame rate and other players

Strength stays on the curve

Today a level-5 power does about 1.5 times its level-1 damage. The flat bonus becomes +6% a level, and the steps carry the rest, so level 5 lands about as strong as today. The gates stay balanced, and what you gain is a new shape, not just a bigger number (decision 3).

Frame rate

Nobody has measured what power effects cost per frame yet, so that comes first: a timer and a bench that replays a crowded gate fight. A higher level must be better composed, not more particles: mostly ribbons and rings, which are cheap. The gate: in that bench on your M4, level 5 costs at most 0.5 ms more per frame than level 1.

Other players

Your level already rides on every effect sent to players within 72 blocks. Their game draws your level's look, gesture and sound, with no new network traffic. The server decides what every step does. Steps that help others (a heal, a dome) are written for allies.

Every power can grow

Wall Stride levels from wall-running, Water Run from running on water, Danger Sense from warned blows that miss you. Walls level from the arrows they stop, sight powers from hits on what they show, and movement from dodging. All of it is capped per minute, so you can't farm it.

Decisions

  1. 1What does a level-up give?A named step each level: a new look and a change in play, a new cast and sound at 3 and 5, the Awakened form at 5"Not just a little bit more damage": each level changes what the power is, and you can see it.
  2. 2Max level?Keep 5Four real steps per power beats more, thinner ones. If you max a power and miss levelling, more levels can come later.
  3. 3How strong is level 5?About as strong as today: +6% a level, the steps carry the restThe gates stay balanced. The alternative is keeping +12% and adding steps on top: about 1.8 times at level 5.
  4. 4What does the K menu show?The whole ladder: every step's name and line, the next in fullYou see what you're working toward. They're your own powers, not undiscovered secrets.
  5. 5When does the levelling moment play?In full when it's calm; a sting and a flaring key in a fightIt never interrupts a fight, and it always says what changed.
  6. 6Proof first?Yes: the Beam's whole ladder, the moment and the K menu, then your look in gameTaste is the risk. One power done in full settles the bar before 62 more.
  7. 7Can every power level through use?Yes, with per-minute caps"You can always level up your powers": nothing stuck at level 1.
  8. 8Order after the proof?The starters, then family by familyThe powers everyone has come first.
  9. 9Names on steps?Yes (Beam, Helix, Lance, Corona, Sunspear)A name makes a step something you earned. Plain words under each.
  10. 10Do others see your level?Yes, alwaysA level-5 friend should look it. Their own effects setting still thins it, as today.
  11. 11One builder or two after the proof?OneYour rule from today. Two would save about 4–5 hours of waiting and use more.
  12. 12Power Core hover (Codex is adding it)?It shows the next step's name and line from the ladderOne source for level text. I'll ask the orchestrator to tell Codex.
