# AWAKEN audit: the early journey and every guidance channel (as built, 2026-10-26)

Repo read: `/Volumes/MEDIA/Awaken/.claude/worktrees/progression-8ed284` (HEAD 84bea58c, the build Euan plays, plus
5bbe162e "Tether Vow: the vault's line answers C-rank…", which landed during this audit; Euan's play copy gave him the
rope at the first vault he walked into, which is what that commit changes). Paths below
are relative to `src/main/java/dev/awaken/` unless they start with `src/`, `docs/` or `data/` (= `src/main/resources/data/awaken/`).
Pending work read (read only): `registry-210f66` (branch carillon/m4-registry, the Registry ceremony) and
`discovery-ux-40ba94` (uncommitted notification director).

Headline: after "THE CITY IS YOURS." the game gives the player **no destination, no thread and no reason to pick a
direction**. The pieces that were designed to do that (the onboarding plan's pan to the High Gate and its Now line, the
tip system, The Last Delivery as the first errand, the first-hour gate rule) are either unbuilt, unwired, or only
reachable by accident. What the forest does offer (a rift, a happening, toasts) is spectacle, not a goal.

## (d) discovery-ux's model in 10 lines (uncommitted, `discovery-ux-40ba94`, SPEC §26 there)

1. One client-side director, `signal.client.Herald`, paces every non-decision System message and other modules' HUD lines (`Notices.client().claim(tier, topic)` / `file(...)`).
2. Tiers (`core.api.Notices.Tier`): CRITICAL (decisions, WARNING, GATE panels, level pane, danger caption) bypasses; IMPORTANT (default) is paced; AMBIENT never interrupts at Standard.
3. `SignalWindow` gains `tier`, `topic`, `moment` (`Builder.tier/topic/moment`), default by kind (`defaultTier`); `Signal.toast(p, text, tier, topic)`; `Toast` payload carries tier + topic.
4. Pure pacing in `signal.ui.Pace`: densities MINIMAL / STANDARD (default) / GUIDED (client option `notifications`); STANDARD = 8 s gap (14 s calm), 3 per 90 s, ordinary waits 45 s then filed, moments wait 3 min; IMPORTANT shown, AMBIENT filed.
5. Same-topic waiting messages merge ("Mossback · +2"); moments go first, then tier, then oldest.
6. Held (nothing paced): any screen, a fight, UI focus, a card up, a moment up, a scene hold, not awakened, and the world's first 5 min (`Herald.SETTLE`); a message within 3 s of the player's own click shows at once; in gate runs untagged messages go CRITICAL.
7. Moments (`signal.client.Moment`): centre banner for a first of its kind / wonder / first rift, ~5 s, own sound.
8. Filing: what isn't shown goes to a session **Journal** (`signal.client.Journal`, K Status tab, order −10, 60 kept, "JOURNAL · N NEW") with a **find cue** (diamond by the crosshair + chime, `FindCue`).
9. Discovery needs intent (`signal.Finds`): study a creature ~0.5 s under the crosshair or hit it; first-ever find = a teaching moment; first of each kind = IMPORTANT toast "kept in your Journal (K)"; later ones AMBIENT.
10. Rift rework: word 2.5 s after the tear, own tear sound to 128 blocks, far rumble bed, `RiftGlow` with a 56-block sky column; it also records that TipHooks has no implementation.

Build on it, don't duplicate: guidance lines (a quest step, the first thread) should be **Now/Horizon HUD state**, not
toasts, so they never compete with the pace; any new toast ("Journal · …", "A gate formed north") should carry a tier
and topic; a tip system should `claim(IMPORTANT, "tip")`. Note the name clash: discovery-ux's "Journal" (filed notices)
vs the Living Wilds JOURNAL (chains and bounties) — both on the Status tab.
