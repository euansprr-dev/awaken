World Sound Listening

AWAKEN's own synthesised places and moments, rendered from the game's files and data. Nature is AmbientSounds and isn't here. Mark what holds the bar and what doesn't; nothing is required.

Clips are levelled for listening; in game they sit lower, under the music. In the place mixes the one-off sounds come about three times as often as in game so 30 seconds holds several.
