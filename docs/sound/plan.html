World Sound

Every biome, every place, every hour: a world you hear from the inside. Birds in the trees you can see, the river where the river is, rain that sounds like what it falls on, and a world that goes quiet when something dangerous, or something magical, is near.

“The number one goal here is to make it actually immersive, not just like, oh, mobs here, this there, but actually like truly immersive in terms of how it feels to be inside of the game.” Euan

Revised by Euan · 2026-10-25

AmbientSounds plays the nature; AWAKEN builds only what's ours. AmbientSounds is in the sound profile and plays every biome. Our layer plays the hushes, hums, magic and places, and turns AmbientSounds down while one of our moments sounds. The first is built: the world holds its breath near a gate. No field recordings of our own. Decisions 3 and 5 below are replaced by this.

The short answer

Partly done. The world itself isn't.

Already there

Room acoustics (Sound Physics Remastered), adaptive gate music, the System's voice, 47 creature voices, leaf rustle and high wind, beds for 4 of the 6 Awakened biomes, a few magic beds, and the city soundscape being built now.

Missing

56 vanilla biomes still sound like vanilla, which has no songbirds, insects (bar bees), wind, surf, river or rain-by-surface. Elderwood and Lumen Forest have no bed. Nothing reacts to you or to the magic.

Use existing mods?

Yes, for the jobs mods do best: Sound Physics (space, kept) and Tread Lightly (footsteps, after a one-hour spike). AmbientSounds is the bar we measure against; it plays sound around your head, not from places, so it isn't shipped.

Build the rest

Nothing on the market knows AWAKEN's biomes, dimensions, gates or happenings. A world layer of our own in core/audio, the Conductor, plays the places, the reactions and the magic.

The bar

What “immersive” means, as things you can hear

Each one is checked in a listening batch or a test, so "done" isn't a matter of opinion.

  1. 01
    No audible loops.Ten minutes in one spot and you never hear the same moment twice: beds are three layers of co-prime length (37, 41, 53 s repeat together after 22 hours); every salient sound is a one-shot from six or more variants.
  2. 02
    Sounds come from places.Birds sing from the trees you can see, the river from the river, surf from the shore, wind from the open side of the ridge. Only a place's diffuse air is non-positional.
  3. 03
    Transitions you don't notice.Biomes blend from a ring of 16 samples over 8 to 15 seconds; dawn, dusk and weather are curves, never switches.
  4. 04
    Silence on purpose.Lulls where the forest goes still for half a minute; deep caves almost silent; the Mirror Marsh deliberately still; nothing at all in the Abyssal Shrine.
  5. 05
    The world reacts.Birds fall quiet near a predator; crickets stop around your feet; birds flush when you sprint through brush; rain on leaves, stone, water and a roof all differ; drips go on after the rain; thunder comes later the farther the strike; wind rises with height; footsteps follow surface and armour.
  6. 06
    The world is AWAKEN's.Wildlife goes quiet near an open gate; a Break spreads a hush; the Awakened lands hum in the System's key; a perceptive hunter hears a lair breathing first.
  7. 07
    Never fights the music or the city.Ambience never touches the music channel, thins under combat music, and yields inside Carillon's walls.

  • Sound Physics' rules shape the design. It skips sounds played around your head, rain, thunder and any ambient.* event, and judges a looping sound once, when it starts. So our air bed is filtered by us, our positional sounds keep other names, and textures re-anchor every ~12 s so walking indoors muffles them.
  • The engine holds about 248 ordinary sounds and 8 streams. AmbientSounds alone would want several streams; our whole Conductor wants one.
  • If a player adds AmbientSounds anyway, our jar carries a few inert files that mute it in our gates and other instanced places, and the Conductor drops its own nature sounds while it runs.

Approaches

Adopt, build, or both

A · adopt

AmbientSounds plus our data

  • Fastest: about 10 builder-hours of data and config.
  • Realistic nature, but sound around your head rather than from places.
  • Blind to gates, strata, the Veil, happenings and powers.
  • Streams every sound, competing with our music; no footsteps.
B · our own

The Conductor, nothing else

  • Full control, highest ceiling.
  • Rebuilds footsteps that a mod already does well.
  • About 34 builder-hours.
C · hybrid · recommended

Conductor plus the best mods

  • The Conductor for biomes, places, reactions and magic.
  • Sound Physics for space, Tread Lightly for footsteps.
  • One engine decides what plays; mods do physics, not taste.
  • About 28 builder-hours.

What would change my mind. Two of three facts about AmbientSounds are already in: it does take data from our jar, and it doesn't play from places. The third is your ears. If it beats our first forest in batch 1's blind A/B, the gap is content: we raise ours with better recordings, or adopt it for vanilla nature beds only while the Conductor keeps the places, reactions and magic. If Tread Lightly fails its spike, our own surface-and-armour steps come in Phase 3 (+3 h).

What makes it ours

Six touches no nature pack has

The world holds its breath

Near an open gate the birds and insects thin out and the wind leans toward it. As a Break's clock runs down the hush spreads, until only the hum is left.

The gust you see is the gust you hear

Our wind is deterministic and shared: a gust front rolls downwind at 7 blocks a second. You hear it in the upwind trees first, then over you, in step with the waving leaves. Two players hear the same gust.

The lands sing in the System's key

A faint harmony in D major pentatonic: Glass Steppe's glass ringing in the sun, the faults beating, Frost Spires' aurora at night, Lumen specks chiming as you pass, Elderwood's colossi groaning in big gusts.

Mirror Marsh answers

A sound you make there returns a moment later from across the water, as if from your reflection.

The hunter's ear

Awareness is power: PER widens what you hear. A lair breathing from its direction, a hidden cache's shimmer, a spirit's whisper in the Veil.

Clarity is lucidity

In a dream the world's sound sharpens as you become lucid. You hear yourself wake up inside it.

Your call

Seven decisions

  1. Approach C, the hybrid. Our Conductor for biomes, places, reactions and magic; mods for space and footsteps.Recommended.A is faster but generic and blind to AWAKEN; B rebuilds footsteps for nothing.
  2. Footsteps: Tread Lightly in the sound profile, if the spike passes.Recommended.Otherwise our own steps by surface and armour in Phase 3.
  3. AmbientSounds as the benchmark, not shipped. We A/B against it in batch 1 and adopt it for vanilla nature beds only if it wins on your ears. Our jar mutes it in our dimensions for players who add it.Recommended.
  4. No Raise Sound Limit Simplified. Its asynchronous playback breaks assumptions our music player relies on, and our budget fits the vanilla engine.Recommended: leave it out.
  5. CC0 recordings from Freesound for birds, insects, water and rain on surfaces: about 70 files from a shortlist you tick.Recommended.Downloading originals needs a free Freesound account's API key: yours, or a project account.
  6. Five listening batches of ten minutes or less, unmarked = keep once you've opened the page.Recommended.
  7. The six magic touches: the gate hush, the gust you hear, the lands in the System's key, Mirror Marsh's answer, the hunter's ear, clarity in dreams.Recommended: all six.

Not recommended, for the record: Sounds (can't load on 26.3, all rights reserved, and it re-voices the UI, which is the System's), Dynamic Surroundings (nothing past 1.21.1), Presence Footsteps (Fabric only, Polyform Shield licence).
