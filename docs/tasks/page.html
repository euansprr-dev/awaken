One Tasks page

The band gets one list of everything you can do right now, the Journal keeps only what you found, and the Hearth page holds your home and your beasts. The Truths page and every other list that asks you to choose goes, and what each of them gave you lands somewhere sensible.

What you're after

The job: at any moment, open the band and see what you can do next, and trust that list. The pain: the game kept telling you to do things you'd done (the Lampwing to find, the fire to set), and the things it offers are scattered over eleven sections on two pages, half of them asking you to pick from a menu. What it should feel like: one short list, nothing on it you've done, nothing on it you can't start, and the one you care about on your screen.

A strong result: after setting your fire, nothing anywhere mentions setting it; after taming the Lampwing, nothing mentions finding it; and the Tasks page reads like a friend's short list: six to a dozen lines, each saying what to do and where. A weak result: the same lists, regrouped. Unacceptable: a done thing anywhere, the same goal twice, a long wall you scroll through, or losing a reward the game used to give.

Keep: the top-right card and the top-left place (one each, as you asked on 26 and 27 October), Status first when you press K, the Powers, Codex and Map pages as they are, and "found, not told": no daily lists, nothing naming a boss before you meet it. Not in this plan: new quests or content, the look of the Powers, Codex and Map pages, the toasts' design.

Part 1 is built: no done goal shows anywhere

Done first, as you asked, and on CI now. Two root causes, both fixed for the whole game, not just these two cases:

  • The Lampwing on the top left was the Hearth page's Track: it set a destination that only cleared when you walked within 16 blocks of it. Every destination the game sets now carries the goal it's for and drops the moment that goal is met, however it was met (the beast tamed at your fire, a bell found, a raid's chest emptied, a metal no longer needed).
  • Set Your Fire still offered was the trail's "find level ground" step, which waited for a manual tick that never came once you'd set the fire from Ilse's coal. A trail step now counts as done when any later step is done, and a trail you've already finished never opens.
  • Also: Saved for later drops a promise when you walk into the place it was for; Bell Book leads drop a bell you hold; a tip you did before it showed never shows.
  • One check (trail_done_never_shows, kin_done_never_shows) sweeps every place a goal can show: the card, the corner, the Now lines, decisions waiting, Saved for later, trails, the Hearth page and the Bell Book.

Three ways to do it

A · Tidy the pages

Move sections around

Rename Journal sections, put the to-do ones on a new page, leave each list as it is.

Loses: the same goal still shows two or three times (the first thread, the trail and the Hearth line all say "place your Ember"), and every list still asks you to choose.

B · Recommended

One list fed by every source

Each system says what it would put in front of you, with a key for the goal it's about. The server merges rows with the same key, drops anything done, and sends one list. One row is pinned to your screen.

Why: it fixes the doubles at the root, reuses part 1's sweep, and leaves each system's rules where they are.

C · A quest log

Rewrite as quests

Turn every system into quest data with steps and rewards, one engine for all.

Loses: weeks of rework, the live sources (a star fell, a beast nearby) don't fit quests, and it would bring back the "list of chores" feel you turned down on 29 October.

How it behaves

What counts

A row is something you can start now. Anything that needs a power, rank, item or place you don't have yet isn't a task: it's a find, on the Journal, until it opens.

One row per goal

Every row carries the key of the goal it's about (hearth/ember, kin/lampwing, bell/12, trial/D, make/wildhide_vest). Rows with the same key merge: the most specific words, the place and the count are kept. "Place your Ember" from the first thread, the trail and the Hearth line is one row.

Never done

The server builds the list from live state each time and drops any row whose goal is met before it's sent, using part 1's done checks. The sweep test covers every source.

One pinned

The pinned row is the card and the corner's place. With nothing pinned, the game's lead shows, as today. A pinned row that's done unpins itself and the card goes back to the lead. Track on the Hearth page, the Bell Book and the Armoury is the same pin.

Never a wall

Groups in a fixed order: On your screen, Decide, The way on, Home and beasts, Out there, Make. Each shows its three nearest or most urgent and folds the rest. Rows never reorder while the page is open. Nothing expires daily.

Hide

Optional rows (a trail's step, a suggestion) can be hidden; "Hidden (2)" at the foot brings them back. The way on, decisions and the pinned row can't be hidden, so you can't hide your way into being stuck.

Nothing to do

"Nothing waiting right now. Explore, build or rest: new things show here as you find them." If one source fails, it's skipped and logged, and the page shows the rest.

Sent when it changes

Built at most once a second while the page is open and every 5 seconds otherwise, sent only when it changed. Per player, so it's multiplayer-safe.

Old saves

A followed trail becomes the pin. Declared Truths already met come true on first login, in one line rather than a toast each. Directives, the Regimen and request offers are dropped quietly; anything handed in for an open request comes back. A new small save (awaken:tasks, versioned) holds the pin, hidden rows and what you've seen.

Tests and building

One GameTest per mechanism on CI: tasks_one_row_per_goal (the Ember from three sources is one row), tasks_done_never_shows (part 1's sweep, over every Tasks source), tasks_pin_drives_card (pin a bell, find it, the card goes back to the lead), kin_want_on_tasks (watch three lines, the row says 0/3, three helps and it's gone), truths_by_themselves (with an old save), and the band layout check with seven labels. The Directive, Regimen and request tests go with their systems. One CI tour shoots Tasks, Journal, Hearth and Status.

Phase 1

The Tasks list

The list, keys and merging, the pin, the page with Decide and the Path choice, the card and Track buttons on the pin. The old Journal sections stay until phase 2, so nothing goes missing in between.

Phase 2

Remove and rehome

The Truths page to Status, Truths by themselves, Directives, Regimen and requests out, the Relay's new job, Trails folded in, the Journal to finds only, the Hearth page's Home, old saves.

Phase 3

Check

The CI tour shots, the pacing sim rerun, SPEC §27.13 and §30, and the full suite on CI. Each phase hands back green and merges on its own, so any one can be reverted.

About a day of building in this session, phase 1 the largest. What could change that: a source whose goal has no clean key (then it matches on words, with a test), or the pacing sim showing a bigger gap than expected.
