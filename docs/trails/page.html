Trails

A way to be guided without being pushed. Each part of the game gets a short list of things to try. They open when you reach them, wait in the K menu, and only show on your screen if you choose to follow one. When you have no idea what to do, you open one place and it tells you.

"Whenever you don't know what to do at all, you can always go in there and find, okay, these are the things that are available for me to do right now. ... If you want to be guided, you can, but it's not like a forced thing. ... Really plan this for everything."Euan, 27 Oct 2026

The idea in three lines

It opens

At a moment you reach

The moment you set your fire down, "Your Hearth" opens. One short toast and a dot on the Journal. Nothing pops up again, and it never starts by itself.

You choose

Follow it, or don't

Press Follow and its next steps appear on the checklist card top right, with the current one on the Now line. Press again to drop it. Nothing else on your screen changes.

Always an answer

"What can I do now?"

The top of the Journal lists the three best next steps across everything you have open. If nothing is open, it says what opens the next one.

How a trail is written

A trail is a small JSON file, like a quest or a bounty. The owner of each area writes their own in plain speech. A step is a condition on the world, so the game ticks it by reading state, not by asking you to report.

// data/awaken/awaken/trail/your_hearth.json
{
  "title": "Your Hearth",   "area": "home",
  "blurb": "A fire, a roof, a bed, and the town that comes with them.",
  "opens": ["hearth"],   "order": "free",
  "steps": [
    { "text": "Use your fire to open Your Hearth.",           "done": ["counter:awaken:hearth_guide_seen"] },
    { "text": "Build a closed, roofed room next to it.",       "done": ["room:shelter"],
      "hint": "Your Hearth says what a room is missing." },
    { "text": "Make a Sanctuary: a bed and a Meditation Mat.",  "done": ["room:sanctuary"], "where": "hearth" }
  ],
  "finish": { "line": "Your Hearth is a home now.", "levels": 0.05 }
}
Already ticked

Reads what you did

A step you finished before the trail opened is ticked the moment it opens. A step you finish without following still counts. Once ticked, it stays ticked.

Each module owns its words

Tokens, not code

room:sanctuary belongs to the Hearth, style:spear to Arms. A module adds a family of tokens in one line. Most steps use counters the game already keeps.

An escape hatch

Mark it done

A step the world cannot read can be marked by hand. A test checks that every token in every trail resolves, so a typo fails the build, not a player.

Not overwhelming, as numbers

2new trails announced a play-day, at most
3rows under "What you can do now"
5steps on the card at once
1followed trail, and one thing on the card
0trails shown for places you have not met

A trail whose lock you have not seen is invisible. One whose lock you have seen ("opens at D-rank") shows as a faded silhouette and files a "What waits" entry, the same rule The Way Forward set. Finished trails fold away and never reopen.

How it plugs into what exists

Nothing new on the HUD. Trails feeds the channels that are already there.

Existing systemWhat changes
Task card (top right)The followed trail is a source for it. Follow, the tracked chain and the Armoury's Track share one slot; the last one you chose shows.
Now line (top left)The current step, at priority 55: under danger, rifts and a tracked chain, over bookkeeping. With a place, it carries the compass.
K, Journal pageA TRAILS section on top and a browse view inside the page. The band keeps its six pages.
What waitsA silhouette trail files a promise, called back when it opens.
TipsA step can offer a tip once, when it becomes current.
The Hearth's own Now linesKeep the first-ten-minutes nudge. The room-by-room lines hand over to the trail when you follow it, so there is one voice.
Clerk, Hale, Oren, Mira"Anything for me?" says the first step of your best trail in their own voice.
Directives, quest chainsUntouched. Directives stay random and daily; chains stay story. A trail step can point at a chain.

What it costs

At AI pace, scaled from the git history of the project (all of Awaken took about 30 hours). One new small module, trails.

  • T0EngineFormat, loader, saved state, tokens, evaluation, network, task card and Now line, the validity test.4 to 5 h
  • T1The K menuJournal section, browse view, unread dot, opening toast, one look tour on the CI runners.3 to 4 h
  • T2The Hearth pilotThe seven trails, the Hearth's tokens, the Forge steps, the hand-over of the Now lines.3 to 4 h
  • T3Waves by areaWeapons and Body first, then Gates and Powers, World, Deep and Story. One builder each, in parallel.8 to 10 h
  • T4Polish"Anything for me?", silhouette promises, tips, the Armoury's Track as a step type.1 to 2 h

About 20 to 25 builder hours, a third of that in wall-clock time with the waves in parallel. T0 and T1 carry the risk (the K menu is where everything meets); T2 proves the format on the biggest area before the waves copy it.

Decisions for you

My recommendation is first in each. Say "go with all recommendations" or change any.

1

Name recommended: Trails

It reads as something you walk, which is the idea.

Other names: Guides (clashes with the Your Hearth screen, which already calls itself a guide), Pursuits, "Things to try".

2

Where it lives recommended: top of the Journal page

A section on top plus a browse view inside the page. The band keeps its six pages.

Other: a seventh page. Cleaner to find, but it breaks the six-page rule the K menu was built on.

3

Announcing a new trail recommended: one toast and a dot

The toast comes through the notification director, so it waits for a calm moment.

Other: the dot only, fully silent.

4

How many you can follow recommended: one

There is one task card. Following a second one replaces the first, and the first is one press away.

Other: up to three, with the card cycling.

5

Rewards recommended: 5% of a level, once, on finishing

Small, real, and nothing is locked behind a trail.

Other: no reward at all.

6

Trails you have not met recommended: hidden, then a silhouette

You only see a trail once you have met its lock, so the list never spoils.

Other: show every trail from the start.

7

Progress recommended: read from the world

Latched, with a hand tick for the rare step the world cannot read.

8

The Hearth's existing Now lines recommended: keep the first nudge, hand the rest over

So the trail and the old lines never say two different things.

9

Order of work recommended: engine, K menu, Hearth, then waves

Weapons and Body are the first waves.

10

The Forge steps recommended: inside The Forge trail

The four prompts I proposed earlier become steps of that trail instead of separate toasts.

If you approve

Say "go with all recommendations" and I will start T0 and T1, then the Hearth. The Journal page changes in T1, so you see it in a tour before anything else is built on it.

What I could not check

  • That the Hearth's saved record holds everything the new Hearth tokens need. The docs say it does; the builder reads it first.
  • Whether the task card can carry a per-player "what is on the card" choice without touching the living module's tracked-chain code.
  • How the Journal page looks with a section added. The mock-ups above are drawn from its structure, not from a screenshot; one tour will show it.
  • The 20 to 25 hour figure is a scaled guess from the project's own history, not a measurement.
