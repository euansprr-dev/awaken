# The combat sim on decision 14's damage (2026-11-02)

Decision 14 (SPEC §46.5, approved 2026-11-02) adds +1 damage to every forged weapon, +2 to the eight slower styles. The
sim fights with the rank's forged Longsword, so its weapon goes E 5.5 → 6.5, D 6.5 → 7.5, C 7.5 → 8.5, B 8.5 → 9.5,
A 9.5 → 10.5 (+18%, +15%, +13%, +12%, +11%). `tools/sim/forge_bump_sim.py` runs `tools/sim/combat_sim.py` with the bump
(`BUMP=1`) or without; 200 seeds a cell, seed 20261026. Targets as in docs/combat/PLAN.md §6.1.

## Under the recommended curve (`--preset recommended-hp`)

| Lords passing | as tuned | with the bump | with the bump and Lord health x the bump |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tank, win ≤ 10% | 8/15 | 6/15 | 7/15 |
| Dodger, win ≥ 60%, TTK 80-120 s | 11/15 | **3/15** | 11/15 |
| Parrier, win ≥ 80%, TTK 80-120 s | 12/15 | **5/15** | 12/15 |
| Expert, win ≥ 98%, TTK ≤ 75 s, half health left | 5/15 | 12/15 | 5/15 |
| Diamond sword at D, win ≥ 70% | 4/4 | 4/4 | 4/4 |

The bump alone takes every Lord fight about a tenth shorter (the Dodger's and Parrier's medians fall by about 10 s,
most of them under the 80 s floor the curve was tuned to). Raising each Lord's health by its rank's bump (E x1.18, D x1.15,
C x1.13, B x1.12, A x1.11, S x1.10) gives the same fights back: every cell within a few points and seconds of the tuned
run; the widest gap is the Bell Warden's Tank winning 15% against 8%. The compensated `lords.<id>.hp` overrides are
the preset's times those factors.

## As built (no preset: GateBestiary's health, no curve)

The game as built already misses the TTK floors (fights of 40-75 s; Dodger 0/15, Parrier 1/15, Expert 8/15). The bump
takes those fights a tenth shorter again (Parrier 0/15, Expert 7/15) and wins a little more often.

## Normal creatures (hits to kill on level, target 3-6)

| Rank | before | with the bump |
|---|---|---|
| E | median 4, 88% in 3-6 | median 3, 76% in 3-6 |
| D | median 4, 92% | median 3, 77% |
| C | median 4, 92% | median 4, 92% |
| B | median 4, 100% | median 3, 100% |
| A | median 4, 100% | median 4, 100% |

At E and D a few small creatures (drone bees, lantern moths) go from three hits to two.

## Recommendation

Land the bump together with creature health raised by the same factors, so every fight stays as it was tuned:
Lords x the bump by their rank (in whichever curve is live: `GateBestiary`'s health today, the recommended curve when
it lands), and normal creatures at E and D x1.15 (C and up stay in range). The health is the mobs module's; the factors
are here. Without it, decision 14 makes the whole game about a tenth easier in fights, most at E and D.
