# AWAKEN cave audit: overworld caves, life underground, the Descent, movement in rock

Read-only audit of `/Volumes/MEDIA/Awaken/.claude/worktrees/world-depth-e0a13d` (branch `claude/world-depth-e0a13d`,
HEAD c7846bc2, working tree clean apart from `docs/world-depth/`). Paths are relative to `src/main/java/dev/awaken/`
unless they start with `src/`, `docs/`, `tools/`, `pack/` or `/Volumes`. Vanilla means the 26.3 sources in
`/Volumes/MEDIA/Awaken/.ref`. The code is the truth; where SPEC and code differ, the code is quoted.
Written for the "caves worth entering" design: what is under the overworld today, what the movement system can do in
rock, and how the Descent's strata relate.

**The facts that shape a cave design**

1. AWAKEN does not touch cave generation. No carver, noise setting, aquifer or cave density function of its own; the
   Awakened biomes copy vanilla's carvers, ores, geodes and monster rooms. The only overworld density change is the
   city basin's 20-block crust (section a).
2. Lush caves, dripstone caves, the deep dark and 26.3's new sulfur caves all still generate, under Awakened biomes
   too. With the pack's `world` profile, Tectonic adds its underground rivers and lava tunnels (its defaults).
3. AWAKEN's only cave place is the Crystal Nest: one sealed geode-like dome (radius 9) per 96-chunk cell, 20-50
   below sea level under dripstone or lush caves, not joined to natural caves. Everything "living" (anomalies,
   happenings, camps, Wayfarers, herds) is placed on the surface heightmap.
4. The Hollows family (5 creatures) lives in the three older cave biomes, under a roof only. Sulfur caves have no
   AWAKEN creature, lair, sound or look.
5. The Tether bites any block with collision (44 blocks), zips up to ceilings, and in any rope state the player takes
   **no fall damage**: a vertical shaft is safe for a Tether holder. There is no rappel (the rope only reels in).
6. Wall-runs need a flat, axis-aligned face at least 2 blocks tall that stays on the same plane (within 0.1) for the
   whole run, with the feet 1.5+ above the ground. Natural cave walls rarely qualify: wall-running in caves is a
   terrain job. Ledge hangs, drops, mantles and slides work on any natural block.
7. Tight spaces: the slide drops to crawl height (0.6) and ends in vanilla's crawl under a 1-high gap; a mantle fits
   into a 2-high passage; zips work along tunnels. Nothing else is built for tunnels.
8. The Descent is ten separate dimensions under the Well, generated by one custom generator from scratch: a vertical
   abyss with a spiral ramp, a camp and a pit. It reuses nothing from overworld caves. Six of its forms carve noodle
   tunnels into the rock round the shaft, but nothing is placed in them.
9. Living Wilds Phases F and G (wonders, secrets, legends, treasure maps, settlers, village names, roads) are not
   built: no `living/wonder` or `living/village` package. The planned cave content ("The Opening" sinkhole, hollow
   dens at cave mouths, region secrets like "a cave behind a waterfall") exists only in `docs/living-wilds/PLAN.md`.
10. The richest existing underground hook is the Veil: a spirit passes through rock and sees ores, spawners and buried
    loot chests; a buried chest can open a Veil Gate rift. Any cave site with a loot chest plugs into it for free.

### Cave biomes

AWAKEN has no underground biome of its own. The Awakened biomes are injected with `lithostitched:replace_partially`
over **surface** biome targets only, where the `awaken:lands/awakening` noise is over a threshold (−0.22 to −0.41):
`src/main/resources/datapacks/awakened_lands/data/awaken/lithostitched/biome_injector/lands/*.json`. Vanilla's
cave biomes are separate climate entries, so they are untouched and generate wherever their parameters fit
(`/Volumes/MEDIA/Awaken/.ref/src/net/minecraft/world/level/biome/OverworldBiomeBuilder.java:906-933`):

| Cave biome | Vanilla rule | So under the Awakened table |
|---|---|---|
| Dripstone caves | continentalness 0.8-1.0 (deep inland) | under any land far from the sea; Tectonic's `awakened` larger continents give more such land (inference, not measured) |
| Lush caves | humidity 0.7-1.0 | under the wettest columns (jungles, the wettest dark forest = Lumen Forest, old-growth taiga) |
| Sulfur caves (new in 26.3) | coast-to-inland continentalness, erosion 5-6, weirdness −1.1..−0.85 | flatter, eroded land |
| Deep dark | the bottom (`addBottomBiome`), erosion 0-1 | under low-erosion uplands (mountains: Shattered Highlands, Frost Spires, Alpine ranges) |

Underground columns that are not a cave biome report the surface biome (replaced in all its parameter points), so a
plain stone cave under Elderwood "is" Elderwood to every system that reads the biome (spawns, Lens, sound).

The biome table (`terrain/BiomeTableTests.java:16-75`) records per biome what AWAKEN puts there: `dripstone_caves`
(Chime Cricket 26, Drip Lurker 22, Gloomstalker 10, Thousandfoot 1, Wisp Hound 10; lair Thousandfoot), `lush_caves`
(Chime Cricket 12, Gloomstalker 10, Wickling 26, Wisp Hound 10; second look `crystal_nest`), `deep_dark` (Chime
Cricket 6, Hushling 24), **`sulfur_caves` (nothing)**.

## (c) The Descent (`descent/`)

### The Well

400-700 blocks from world spawn, picked from 32 seeded candidates on dry, flat land, built at runtime (SavedData):
a sunken court round a 15-wide shaft, terraces, twelve pillars at radius 18, landmark radius 23, shaft 110 deep
(`descent/well/Well.java:30-38, 43-53`). Falling in, or the lift, sends you to stratum 1
(`Well.java:472-476`). The Horizon names it from level **10** (`world/landmark/LandmarkKind.java:11`).

### The strata

One generator, `awaken:stratum` (codec field `stratum` 1-10, 0 = the Endless): terrain written straight into the
chunk, no carvers, no biome features except loot chests, **no structures** (`createForFlat(..., Stream.empty())`),
no generation-time mobs (`descent/world/StratumGenerator.java:80-85, 155-178, 179-186`). Height 0-256, no skylight,
fixed time, ambient light 0.04-0.22 (`descent/world/Layout.java:11-16`;
`src/main/resources/data/awaken/dimension_type/stratum_*.json`). Every form: an arrival platform at feet y **200**,
a spiral ramp (the helix) down the shaft wall, a camp at y 20 (47 in form 7, 40 in form 10) with a Waystone, bed and
lantern, a 5×5 pit at the centre, the guardian's lair 14 north (`Layout.java:19-25, 55-69`). Rock beyond radius
150 (124 in form 7) is solid (`descent/world/Terrain.java:108, 308-329`).

**Is the Descent "the cave game"?** No. It is a vertical dungeon run with one authored recipe per stratum (platform,
ramp, camp, pit) and one rule each: an open shaft, not a cave network. Its open shafts (radius 26-112, 150-180 deep)
are exactly the space the Tether and Wings are good at, and with the rope's no-fall-damage rule a Tether holder can
skip most of a helix. Nothing from overworld caves is reused: its own generator, vanilla blocks (cave vines, roots,
moss, tuff, calcite) and vanilla chest loot tables only. No overworld cave leads into it; only the Well does.

## (d) The movement system in rock

Everyone has the Runner's Kit from the first second (config `runnersKit`, default true): every `Move` is unlocked
whatever its listed rank (`body/BodyConfig.java:18-21`; `body/Body.java:41-45`). No move checks the dimension, the
sky or block types except where the table says (`body/BodyServer.java:77-80`). Every probe reads collision shapes, so
any natural block counts unless noted.

**Verdict for (d):** cave parkour is mostly a **terrain job**. Hangs, drops, mantles, slides, rolls, vaults, vine
climbs, zips and swings already work on natural rock. Wall-runs need flat axis-aligned walls (terrain), the pipe grip
and eaves need climb-line blocks (placement), and stair slides need stairs. Two things are **mechanics jobs**: a
rope you can pay out (rappel, fixed lines), and a reason not to simply rope-drop every shaft (falls are free in
rope states today).

## (g) Verdict

### What a player finds underground

| When | Player | What is down there |
|---|---|---|
| **Hour 1** | L5, E rank, Runner's Kit | Vanilla 26.3 caves (Tectonic's rivers and lava tunnels with the pack), vanilla structures (mineshafts, monster rooms, geodes, trial chambers). In the three older cave biomes, Hollows hints before the awakening (Chime Crickets, Wicklings), then hunters at Commons levels 1-8 (+10 in the deep dark). Possibly a "Lair of the Thousandfoot · 1.2 km NE" Now line (from L3) pointing at a sealed dome 24+ blocks inside rock, with no depth cue. No anomaly, happening, camp, quest or sound of AWAKEN's below ~24 blocks. The Well exists 400-700 out (not on the Horizon until L10); stratum 1 is open but uses level-17 content. Moves: hang, drop, mantle, slide into crawlspaces, vine climbs; wall-runs almost never |
| **Hour 5** | L11, D rank for about half | The same caves. Strata 2-3 open at D. Mining's Clean Strike (L5) and Ore Sense (craft level 10). Wall Stride cores at D. Hollow dens (surface) from region level 8. The Veil (~8 h by the plan) brings the first real underground sense: ores, spawners and buried chests through rock, and Veil rifts at buried chests |
| **Hour 20** | L27, C rank | The Tether: any rock face is an anchor, shafts are zip-and-hang ladders and free falls; strata 4-5 open; the Leaper soon after. The overworld underground itself has not changed since hour 1 |

### Biggest gaps

1. **No places.** One cave structure (the Crystal Nest, sealed) and one Precursor chamber; nothing to walk into,
   no cave landmark, no Horizon source for anything underground, the lair line has no depth.
2. **The living world stops at the surface.** Anomalies, the Pulse, camps, Wayfarers, the ledger and the Atlas are
   all heightmap-based (section b).
3. **Movement has nothing authored in rock.** No climb-line blocks, flat run walls or anchor features in caves; no
   rappel; free falls in rope states make depth harmless for Tether holders; Aether refills slowly underground.
4. **The senses are blank.** No `hollows` soundscape (AmbientSounds and Sound Physics carry caves), no Lens look for
   any cave biome, and plain caves borrow the surface biome's grade.
5. **Sulfur caves** (new in 26.3) have no fauna, lair, sound or look.
6. **The Descent is disconnected.** Its tunnels are empty and nothing in the overworld's caves hints at it.
