## Scope (PLAN section 4; build all of it)
1. **The Hearth Ember** (`awaken:hearth_ember`): a small iron lantern holding a bell-gold coal, held like the Watch's
   3D hand lantern, its light drawn on the ground round you (the technique of `citizens.client.CityGlow`; no light
   blocks). One per player, kept through death (like the quest relics). How you get it:
   - the end of The Last Delivery at the Wayfarer camp: Ilse's line "First night out? Take a coal from our fire. Carry
     it till you find your ground, then set it down. It'll know." and the Ember as the finish reward;
   - talking to Ilse (or any camp resident) while you have no Ember and no Hearth, after registration;
   - classic worlds (no Carillon): given when the player awakens.
   The Now line (`HearthNow`): "Set your Ember where you'd live", under a tracked chain step, over bookkeeping.
2. **Setting it down** founds a **Tier 0 Hearthfire** (radius 24): a stone ring block with a living flame (light 14, our
   own art). Refused in the city reserve (`CityBasin.reserved`), a Precursor ward, within 64 of a gate, or another
   Hearth's radius, each with a plain reason. The moment: the coal sinks into the ring, warmth runs out over 24 blocks
   (our particles; the grass brightens a shade on the client), a low hearth tone, fireflies at night, then the
   **YOUR GROUND** card with a name field (offer a name from the region's grammar; the player can type their own). From
   then on, entering your land shows "<NAME> · your hearth" as a place caption through the director. Tier 0 gives
   respawn (unless you sleep elsewhere), rest healing and calm land (check every existing Hearth rule keys off any
   Hearth: Living Wilds' calm, no happenings, no gates within 64, no camps within 120). No town, no Spire, no requests at
   Tier 0. Until the first room stands, using the fire with an empty hand lifts the Ember again.
3. **Tier I**: a Lord's Heart used on the Hearthfire (or the Anchor placed on it) turns it into today's Anchor in place.
   Existing Anchors in old worlds are Tier I with the reading on.
4. **The reading** (in `RoomScanner.build`, PLAN 4.5): six readings 0-3 per closed room (closed, light, warmth, room to
   live, made with care, detail and comfort; best of each kind counts), the grade (average, never more than one step
   above the weakest), the name (grade, main material, purpose; or a sign's text inside), saved so a restart doesn't
   rescan every home at once (schema bump). A newly recognised or regraded room shows its name once as an AMBIENT
   caption (topic `hearth_room`). Never a toast per block. Comfort (best bedroom, a warm hearth, table and seats, soft
   things, plants, and the land's field, pen, garden, lit yard, wall) and Style in words.
5. **Rested** on waking in your own bed at home: 8 minutes + 1 per Comfort; perks through `Progression.addPerkSource`:
   `awaken:energy_regen` (existing) and `awaken:craft_rate` (new id; W2 reads it in the crafts). Shown on the band.
6. **Room kinds** (PLAN 4.6; all but the Mine head, which is W6): Shelter, Bedroom, Kitchen (meals crafted in it carry
   a home-cooked mark; eating one extends its effects by half, all inside `hearth`), Forge (today's Armory: keep its
   saved id), Study (recognised; its Blueprint effect is wired after W2 merges), Greenhouse (recognised; its herb effect
   is wired after W2 merges), Stable (grants `awaken:whistle_range`; make the mount module read it if it doesn't yet),

   rings at dawn and dusk), Lookout (12+ up, windows on three sides: the Atlas reveals 256 round home through its hook,
   one more Mira rumour a play-day), and today's Sanctuary, Trophy Hall, Barracks and Workshop (machines spend a quarter
   less essence: a one-line read in `above`).
7. **The room panel**: the room you stand in (name, grade, six pips, the one thing that would lift it most, e.g. "light
