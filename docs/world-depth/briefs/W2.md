## Scope (PLAN section 7; build all of it)
1. **The `delve` module** (`dev.awaken.delve`, `src/main/merge/delve/`, `DelveModule.init` from `Awaken`, a client entry
   from `AwakenClient`, commands `/awaken delve ...` for tours and tests).
2. **Hide, metal, light** (PLAN 7.1): tags `#awaken:tier_metal_<d|c|b|a|s>` (D iron ingot, C diamond, B Deepglass
   shard with an amethyst shard in the recipe, A netherite scrap, S starmetal ingot); every D-up gear piece, mattock and
   forged Arms weapon needs its metal, and the hook-tag options that let a tier skip its metal are removed for that
   slot. E unchanged. Gatewrought armour to 20 and toughness 2.0 (`gear/HunterTier`). Power foci from D up need gold.
3. **Every ore's job** (PLAN 7.2): Blueprints become 4 paper, 1 lapis, 1 essence; catalysts Heat Sunshard, Frost
   Frostspar, Gravity Fault Seam, Charge copper (as today), Spirit Deepglass (make sure each Lands crystal gives an item
   a player can carry: its block item or a shard).
4. **The handouts stop** (decision 8): gate loot (`gates/run/Loot.java`) pays no raw ore beyond each theme's flavour
   item; the gate market (`gates/run/Rooms.java`) stops selling diamonds and netherite scrap. The Mine Echo is W6's.
5. **Ore XP by rarity and depth** (PLAN 7.6; `progression/ProgressionModule.java`, `XpBudget`): coal ×0.5, copper ×0.7,
   iron ×1, gold, redstone, lapis ×1.5, emerald ×4, diamond and Deepglass ×6, a lode's heart a lump; deep ores under
   their own budget; the first of each ore a discovery.
6. **The Mining craft** (`living/craft/**`, `living/client/CraftFx.java`): craft XP keeps its own pace (only the
   Hunter-level share is budgeted); multiply craft XP by the perk `awaken:craft_rate` (W1's Rested sets it); Ore Sense
   works with mattocks; Vein Sight outlines the whole vein; Deep Ear plays a soft sound from the direction of an open
   cave or Deepglass behind the wall; the level-40 perk becomes Deepglass Cutter (Deepglass breaks faster and yields more).
7. **Lodes** (PLAN 7.3): veins of one vanilla ore, 20-120 blocks along a cave wall or through rock, a few per region at
   the ore's own depth, placed deterministically from the seed so `dev.awaken.delve.api.Lodes.nearest(level, pos, ore)`
   needs no scan; a **heart** block at the centre. They glint in lantern light (a texture or glow, our own).
8. **The vein gives** (PLAN 7.4): breaking a heart lights the lode's outline through the rock (the Ore Sense glass, the
   whole vein), runs white cracks along it, kicks the camera, and for 30 s the lode's blocks break in two blows each;
   "LODE · <ore> · <count>" through `signal.Finds`. **Cave-ins**: a heart broken in a big unsupported chamber trickles
   dust for 3 s, then the roof comes down (reuse the pattern of `gates/run/RigCaveIn.java`); never inside a Hearth (use
   `core.api.HearthHooks`), never on placed blocks.
9. **The strike's feel** (client): every ore its own strike sound, sparks off hard stone, grit off soft, a short hit on
   the last blow (`core.api.CombatFeedback`). **The heavy strike**: hold the attack with a pick or mattock to charge on
   Arms' heavy timing, release to bore three blocks along the aim (server checks: breakable, not in a ward, the city,
   a gate or someone's Hearth; three times the durability; hunger).
10. **New finds**: **Deepglass** (block and `awaken:deepglass_shard`; diamond tier to mine; a faint hum; the Veil sees
    it) generated as small veins below y 0 now (W5 adds the seeps) and in the Descent's strata walls
    (`descent/world/Palette.java`); **starmetal** (the fallen star's loot gives `awaken:raw_starmetal`, smelted to
    `awaken:starmetal_ingot`); **bell bronze** (`awaken:bell_bronze_ingot` from copper and amethyst) and a craftable
    bell made from it.
11. **Tools** (PLAN 7.5): the **Tuning Fork** (`awaken:tuning_fork`: tune it on an ore, then use it on stone; the chime
    climbs in pitch as you face the nearest lode of that ore within 48, with a soft arc on the band); **pitons and
    Runner's rope** (`awaken:piton`, `awaken:runner_rope`: a piton on a lip hangs a rope 32 blocks down, climbable with
    the pipe grip through `#awaken:runner_climbs`; the two-piton zip line only if the Ascent's existing line traversal
    can ride a run of blocks, else leave it and say so); the **miner's lamp** (`awaken:miners_lamp`, worn on the head,
    its light drawn on the rock ahead like `citizens.client.CityGlow`).
12. **Pacing**: add a mining model to `tools/sim/pacing_sim.py` (taking ore out of gates moves D and C gear onto mining)
    and check the D and C tier times still land near the level road's steps; report the numbers.
