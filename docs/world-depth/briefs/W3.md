## Scope (PLAN 5.2-5.6)
1. **Vanilla villages off in new worlds** (the Awakened Lands pack, new worlds only; old worlds keep theirs): their
   structure sets stop, and nothing else of vanilla's goes with them (a cured zombie villager becomes one of our people, a
   refugee W6 can settle).
2. **Where and how big**: one settlement plan per region from the region map (`living/region`), by its land and band:
   **hamlet** (3-6 homes, 8-16 people; one or two in most regions) and **village** (8-20 homes, 20-50 people; about one
   region in two) in this phase. Towns and mining towns are W7. Special kinds where they're a style, not new systems:
   frost longhouses, Mire stilt houses, a fishing village on piers, a desert caravanserai, a woodcutters' camp. **The
   first hamlet is placed on purpose 700-1,100 blocks from Carillon on the road north past the Wayfarer camp** (the camp
   is 450 up the High Gate road), so hour one shows the city, a camp, then ordinary people living outside the walls.
   Clear of the city reserve, wards, lairs, gates and Hearths (and Hearths can't be founded inside a settlement).
3. **The planner** (Java, seeded, deterministic, no chunk loads beyond the generator's own height estimate, once per
   settlement and cached): a site on gentle ground near water; a road skeleton that follows the contours (a main street,
   lanes, a green with a well, the bell post and the notice board); plots along the frontages; buildings by size and
   land (cottages, longhouses, two-storey houses, the smithy, the inn, the mill on water, barns, the granary, the bell
   tower, pens, strip fields, a dock). It writes the `Plot` and raster records the city's grammar reads.
4. **Painted by the city's grammar** with styles keyed by biome and wealth (not Carillon's seven districts): lower and
   plainer than Carillon, every house different, the same climbing grid (hoods, cornices, window boxes, drainpipes) so a
   village is a little course too. Per chunk at world generation like every ruin (a structure start; pieces ≤ 14×14
   written straight into sections; ≤ 4-5 light sources a chunk; night light drawn like Carillon's).
5. **Interiors, at last**: a room filler that furnishes a house from who lives there (a bed per person, a table and
   seats, a hearth or stove, storage, the trade's blocks, lanterns, carpets), so every village house can be walked into.
   Build it generic so Carillon's B4 can adopt it later; don't change Carillon's buildings.
6. **The people** on W0's `Settlements` API through a village `RosterBuilder`: households from homes, jobs from
   stations; village jobs (farmer, shepherd, woodcutter, miner, fisher, hunter going out at dawn and home at dusk with a
   carcass on a pole, carter, elder) beside the city's smith, baker, washer, lamplighter, bellringer, tavernkeeper, mason,
   Watch, healer and children. The bell rings dawn and dusk; the midday meal at home; the inn at night; chimneys smoke when
   someone's home. Village speech pools (greetings by standing, news of the region, rumours); a soundscape bed per village.
7. **What you do there**: **trade in emeralds** (the emerald is the coin of the world: install `EconomyHooks` with it;
   each job sells what it makes; each region's villages make something of their own; prices show your standing);
   **requests** on the notice board from the village's real state (counts shown, paid in emeralds and standing);
   **standing** per settlement (Stranger, Known, Friend, Kin) on W0's recognition; **a bed at the inn** (respawn there
   once, the night's rumours); in a village, **a scribe** who sells enchanted books.
8. **State** (saved, versioned): people, prosperity, mood, damage (with original blocks, for W4's Burning and Rebuild),
   buildings added, each player's standing, a short history the Journal can show. W4 builds troubles and growth on it:
   leave clean hooks (`Villages` API in SPEC §12: `settlementAt`, `standing`, `nearest`, a damage and history record).
