## Scope (PLAN section 6)
1. **The shape** (6.1): a call seen or heard from afar → an ask (a choice card, Help / Not now) → a place → a deed → the
   return to the square → a lasting change in blocks, people and standing.
2. **The rules** (6.2): caused by real world state (a camp that grew, a gate due to break, a beast's lair, the town's mine,
   the Deep Tide's new moon, a storm), never rolled from nothing; at most one per village and two within 1,500 of a player;
   none in a world's first two hours or inside a Hearth; found, never listed (the call is in the world: smoke, the village
   bell ringing the alarm fast and heard to 256, a runner who waves you down only if you're Known there, festival lanterns
   and music); the Journal holds a trouble only once you've said Help; missable on its own clock, ending worse without you
   but never a dead end, never a named death, never a fail screen; always paid (emeralds, standing, a keepsake, XP through
   its budget); a world option **calm villages** turns troubles off (festivals and growth stay); multiplayer-safe (a trouble
   counts for a party within 48).
3. **The launch set** (6.3), except the Deep Mine, which waits for W7's mining towns:
   - **Taken**: a named beast's lair or a cave lair within 400 (the fauna lairs; W5's joined Crystal Nest); the alarm at
     night, the parent next morning; the trail (drag marks, torn cloth; the Tracks perk lights it); the lair fight; cut the
     person free; **bring them out**: they hold on to your back (no sprint or wall-run while carried: a carried pose drawn on
     both bodies), or follow you while you hang a Runner's rope for them; the reunion in the square; the lair cleared for
     good; the person greets you by name from then on (and W6 can let them settle); a carved token of yours on the
     family's door.
   - **The Burning**: a camp or nest at tier 4+ within 800 targets the village (the camps already raid Hearths and the
     Wayfarer camp); a readable ladder (a rumour, scouts at the treeline, the warning a play-day ahead); with warning you
     hold the village (shutters close, the Watch lines the square, fire arrows light roofs, villagers form a bucket line you
     can join: W0's fire, scaled up); missed, the houses are charred (real block changes from W3's damage record) and the
     survivors camp by the well.
   - **The Rebuild**: the elder asks for timber, stone and iron (counts on the notice board); masons raise scaffolding and
     rebuild over three play-days from the damage record; the new houses carry a beam of your colour; a family that left
     comes back.
   - **The Bell**: the village bell silent at dusk (stolen by the Unbound, rogue Awakened, or cracked by a break); night
     hunters bolder there; take it back from an Unbound camp, or cast a new one at the smith from copper and amethyst
     (bell bronze) and haul it up the tower yourself; it rings dawn and dusk again with a ring of light over the land;
     breaks nearby are warned earlier and blight stays off its fields.
   - **Gone Missing**: a child, a miner or a hunter didn't come home; a search with lanterns at dusk; clues on the land, a
     ledge only the rope reaches, a shaft only a climb goes down, a cave with a slide out.
   - **The Grey Fields**: a gate near the village due to break (its own breaks-in clock); the elder asks you to close it;
     if you did, nothing happens (the best ending); if it broke, blight greys the fields and some people are Hollowed;
     a cure (Sun's Cure, or an herb remedy if that chain isn't built) brings them back.
   - **The Festival**: every ~10 play-days per village (harvest, first snow, the bell's day); lanterns and music heard far
     off at dusk; the market closes for the day; the rooftop race on an Ascent route over the roofs, the fishing and cooking
     contests (the crafts), dancing; prizes and standing.
4. **Growth** (6.4): prosperity rises with troubles solved, trade and deliveries; a fenced plot gets scaffolding, masons
   work it over two or three play-days, a cart brings a new family; a hamlet can become a village over tens of hours.
5. **History**: each village's history (what you did there, what happened without you) shown in the Journal through the
   existing Journal page (`living` quest journal or discovery-ux's filed notices: use the API, don't build a second Journal).
