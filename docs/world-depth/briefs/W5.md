## Scope (PLAN section 8; build all of it)
1. **Cave places** as structures placed with the lair's cave placement (`LairStructure.cave()`: cave biome, depth, rock
   overhead) plus a check for natural air nearby, each carving short tunnels into the nearest natural cave. Built from a
   library of authored rooms strung together (Deep Rock Galactic, G45). Every place passes M44's five tests (a rule, a
   resource, a sensory set, a story entrance, a route) and has a route that needs no rope (Spelunky, G46):
   - **Sinkhole**: a round shaft 15-30 across, 40-110 deep from the surface into a cave system; ledge rings in odd rows
     (the city's climbing grid; drops within the Ascent's 0.9-3.2), hanging roots and vines, an old rope from a piton, a
     waterfall into a pool deep enough to dive from the rim, a lit grotto at the bottom with a lode (W2's `Lodes`).
     Mist rising by day and bats at dusk; a Horizon site "Sinkhole · a way down". **The first one is placed on purpose
     near the Commons' edge** (600-900 from Carillon, off the camp road).
   - **The Delvers' mine**: the abandoned workings of the people who dug Carillon's bell metal: dressed galleries whose
     cut walls are flat and axis-aligned 2+ high (real wall-runs: the Runner's rule in `body/RunnerRules`), timber
     shafts with ladders and ropes, a rail incline to ride down in a minecart, rope bridges over a chasm, collapsed
     sections to slide under, a foreman's office with a Delver's log (Codex), a lode map read with lapis, an old pick
     (a relic). The logs say they dug too deep and broke into a seep.
   - **Seep**: below y 0, where the Deep presses in: violet-black rock, Deepglass veins and spires (W2's block), muffled
     sound and a low hum, region level +5 inside (`living/api/Regions`), a Hollows nest, and a **seep heart** (a
     Deep-born creature three levels over the region, our own look) whose death quiets the seep.
   - **Chute**: a long slope paved with old stair-cut steps (the stair slide already works on steps) or ice, a 60-block
     ride that throws you out over an underground lake.
   - **Grotto**: behind a waterfall, a glowing pool, a geode you climb inside, a buried cache (a region secret:
     `awaken:secrets_found`; a Veil secret through `#awaken:veil_secrets`), sometimes a legendary fishing spot.
   - **Joined lairs**: the Crystal Nest gets a tunnel to the natural caves; the lair line says how far down ("Lair of the
     Thousandfoot · 1.2 km NE · 40 below").
   - **Vents**: updrafts over magma in lava tunnels and sulfur caves (the Ascent's `Updrafts`, today sandbox-only): glide
     up and refill Aether. Sulfur caves (26.2's, empty of AWAKEN today) get vents and a look.
   Not every place ends in a fight: vary the last room (a vista, a rope puzzle, a lake, a log, a lode).
2. **Moving through rock** (with the Ascent's numbers): the **rappel** (on a Tether line, sneak + jump pays rope out:
   lower yourself at your own pace, kick off the wall, swing into a side passage; server-checked like every rope state)
   and **rope fittings** forged from metal at a smith or the Forge (an iron line reaches 6 further, a gold reel winds
   faster, a Deepglass line pays out smoothly for the rappel). Falls on a rope stay free.
3. **The senses**: a Hollows cave soundscape (drips, far water, stone settling, a breath of air from a passage that leads
   out) and the deep's hum below y 0, louder near a seep (a family soundscape for the Conductor); a Lens look for each
   cave biome (lush green glow, dripstone amber, the deep dark's ink, sulfur's yellow haze, a seep's violet); big places
   get a name and a quiet caption ("THE DELVERS' DEEP · old mine") through the notification director.
4. **A way home**: underground, a Now line "Surface ↑ 34 · NE 60" (the nearest open sky you came from), and a thread of
   the Signal's light along the floor toward it while you hold the band key (The Way Forward's glance will host it later;
   say where it plugs in).
5. **The Descent's tunnels** get Deepglass lodes and small grottos, so the strata gain a mining job; no overworld cave
   connects to the strata.
