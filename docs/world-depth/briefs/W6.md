## Scope
1. **People who live with you** (PLAN 4.7; absorbs Living Wilds Phase G's settlers): someone moves in when you know them
   (a finished Wayfarer chain, Friend standing in their village, Trusted in Carillon, someone you rescued), a spare bedroom
   of Snug or better stands (W1's reading), and their trade has its room. They walk up the road with a bundle and take the
   room ("Ilse's room"). They live a day on the people engine: your Hearth registers as a settlement through W0's API, so
   they work in their room or your fields by day, eat in the kitchen or hall, sleep in their bed. Today's townsfolk (Oren
   E, Mira D, Teodor C, Sun B) keep their ranks but now wait for a bed ("Oren will come when there's a bed for him") and
   gain homes, routes and work instead of wandering 20 blocks round the Anchor.
2. **What they do with what you bring home**: Oren (Forge) tempers and takes **commissions** (hand him the metal and the
   hide and he forges the piece overnight, a temper up); Hettie (by your fields and pens) harvests and replants into the
   nearest chest and keeps two of each animal breeding; Ilse (Greenhouse) brews tonics from herbs you bring; **a miner**
   (Mine head) goes down your shaft by day and brings up ore from its depth each evening; Mira (Lookout) rumours; Teodor
   (Study) reads relics and logs; Sun heals; Wren strings rope lines between your roofs; Pell brings a letter and a rumour
   each play-day.
3. **Your own mine** (PLAN 7.7): the **Mine head** room kind (a shaft 16+ deep from its floor, with ladder or rail); the
   Hearth reads the shaft's depth and any lode it cuts (W2's `Lodes`); a miner settler or the Mine Echo works it, and each
   evening the ore of that depth comes up, and that lode's ore if the shaft cut one. **The Mine Echo's dice are gone.**
4. **Tiers grow from the home** (PLAN 4.8): Tier II Steading (D-rank, four rooms with one Fine, a field or a pen, iron),
   III Hall (C, a Grand room, gold and amethyst), IV Village (B, a bell tower, four settlers, diamonds), V Town (A, eight
   settlers, diamonds, obsidian, a Lord's Heart); the Anchor's flame shows the tier (a block state); settler caps per tier.
   At **Tier IV your village gets its name on the Atlas** as a settlement (the Atlas's hook) and the place caption
   becomes "<NAME> · your village". (Caravans on your road are W7; your festival is W4's: leave a hook a festival can use.)
5. **Your home as a course** (PLAN 9): Carillon's climbable kit (drainpipe, trellis, beams, window boxes, wall ivy,
   hanging ladder, hoist beam) becomes craftable and drops when broken in the wild (Carillon's own blocks stay protected);
   Wren's rope lines; the Training Grounds' ghost on your own route.
6. **The two wiring edits** W1 and W2 left (SPEC §28.1): the Study halves the Blueprints' paper; the Greenhouse triples
   herb ripeness.
