# World depth: what the best open worlds do between the set pieces
Research for AWAKEN's "time between gates" problem: living villages, mining that matters, caves built for movement, a Hearth that reads the player's own base. Compiled 2026-10-26.

For guidance and gating (BotW pull, Witcher icons, Far Cry people-tell-you, Metroid locks, Valheim no-build rule, Outer Wilds log) see `docs/progression/research.md`. It is cited here as "progression research" and not repeated.

**Verification tags**
- **[V]**: read on the page itself (developer, critic, or an official wiki table), or a direct report that quotes the developer.
- **[P]**: partly verified. A fan guide, a wiki I could not open, or a search excerpt of a page that blocked the fetch. Numbers may be off by a patch.
- **[U]**: a lead only. Not traced to a source.

**How to read an entry.** Each entry gives the mechanism with numbers, then where it turns to fluff or overwhelm (critics paraphrased), then the source, then one line for AWAKEN. Critics are paraphrased on purpose. The only direct quote in this file is in G10. Scores are the outlet's own.

**Not found.** No developer interview exists in my results for: Crimson Desert's NPC schedules (the wiki attributing one is [U]), Enshrouded's survivors, Kenshi's town takeovers, Factorio's ore placement, Noita's caves. Those entries say so.

# 1. Crimson Desert (Pearl Abyss, 2026)

### G1. Hand-placed reasons, machine-made filler
- **CLAIM:** Pearl Abyss splits the work. Major landmarks, quests and events are placed by hand. Vegetation and terrain are generated by rules and tuned for movement, not realism. Distant towers and villages are real places you can walk to. Time, weather and player actions change the world, and grass and trees react to movement and attacks. Neither report covers NPCs, caves or climbing. **[V]**
- **SOURCE:** Lee Woo Min, Gamemeca, 27 Aug 2026 (https://www.gamemeca.com/en/view.php?gid=1779680). Minwoo "Roootz" Kang, Inven Global, dated 26 Aug 2026 on the page (https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25231/pearl-abyss-shares-crimson-desert-development-philosophy-at-gamescom-dev). Speakers: Geuntae Ahn, Jinhwan Kim, Jinho Yun, Gamescom dev. See progression research entry 4.
- **AWAKEN:** Use the same split. Machines for terrain, trees and ambient mobs. Hands for anything the player is meant to remember: a village's square, a cave's first room, an event's staging.

### G2. Launch critics: the places are praised, the quest wrapper is not
- **CLAIM:** Most critics rate the world and its small stories. They split hard on what sits on top, and on whether exploring pays.
  - PCGamesN (Paul Kelly, 18 Mar 2026, 6/10): little reward for exploring; a port town read as a diorama; locals give a generic greeting; side content was busywork with the odd fight. It praised the floating Abyss puzzles as satisfying. **[V]**
  - Game Informer (Hayes Madsen, 18 Mar 2026, 7/10): calls the game overdesigned. Settlements are praised for atmosphere and small stories. Hundreds of side quests, some padded (a dye quest ran 15 minutes). **[V]**
  - Men's Journal via AOL (Brandon Key, 18 Mar 2026, no score): side tasks repeat (talk, clear a bandit camp, fetch a lost animal). Mining, woodcutting and arm wrestling are called shallow padding. **[V]**
  - Gaming Trend (Ron Burke and Joe Morgan, 18 Mar 2026, 95/100): side missions nearly as strong as the main story; 15 minigames (arm wrestling, archery, boxing, a card game); the map is full of caves, puzzles and mysteries, with usually something to find. **[V]**
  - Gfinity (Chris Davison, 7 May 2026, 8/10): lively towns, NPCs of varied age and background, pub games, fishing, fighting pits; hidden caves and treasure reward exploring. **[V]**
  - Lords of Gaming (Mahmood Ghaffar, 16 Apr 2026): NPCs keep routines you can watch, such as adults teaching children to chop wood at a lumber farm. **[V]**
  - A fan wiki says Pearl Abyss described villagers' routines as a reason to explore at different times of day. I could not trace that to Pearl Abyss. **[U]**
- **FLUFF RISK:** Same shape in every complaint. Talk, kill bandits, return. The activity list is wide, the quest design on top of it is narrow.
- **SOURCE:** https://www.pcgamesn.com/crimson-desert/review ; https://gameinformer.com/review/crimson-desert/open-world-overload ; https://www.aol.com/crimson-desert-review-most-gorgeous-220000162.html ; https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/crimson-desert-review/ ; https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/crimson-desert-review-an-ambitious-massive-and-occasionally-flawed-masterpiece ; https://lordsofgaming.net/2026/04/crimson-desert-review-in-progress-challenging-conventions/ ; https://thegameswiki.com/crimson-desert/wiki/npc-daily-routines (fan wiki)
- **AWAKEN:** Praise lands on places, puzzles and small stories. Blame lands on repeated quest verbs. Give each village events with their own verb, not go there, kill, come back.

### G3. The Greymane camp: a base that grows from quests and crews
- **CLAIM:** The camp starts as three tents. It grows to vendors, a blacksmith, a ranch and a farm. The player assigns crew to produce resources, which Gaming Trend found fun. Gfinity adds training stations, more recruit slots and dispatch missions. Game Informer says the camp is also where you unload, because inventory is small and forces trips to town or camp. **[V]** A fan guide counts five camp upgrade states at Howling Hill in chapter III, with the move to Pailune as the last, and calls dispatched comrades the main resource engine. **[P]** (Vulkk, 2 May 2026, page blocked, read via search excerpt.)
- **FLUFF RISK:** Game Informer notes the Greymanes reunion, the story's emotional core, is optional and easy to miss. Gamer Headlines (Roxanne Bernade, 23 Oct 2026) says camp and side activities link to the story, but not always persuasively, and can feel like errands. **[V]**
- **SOURCE:** https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/crimson-desert-review/ ; https://gameinformer.com/review/crimson-desert/open-world-overload ; https://gamerheadlines.com/crimson-desert-review/ ; https://vulkk.com/2026/05/02/crimson-desert-greymane-camp-guide-all-features-unlocks-and-upgrades/
- **AWAKEN:** The camp works as a visible ladder: more people, more shops, more jobs. It fails when the people are not met in the world first. Put the recruits in villages before they are in the Hearth.

### G4. Re-Blockade: a cleared place that can be lost again
- **CLAIM:** Patch 1.05.00 (May 2026) lets enemies retake forts and quarries you cleared. 23 sites, 13 factions. Three frequency settings (Stable, Conflict, War). On by default, with an off switch. It refreshes when you sleep, use a cooking pot, save and load, or fast travel. Rewards are XP, occasional relics and some resources, no gear. **[P]** Reports say players disliked it for weak rewards and few ways to defend. Patch 1.10.00 (5 Jun 2026) added Battle and Reconstruct stages and a Protection request through a region's contribution assessor, and liberated strongholds now pay contribution, provisions and trade goods. **[P]** The roadmap also lists new ways to defend strongholds from invasions. **[P]**
- **FLUFF RISK:** A place that changes back without worthwhile pay turns into a chore, and players turn it off. The game offers a Stable setting that turns it off.
- **SOURCE:** Shashwat, Nerd'sChalk, updated 4 May 2026 (https://nerdschalk.com/crimson-desert-how-to-re-blockade-every-fort-location-for-insane-xp-in-latest-patch-update/) ; Windows Central, summer patch (https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/crimson-desert-first-summer-patch-rolling-out-re-blockade-mounts-mini-games-more, body blocked, headline and search excerpt only) ; Digital Citizen (https://www.digitalcitizen.life/crimson-deserts-new-update-makes-stronghold-defenses-more-rewarding/, blocked, excerpt only) ; roadmap write-up (https://players.com.ua/en/news/pearl-abyss-unveils-new-crimson-desert-roadmap-through-september-2026/, excerpt only).
- **AWAKEN:** If a gate-cleared outpost or a defended village can fall again, the defence needs its own pay and a clear warning, and the player must be able to opt out of the pressure.

### G5. Gathering friction was fixed with storage and fewer hits
- **CLAIM:** Patch 1.04.00 (Apr 2026) added house layouts, 1,000-slot chests for gatherables and collectibles, food coolers and wardrobes. **[P]** Patch 1.12.00 lets players decorate the area outside the house, with 58 new items and two new crafting tools. **[P]** Patch 1.00.03 made trees need fewer hits and added fast-travel points early. **[P]** Gamer Headlines (23 Oct 2026) says resource collection was a pain point that patches eased with storage and inventory categories. **[V]**
- **FLUFF RISK:** Mining, woodcutting and ore hunting are required for gear upgrades, and food is the only healing source, so boss fights push the player back into gathering and cooking (Game Informer). The grind was real until the carrying capacity changed.
- **SOURCE:** https://gamerheadlines.com/crimson-desert-review/ ; https://gameinformer.com/review/crimson-desert/open-world-overload ; patch coverage: https://gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78324-crimson-desert-s-newest-patch-adds-difficulty-settings-overhauls-housing-and-expands-pets ; https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/crimson-desert-update-11200-adds-outdoor-housing-decor-new-gear-and-major-bug-fixes ; https://dotesports.com/crimson-desert/news/crimson-desert-patch-1-00-03-notes (all read as search excerpts)
- **AWAKEN:** Before adding ore types, fix the carry. Stacks, a Hearth chest that pulls from nearby containers, and fewer swings per block will do more than more nodes.

### G6. Mining, climbing and caves in Crimson Desert
- **CLAIM:** Mining needs a pickaxe from a quest (bring 2 iron ore) or 31 copper at a shop. Look-mode marks mineable ore blue and the rest red. A palm ability can break deposits without a pickaxe. Iron shows on cliffs and river walls, and cave mining feeds upgrades. **[P]** (guide sites, excerpts). Climbing is borrowed from BotW: almost any surface, limited by stamina. The guiding rule is that anything you can see, you can reach. Paths usually end in loot, recipes or quests. **[V]** (Lords of Gaming) Another reviewer says stamina runs out often. **[P]** Game Informer calls exploring ancient ruins a highlight, with Abyss artifacts earned through puzzles, and notes one puzzle locks its fast-travel point until solved. **[V]** The Abyss storyline felt disjointed at first, then clicked after a puzzle temple. **[V]**
- **SOURCE:** Mahmood Ghaffar, Lords of Gaming, 16 Apr 2026, updated 12 May 2026 (https://lordsofgaming.net/2026/04/crimson-desert-review-in-progress-challenging-conventions/) ; mining guides: https://allthings.how/how-to-get-a-pickaxe-and-start-mining-in-crimson-desert/ , https://beebom.com/where-to-find-pickaxe-and-mine-in-crimson-desert/amp/ ; GameSpot review (https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/crimson-desert-review-highest-fantasy/1900-6418474/, blocked, excerpt only) ; TheGamer review (https://www.thegamer.com/crimson-desert-review/, excerpt only)
- **AWAKEN:** The pickaxe tell (a mark that says "this one breaks") is good UI at no cost. Stamina on climbs makes a route a decision, which the Runner moveset needs for caves.

### G7. Is it "intuitive and not overwhelming"? The critics say partly
- **CLAIM:** Madsen at Game Informer found too many moving pieces and never reached systems like trading and banking. Gamer Headlines calls it demanding, with a learning curve and uneven pacing, and recommends it to players who like learning a world. **[V]** TechRadar's review (excerpt) says most players will still be in the starting region after 50 hours. Tom's Guide titled its review as an overwhelming RPG experience (body not read). Metacritic sits near 77 on PC and PS5, OpenCritic near 74% recommend. **[P]** The first Abyss puzzle needed a day-one patch (see progression research 5).
- **SOURCE:** https://gameinformer.com/review/crimson-desert/open-world-overload ; https://gamerheadlines.com/crimson-desert-review/ ; https://www.techradar.com/gaming/crimson-desert-review ; https://www.tomsguide.com/gaming/crimson-desert-review
- **AWAKEN:** The reference game is itself accused of overload. Copy the sense of discovery and reachability. Do not copy the count of systems open at once.

# 2. Zelda: Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom

### G8. Korok seeds: cheap pulls with tiny pay, beside authored shrines
- **CLAIM:** Fujibayashi wrote that the team first hid random rocks at suspicious spots and found rocks alone were not intriguing or rewarding enough, so they hid small Koroks there. **[V]** (reported 25 Jun 2017 from his Zelda.com developer post). There are 900 seeds. 441 are enough to max the inventory. The 900th pays a golden joke item, which Fujibayashi told IGN the team thought was funny. **[P]** Many outlets read the joke as a jab at collect-everything games. One fan breakdown in my results puts seeds at about 72% of the original game's completion percentage (source not traced). **[U]**
- **FLUFF RISK:** The seeds are a checklist at heart. They work because most are tied to a small observation or puzzle, the pay (inventory slots) is useful up to a cap, and nothing big is gated behind them. Past the cap they are completionism only.
- **SOURCE:** Nintendo Everything, 25 Jun 2017 (http://nintendoeverything.com/zelda-breath-of-the-wilds-korok-seeds-were-originally-stone-objects/) ; Zelda Dungeon (https://www.zeldadungeon.net/?p=126854, blocked, excerpt) ; Nintendo Life, Mar 2017 (https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2017/03/heres_what_you_get_for_collecting_all_900_korok_seeds_in_zelda_breath_of_the_wild) ; Fandom feature on the joke reward (https://www.fandom.com/articles/legend-zelda-breath-wild-golden-poop, excerpt). For shrines as visible, scarce pull see progression research 1.
- **AWAKEN:** A small collectible is fine if it hides in a place the eye already wants to look, pays something that stops mattering at a cap, and gates nothing big. Do not let it dominate any completion measure.

### G9. Tarrey Town: a village you grow by finding people
- **CLAIM:** In BotW's "From the Ground Up" the player buys the Hateno house, then recruits four builders spread across the map (found at a mine near Goron City, at Kara Kara Bazaar, at an inn in Rito Village and in Zora's Domain), twice delivers 10 wood, and earns access to Tarrey Town and 3 diamonds. **[P]** In TotK the town already stands. A side chain ends in buying a plot for 1,500 rupees and building a house from two rooms with Ultrahand. Sources disagree on the Sundelion count for the first step. A build cap of 15 parts applies. **[P]**
- **FLUFF RISK:** Unwinnable (15 Oct 2025) calls the BotW quest optional and tedious in itself, but says TotK's payoff only works because it assumes you did it. Nintendo Life's staff writer praises that the quest changes Hyrule, even though it is mostly fetching. Zelda Dungeon readers wanted a bigger settlement-building expansion. **[P]** (pages blocked, excerpts)
- **SOURCE:** https://zelda-archive.fandom.com/wiki/From_the_Ground_Up ; https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/zelda-breath-of-the-wild-tarrey-town-quest ; https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/zelda-tears-of-the-kingdom-tarrey-town-quest-how-to-build-a-house ; https://unwinnable.com/2025/10/15/ruins-in-reverse/ ; https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Home_on_Arrange
- **AWAKEN:** The pattern to copy: finding people across the map is the quest, the town visibly fills with the people you found, and a sequel-style payoff (a plot to build on) arrives later. The tedium comes from the fetch legs, so make the legs scenic or short.

### G10. Caves that make a familiar cliff look different
- **CLAIM:** Takizawa said finding a cliffside cave may make players wonder whether other cliffs hide caves too. His line: when a familiar place gains something of value, "you begin to see the world in a different light." Dohta noted that in BotW cliffs could only be climbed and their sides were bare. Fujibayashi said caves are now explorable, which the team could not do before. Dungeons connect directly to the surface. **[V]**
- **SOURCE:** Nintendo, Ask the Developer Vol. 9, Tears of the Kingdom, part 3 (https://www.nintendo.com/sg/interview/totk/03.html). Date not on the fetched page; the series ran in May 2023.
- **AWAKEN:** One cave entrance next to a road turns every cliff in the region into a question. Place caves so that a player who finds the first one begins to scan walls.

### G11. The Depths: a late idea, built by inverting the surface
- **CLAIM:** The Depths were not in the first plans. After the dive mechanic was tested, the team wanted more vertical range. They first considered deep vertical caves but felt caves alone would not surprise players enough, so they made an underground area about the size of the overworld. They reused the BotW map, turning river boundaries into high walls and swapping highs and lows. Programmers built a prototype quickly. Fujibayashi still said it took many adjustments to reach its final shape. **[V]** (as reported from an art-book interview).
- **FLUFF RISK:** I found no major-outlet critique. User reviews say the Depths is large, dark and mostly empty, tedious, and costly in resources for the reward. They are individual opinions, not critics. **[U]**
- **SOURCE:** Nintendo Everything, 22 Sep 2024 (https://nintendoeverything.com/zelda-tears-of-the-kingdom-depths-origins-interview/) ; Zelda Dungeon (https://www.zeldadungeon.net/the-legend-of-zelda-tears-of-the-kingdom-developers-say-the-depths-were-made-in-a-surprisingly-short-period-of-time/) ; Backloggd user reviews returned by search, several users, not individually traced (https://backloggd.com/u/Osiera/reviews/)
- **AWAKEN:** A cheap way to double a world is to reuse its own map in reverse. A cave layer that mirrors the surface teaches the player to read it. Lack of content per area is the risk, so put at least one authored thing in each large cave region.

### G12. Zonaite and Zonai charges: ore as a currency for toys
- **CLAIM:** Zonai devices come from capsules out of device dispensers (about 18, most in the sky, two on the surface, one in the Depths). Dispensers take Zonai charges or construct horns. Charges come from defeating constructs and from trading Zonaite mined in the Depths; the guide says an abandoned-mine construct sells charges for 3 Zonaite apiece. A tutorial teaches mining Zonaite and exchanging it. **[P]**
- **SOURCE:** https://nintendolife.com/guides/zelda-tears-of-the-kingdom-all-zonai-devices-how-to-use-where-to-get-capsules ; https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Zonai_Charge ; https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Device_Dispenser
- **AWAKEN:** This is the cleanest example of "ore with purpose" that is not a tool tier. Ore buys the thing the movement system plays with. A grapple's upgrades, a wall-run module or a rope's length could be bought with a cave-only ore.

# 3. Red Dead Redemption 2 (Rockstar, 2018)

### G13. The camp: contribution, visible growth, moods you can hear
- **CLAIM:** A portion of loot goes to the camp as donations. Alcohol found in the world lifts morale. Tents upgrade and unlock features (Dutch's tent unlocks fast travel). Good morale means the gang sings. Upkeep is optional but you miss rewards if you skip it. NPCs follow routines, and reactions depend on how you look and what the NPC is (a sheriff and a farmer differ). **[P]** (hands-on previews, GamingBolt, 23 Sep 2018). EGM's five-star review says contributions upgrade the camp, growth is a visible measure of progress, and upgrades improve residents' moods in their conversations. Characters remember favours and wrongs. **[V]** The camp keeps relocating as the story moves.
- **SOURCE:** Shubhankar Parijat, GamingBolt (https://gamingbolt.com/red-dead-redemption-2-info-blowout-camps-gunplay-npcs-ai-and-more) ; Nick Plessas, EGM (https://egmnow.com/red-dead-redemption-2-review/)
- **AWAKEN:** The camp reacts to what the player brings and shows it in speech, not in a menu. Copy the shape: a share of loot flows in automatically, moods are heard in dialogue, and the upgrades are visible.

### G14. Strangers and towns
- **CLAIM:** Game Informer (Matt Bertz, 25 Oct 2018, 10/10) says new characters keep turning up with side missions, remote properties often hold memorable residents, towns have distinct personalities (Saint Denis the highlight), and civilians rarely repeat dialogue. Restricted fast travel is why an 80-plus-hour story rarely feels repetitive. **[V]**
- **FLUFF RISK:** The same review says violence dominates nearly every mission, perhaps to a fault. EGM says the activity list is long enough to be almost overwhelming and travel can feel dull.
- **SOURCE:** https://gameinformer.com/review/red-dead-redemption-ii/an-open-world-western-for-the-ages ; https://egmnow.com/red-dead-redemption-2-review/ ; for hand-made over procedural see progression research 16.
- **AWAKEN:** Places with their own residents beat a pool of generic quests. Restricted travel makes each road a story.

### G15. The chores critique
- **CLAIM:** Joe Veix (The Outline, 28 Feb 2019) argues RDR2 is mostly tedious mandatory chores: bathing, feeding and brushing the horse, shopping, long rides with exposition. Rewards for chores are minimal, while mission payouts are so generous that nothing feels scarce or earned. He holds up Stardew Valley and Fortnite as games where chores carry real incentives. **[V]**
- **SOURCE:** https://theoutline.com/post/7118/red-dead-redemption-2-is-a-game-about-a-cowboy-who-does-chores
- **AWAKEN:** A chore earns its place only if it pays something the player wants and cannot buy. Scarcity is part of the pay.

# 4. Kingdom Come: Deliverance II (Warhorse, 2025)

### G16. Villages, trades and the smithing that you do with your hands
- **CLAIM:** The Sixth Axis (Gareth Chadwick, 3 Feb 2025, 8/10): villages around Trosky are convincing next to the city of Kuttenberg; trades like blacksmithing and odd jobs build standing; smithing means heating metal and shaping a blade, which felt more immersive than a quick-time event, though hammer feedback is unclear. Crowds gather at the stocks to hear a criminal's charges. NPCs react inconsistently, and can get stuck or circle. Kuttenberg felt busy but under-populated for its size. **[V]**
- **FLUFF RISK:** Steam threads call smithing and alchemy slow and time-wasting, and some players call Kuttenberg empty with few quests in villages. Those are single-player opinions. **[U]**
- **SOURCE:** https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2025/02/03/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-review/ ; Steam: https://steamcommunity.com/app/1771300/discussions/0/591763033886355550
- **AWAKEN:** A craft you do in several visible steps can carry a whole job. Keep each step short and let the player skip to a result after the first few runs.

### G17. Quests that fail forward, a world on a clock
- **CLAIM:** Warhorse's PR manager Tobias Stolz-Zwilling says side quests focus on everyday life and are often humorous (ComicBook.com, 12 Feb 2025). **[V]** Earlier (Checkpoint, Dec 2024) he is reported saying side quests can fail, be done several ways and vary a lot; there is no reload-on-fail, so a failure plays the quest differently; NPCs have a daily cycle; quests are timed, and a character leaves without you if you miss the departure; the main quest is always solvable. **[P]** (page blocked, excerpt.) Tom's Guide found quest design the game's strongest part but said there might be too many (excerpt). **[P]**
- **SOURCE:** https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-rpg-interview/ ; https://checkpointgaming.net/features/2024/12/talking-kingdom-come-deliverance-2-questing-with-warhorse-studios/ ; https://www.tomsguide.com/gaming/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-review
- **AWAKEN:** Event chains in a village can fail forward. A missed festival, a lost caravan or a raid ends in a different place, not in a reload. This is also how a place changes for good without a scripted win.

# 5. Valheim (Iron Gate)

### G18. Comfort: the bed, the fire and the roof read as a number
- **CLAIM:** Sleep in a bed or spend about 20 seconds near comforts to gain Rested. Rested lasts at least 7 minutes plus about 1 minute per comfort point, and gives +50% health regen, +100% stamina regen, +50% experience gain. Comfort sources: shelter (a roof, walls around most of the space and a fire) counts 2; a hearth 2, campfire 1; beds 1 or 2; rugs 1 each; table 1; chair 2; a banner 1. Each category counts only its best item, so ten rugs add nothing. The best layouts reach 18, about 25 minutes. **[P]** (a fan guide; other guides give slightly different timings.) Smoke needs a path out of the building, and exposed fire is put out by rain.
- **FLUFF RISK:** I found no critic who calls comfort fluff. The risk is the opposite: the buff is small, so players build for looks and ignore the number.
- **SOURCE:** https://www.corrosionhour.com/valheim-how-does-comfort-work/ ; https://games.gg/valheim/guides/valheim-comfort-guide/ ; for the portal rule see progression research 30.
- **AWAKEN:** This is the model for the Hearth. A short, public list. A cap per category. A reward you feel (a buff, a shop discount) and a roof that matters. Make the cap so clear that stacking does nothing.

### G19. Ore tiers behind biomes and bosses: the "why mine" loop
- **CLAIM:** Jeff Vogel's design deep dive: five bosses, each opening a new metal; metal bars gate the next gear; portals work only to places you have already walked to; ore and bars cannot go through them, so you mine at the place and haul it home. He defends this as the source of the epic feel. **[P]** (GameBanshee excerpt, undated.) Metals by biome: copper and tin in the Black Forest, iron in the Swamp, silver in the Mountains, black metal in the Plains, carapace in the Mistlands, flametal in the Ashlands. **[P]** (supercraft.host guide.)
- **SOURCE:** https://www.gamebanshee.com/ah99q (page blocked, excerpt) ; https://supercraft.host/article/valheim-deep-north-guide-2026/ ; see progression research 30.
- **AWAKEN:** One metal per place, and a reason to bring it home. Let the Hearth be where the haul turns into something, so the base is the destination of the ore.

### G20. Valheim 1.0 added a grapple, and filled the empty biomes
- **CLAIM:** Valheim reached 1.0 by early Sep 2026. Iron Gate's Lisa Kolfjord and Andreas Tomasson said earlier regions were mostly empty, and the newer ones now carry much more: two dungeons, several enemies, new armour and weapons. A new grappling hook arrives earlier in the game to help with vertical terrain. Structural integrity and ventilation are part of the building rules. **[V]**
- **SOURCE:** Dean Takahashi, GamesBeat, 8 Sep 2026 (https://gamesbeat.com/valheim-finally-hits-1-0-after-five-years-in-early-access-interview/)
- **AWAKEN:** A shipped Minecraft-adjacent survival game chose to give the player a grapple early and then spent effort on content density. The rope does not replace content. It raises the cost of leaving areas empty.

### G21. Taming as a reason to build
- **CLAIM:** A tamed animal needs its preferred food dropped nearby and the player to stay close until a meter fills, roughly three food items over about 30 minutes for a boar. Pens come first. Wolves can be told to guard or follow into fights. Offspring inherit star ratings, so breeding is a long game. Lox and some others can be ridden. **[P]** (guides)
- **SOURCE:** https://windowscentral.com/valheim-animal ; https://www.pcgamesn.com/valheim/animal-taming-breeding
- **AWAKEN:** Taming gives a base a living purpose (a pen, a feeding spot, a guard). If AWAKEN adds taming, tie each animal's value to a base feature.

# 6. Enshrouded (Keen Games)

### G22. The Flame Altar ladder: survivors, ore and a boss at every step
- **CLAIM:** To raise the Flame Altar's level the player needs survivors in sheltered beds (0 at level 2; 2 at level 3; 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, and 18 at level 11), a boss trophy from level 3 on, and a pile of materials that climbs with the world: copper ore at level 4, tin at 5, iron at 6, silver bars at 8, gold ore at 9. Each level opens a region (Springlands at 2, Revelwood at 3, Nomad Highlands at 4, and so on to Cinder's Cradle at 10), raises the max number of altars (from 2 to 16), and lengthens Shroud time at base (6 minutes at level 2, up to 13 at level 9). Level 11 lets altars push back the Shroud. Altar build zones grow from 40 cubed to 160 cubed with upgrade cores. **[P]** (guide table, updated 21 Oct 2026.)
- **SOURCE:** https://allthings.how/enshrouded-1-0-every-flame-altar-upgrade-requirement-flame-levels-1-11/ (1.0 released 15 Oct 2026)
- **AWAKEN:** This is a base ladder that combines three things the player must do: house people, kill a boss, mine a metal. It also ties each rung to a new place to explore. A Hearth level could work the same way: shelter, a gate clear, and a cave ore.

### G23. Survivors have jobs and the base grows into a settlement
- **CLAIM:** CGMagazine (Eduard Gafton, 15 Oct 2026, 8.5) says rescued survivors settle in the camp, take jobs like alchemist or blacksmith, and give quests; the reviewer calls watching a hut become a settlement among the best moments. Crafting is more novel than other systems but tedious: a small starting inventory, and items cannot be dropped for later. **[V]** Guides add that rescued craftspeople (blacksmith, alchemist, carpenter) unlock advanced stations, with each station family tied to one NPC. **[P]**
- **SOURCE:** https://www.cgmagonline.com/?p=1179452 ; https://xgamingserver.com/blog/?p=22184 (guide, low reliability)
- **AWAKEN:** The pleasure is seeing a hut become a place. Station unlocks tied to rescued people make each rescue a visible change to the base.

### G24. Early-access notes on building and the Shroud
- **CLAIM:** RPG Site (Junior Miyai, 25 Jan 2024, 8/10): base building works but needs quality of life, such as build menus that cannot draw from nearby containers. While enshrouded you cannot fast travel or build, so the Shroud is a timed, rule-based wall. Nearly any visible place can be reached with grapple and glider. **[V]** IGN's early-access review (7/10, via OpenCritic) praises the mix of Valheim-style survival and BotW-style exploration. **[P]**
- **SOURCE:** https://www.rpgsite.net/review/15375-enshrouded-review ; https://opencritic.com/game/16119/-/reviews
- **AWAKEN:** The Shroud shows a no-build rule done as a consistent in-world law, which supports progression research 30. A pull-from-nearby-containers crafting rule is the cheapest QoL win for any base.

# 7. Terraria (Re-Logic)

### G25. Housing: a rule list the game checks every time
- **CLAIM:** A valid house needs: a closed frame of solid blocks, platforms or doors; total size at least 60 and under 750 tiles; minimum interior rectangles such as 6x6 or 5x7 (8x8 or 7x9 with the frame); a light source, a table, a chair and a door (each from a long list of qualifying items); no run of 5 empty tiles; a solid tile or safe background wall within 2 tiles of every tile; and an evil-biome score under 300. It does not have to be rectangular. Walls matter: with no empty wall space the house can only be assigned by hand. An NPC moves into a random empty valid house. Damage that breaks validity makes the NPC leave, and they wait one minute before looking again. They never spawn into a house visible on screen. **[V]**
- **SOURCE:** https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Housing (based on the 1.4.5.8 source code)
- **AWAKEN:** This is the clearest "reads the player's own building" rule in games. The checklist is short, the room can be any shape, and the check is run on the player's own blocks. The weakness is that the player must learn the list from the wiki unless the game says what a room lacks.

### G26. Town NPCs arrive because of what the player did
- **CLAIM:** Guide is there from the start. Merchant: all players together hold more than 50 silver. Nurse: a player has more than 100 health and the Merchant lives there. Demolitionist: an explosive in the inventory. Dye Trader: a dye or dye ingredient. Angler: found in an ocean. Zoologist: 10% of the bestiary (55 entries). Dryad: after Eye of Cthulhu, Eater of Worlds, Brain of Cthulhu or Skeletron. Arms Dealer: bullets or a gun in the inventory. Tavernkeep: found after an evil boss. 26 town NPCs in all (18 before Hardmode). **[V]**
- **SOURCE:** https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Town_NPCs
- **AWAKEN:** The arrival condition is the clue. The Merchant comes for money, the Nurse for health, the Demolitionist for a bomb. A village could send a specialist when the player's pack or deeds show a need.

### G27. Happiness: biome, neighbours and a price you can see
- **CLAIM:** Players open the Happiness option in dialogue and the NPC lists what pleases or bothers them, but not what to change. Vendors show a face icon and an exact price modifier. Rules: each NPC likes and dislikes biomes and neighbours. Neighbours count within 25 tiles of a home tile. A solitude bonus applies with at most two others within 25 tiles. Each extra neighbour past three adds a crowding penalty. Prices: loved biome 88% buy and 114% sell; liked 94% and 106%; disliked 106% and 94%; hated 112% and 89%; crowding penalty 105% buy per extra NPC. Modifiers multiply. Range: happy NPCs reach 75% buy and 133% sell, unhappy 150% and 67%. The worst prices apply if homeless, too far from home, or in the Dungeon, Corruption or Crimson. **[V]**
- **FLUFF RISK:** The panel names factors but not remedies, so players go to the wiki.
- **SOURCE:** https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/NPC_happiness
- **AWAKEN:** A numeric reward you can see in a shop is the most legible payoff for a good base. Print the cause and the fix in the dialogue ("wants quiet neighbours", "prefers the hills").

### G28. Events: announced, scaled, one at a time, and they leave something
- **CLAIM:** Blood Moon: 1 in 9 chance on a night when a player has more than 120 health; runs 7:30 PM to 4:30 AM. Goblin Army: 1 in 3 each dawn after a shadow orb or crimson heart is smashed (and a player has over 200 health); announced by message from the west or east; kill count 80 plus 40 per player; the Goblin Tinkerer then appears underground. Pirate Invasion: kill count 120 plus 60 per player. Slime Rain: 150 slimes, 75 if King Slime was killed. Invasions only spawn enemies if the player is within 67.5 tiles below the surface and 187.5 horizontally; only one invasion runs at a time. Lantern Night follows the first defeat of a boss or invasion, and the Party Girl sells lanterns. Party events start randomly by day. **[V]**
- **SOURCE:** https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Events
- **AWAKEN:** An event is a message, a number to beat, a limit of one at a time and a leftover (a new NPC, a lantern night). That leftover is what turns an event into a change to a place.

### G29. Ore behind a number: pickaxe power
- **CLAIM:** A tile can only be broken if the pickaxe's power meets its minimum. Meteorite 50%. Demonite and crimtane ore 55% below the surface. Hellstone 65%. Cobalt and palladium 100%. Mythril and orichalcum 110%. Adamantite and titanium 150%. Chlorophyte 200%. Lihzahrd brick 210%. The maximum is 230% with a late drill. Hardmode ore is a ladder: smash altars with a hammer from the Wall of Flesh, mine cobalt or palladium, craft the next pickaxe, mine mythril or orichalcum, craft again for adamantite or titanium, then upgrade a forge. Hellstone yields molten armour, and the Wall of Flesh is summoned in the Underworld. Dungeon bricks below the surface need 100% power. **[V]**
- **SOURCE:** https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Pickaxe_power ; https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Progression
- **AWAKEN:** This is the reference for "ore with purpose". A visible tier on the tool, a visible tier on the block, and a rule the player learns by trying. A cave section can be locked by block hardness without a quest marker.

# 8. Minecraft itself, and what mods add

### G30. Vanilla villagers already run on a clock and claim your things
- **CLAIM:** Java Edition: employed villagers start work at 08:00 (tick 2000), gather at a bell at 15:00 (tick 9000) and sleep at 18:00 (tick 12000). Unemployed villagers search a 48-block sphere for unclaimed job sites. Trades have five tiers, unlocked by trading. Reputation comes from gossip: each trade adds 4, curing a zombie villager adds 20 (times 5), attacking adds a penalty, killing nearby a larger one; iron golems not built by players turn on a player at -100. Breeding needs 12 food points and a free reachable bed within 48 blocks. **[V]**
- **SOURCE:** https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager
- **AWAKEN:** The skeleton for living villages is already in the base game. A day clock, a job block that creates a job, a bed that creates a resident. A Hearth built on this reads any base that has beds and workstations, with no new block.

### G31. A village is a set of claimed points, and raids end with a change
- **CLAIM:** In Java, any subchunk that holds a claimed point of interest (bed, job site or bell) counts as a village. A raid is 3, 5 or 7 waves by difficulty; Raid Omen II or higher adds a wave. If all villagers die or every claimed bed, bell and job block is destroyed, the raid is lost. A win ends with fireworks, villagers throwing gifts, and Hero of the Village (trade discounts). Raids expire after 48,000 ticks (40 minutes). **[V]**
- **SOURCE:** https://minecraft.wiki/w/Raid
- **AWAKEN:** The game already treats a player-made cluster of beds and workstations as a village. A Hearth that counts the same points reads the player's build with no new rule, and a win-state that pays (discounts, a gift) is a ready model for an event that changes a place.

### G32. Caves and ore in 1.18
- **CLAIM:** Part II of Caves and Cliffs overhauled cave generation into cheese caves (large caverns), spaghetti (long tunnels) and noodle (narrow winding), with aquifers giving local water and lava levels, and cave biomes (lush, dripstone) underground. Each ore now peaks in frequency at a different elevation, and large ore veins were added (granite and copper above Y 0, tuff and iron below). The world gained 64 blocks of height and 64 of depth. **[P]** Mojang's own article says ore placement was retuned over months using community feedback. **[P]**
- **SOURCE:** https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition_guides/Caves_%26_Cliffs:_Part_II ; https://www.minecraft.net/article/caves---cliffs-part-ii-the-features (could not be opened; from search excerpt) ; https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/4415128577293 (blocked)
- **AWAKEN:** Vanilla caves are tall and big. They were designed to look good, not to be run through. A movement-first cave pass would add rooms made for wall runs and rope swings inside the existing three shapes.

### G33. MineColonies: the large precedent for jobs in a Minecraft village
- **CLAIM:** A colony mod with about 89.7 million downloads (supports NeoForge 1.21.1, last file 4 Oct 2026) lists 51 buildings, 45 worker types, 9 systems (Requests, Raids, Research, Colony Border among them) and 4 needs (Food, Gear, Happiness, Sleep). Jobs include builders, farmers, miners, fishers, foresters, smelters, bakers, couriers, guards. Over 10,000 schematics. **[V]** (CurseForge and the official wiki index.) Third-party guides say a Town Hall level caps buildings and citizens, and that colonists arrive when there are beds, open jobs and enough happiness. **[P]**
- **SOURCE:** https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/minecolonies ; https://minecolonies.com/wiki/ ; guides: https://cyberpost.co/what-is-the-max-number-of-citizens-in-minecolonies/
- **AWAKEN:** Proof that a large Minecraft audience wants citizens with jobs. Its weight is also the lesson: 45 worker types is a game of its own. AWAKEN should take its short list of needs and its Town Hall ceiling, and leave the rest.

### G34. Palworld: workers with a suitability, assigned to base jobs
- **CLAIM:** Palworld has 12 work suitabilities (cooling, electricity, farming, gathering, handiwork, kindling, lumbering, medicine production, mining, planting, transporting, watering). Each creature has a suitability level around 1 to 4, and a higher mining level unlocks rarer materials such as coal, sulfur and pure quartz. A monitoring stand lets the player direct jobs. Work-speed structures give up to a 20% efficiency boost. **[P]**
- **SOURCE:** https://palworld.wiki.gg/wiki/Work_suitability ; https://www.denofgeek.com/games/palworld-best-base-pals-for-every-work-suitability
- **AWAKEN:** A base worker's job is a short list of skills with levels, and a higher level opens rarer output. That ties a worker to ore, which is a more interesting link than "worker adds a flat bonus".

# 9. Rooms that read the player's building (beyond Terraria and Valheim)

### G35. Dragon Quest Builders 2: a room panel that tells you what it is
- **CLAIM:** A room needs walls at least two blocks high, a door and a light. Each villager prefers a size (1 to 5 hearts: tiny 4 to 15 open blocks, small 16 to 35, normal 36 to 63, large 64 to 99, enormous 100 to 150; over 150 is not a room), a fanciness (1 to 5 hearts, gold items raise it most) and an ambience (normal, cute, cool, natural, flamboyant, cheeky) set by floors, walls, decorations and dye. Press a button inside any room to see size, fanciness and an ambience pie chart. Matching five rooms to their residents' preferences unlocks the Emblematic Clock recipe. **[V]** In the first game, rooms gave points towards town level. **[P]**
- **SOURCE:** https://dragon-quest.org/wiki/Room_preference ; https://dragon-quest.org/wiki/List_of_room_recipes_in_Dragon_Quest_Builders_2 ; Gematsu on the first game: https://gematsu.com/2015/10/dragon-quest-builders-details-town-experience
- **AWAKEN:** The panel is the key idea. The player builds, presses one key, and the room says what it is and what it lacks. Resident preferences give players a design brief without a quest.

### G36. Animal Crossing: New Horizons: an island rating from the whole layout
- **CLAIM:** Isabelle rates the island in two scores (Development or "Life", and Scenery or "Nature"). Datamining says 5 stars needs at least 665 and 450 respectively. Trees give 1 point each up to 190 points, more than 220 trees blocks 5 stars, and more than 15 loose items or a messy map counts against it. The score uses 8x8 grids. Guides say 8 to 10 villagers and open shops count. Nintendo has not published the formula. **[P]**
- **FLUFF RISK:** Because the formula is hidden, players learn it from datamining, and it ends in a spreadsheet.
- **SOURCE:** https://www.thegamer.com/dataminer-discovers-how-to-get-5-star-island-rating-animal-crossing-new-horizons/ ; https://nintendolife.com/guides/animal-crossing-new-horizons-how-to-get-a-5-star-island-rating
- **AWAKEN:** A whole-area rating works, but hide nothing. Let the player ask for it in dialogue and hear which part is weak.

### G37. RimWorld: rooms are scored on five stats
- **CLAIM:** Rooms have Impressiveness (derived), Wealth, Space, Beauty and Cleanliness. Roles come from what is inside (a bed makes a bedroom; a barracks has several). Colonists get a mood thought such as the impressive-dining-room one only when they use the room for its purpose, and it lasts 24 hours. A 2016 forum post gives a formula that weights the weakest stat, so one filthy corner drags the whole score down. **[P]** (the formula is **[U]** for current versions.)
- **SOURCE:** https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Room (blocked, excerpt) ; https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Impressiveness
- **AWAKEN:** "The weakest stat counts most" turns a base into something to tidy, not to stack. The "only when used" rule keeps the reward tied to a person living there.

### G38. Dwarf Fortress: nobles demand rooms, and arrive when you are rich enough
- **CLAIM:** Each noble title asks for rooms of a set quality: a baron wants 4 (decent) each for quarters, dining and office plus a tomb; a count 6 (great); a duke 7 (grand); a monarch 8 (royal). Mandates are binding and unmet ones make the noble upset; jealous nobles object to lower-ranked dwarves having better rooms. Landholders arrive on thresholds: a baron at population 20, created wealth 100k and exported wealth 10k; a count at 200k and 20k; a duke at 300k and 30k. A mayor needs population 50. **[V]**
- **SOURCE:** https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Noble
- **AWAKEN:** A notable arrives because the base is big enough or rich enough, and then asks something of it. That is a clean model for "a Registry inspector arrives once your Hearth passes a threshold, and asks for an office". Impossible demands are the over-reach to avoid.

# 10. Stardew Valley (ConcernedApe)

### G39. Festivals: the town closes on purpose
- **CLAIM:** 12 festivals a year: 3 in spring, 3 in summer, 2 in fall and 4 in winter. On most, homes and shops are locked all day (even if you do not attend), time stops, and you are returned to the farm late at night; animals are fed automatically. A few (Desert Festival, Trout Derby, SquidFest, Night Market) leave shops open and time running. ConcernedApe's 2014 dev post says festivals mark the seasons, bring the community together and offer shopping booths, games and a chance to show romantic interest; the Luau has the community add ingredients to a potluck soup. **[V]**
- **SOURCE:** https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Festivals ; ConcernedApe, 21 May 2014 (https://www.stardewvalley.net/?p=859)
- **AWAKEN:** A festival is a calendar rhythm that people plan around, and the world visibly changes (closed doors). Its pay is social and cosmetic, with a small mechanical reward.

### G40. The Community Center: a restored building changes the valley
- **CLAIM:** The Community Center is a ruin the player restores by filling bundles: 6 rooms and 30 bundles. Finished rooms unlock town features (the greenhouse, the bus). The Joja route instead buys each upgrade: 5,000 gold for membership and about 135,000 gold for the projects. The two routes are exclusive in one save. The bundle route is season-driven and teaches the player a range of skills from mining to farming; the Joja route is faster but loses some charm, such as the theatre. **[P]**
- **SOURCE:** https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Bundles (could not be opened; from search excerpt) ; https://www.holy.gg/en/post/stardew-valley-jojamart-or-the-community-center
- **AWAKEN:** A place that changes for good and offers two moral routes (community or corporation) is rare and loved. A village could be restored by a bundle of local goods or bought out for coin, and the choice shows.

### G41. Villagers: a wide cast that moves with time and weather
- **CLAIM:** 46 villagers: 12 marriage candidates, 22 other giftable villagers and 12 you cannot give gifts to. Each has a routine that changes with the time and weather, and likes and dislikes of their own. **[V]**
- **FLUFF RISK:** Barone has said some fans call the game a chore simulator **[P]**, and The Outline holds Stardew up as the opposite: chores that carry real incentives (see G15).
- **SOURCE:** https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Villagers ; NPR via WGBH, 24 Jan 2025 (https://www.wgbh.org/culture/2025-01-24/the-legacy-and-future-of-the-farming-game-stardew-valley, blocked, excerpt)
- **AWAKEN:** A cast of 12 deep people and 22 lighter ones is a ratio worth copying. Few people get arcs, and many get a routine and a gift taste.

# 11. Towns that change: Mount & Blade, Kenshi, Dwarf Fortress

### G42. Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord: villages feed towns, raids hurt
- **CLAIM:** Villages supply food to their bound town. A bigger rural population raises the town's prosperity cap and a raided village loses people, so the cap falls and recovery is slow. Hearths are the village's size measure and grow roughly one a day. A TaleWorlds developer post on hostile actions says raided goods move to the raider over time and the village needs time to recover, raiding a village of a kingdom you are not at war with counts as a crime, and AI lords cannot all be pulled to a raid or the rest of the kingdom would be left open. **[P]** The player-side claims come from forum posts and are patch-dependent. **[U]**
- **SOURCE:** TaleWorlds dev blog, "Actions and Consequences" (mirror: https://gamebanshee.com/khpcr, blocked, excerpt) ; Steam threads, e.g. https://steamcommunity.com/app/261550/discussions/0/2265817650398267153
- **AWAKEN:** A village that can be hurt and then heals slowly gives players a reason to protect it. Show the hurt (fewer people, shut shops), and the recovery on a clock.

### G43. Kenshi: the world moves without you
- **CLAIM:** The Register (31 May 2019) says the world goes on with or without the player and compares it to Mount & Blade: Warband. Factions fight and attacking one makes you its enemy for a while (fan wiki). A modder says removing a town's leader lets another faction take over, which hints the base game's takeover is narrow. I found no developer interview about town takeovers. **[U]**
- **SOURCE:** https://www.theregister.co.uk/2019/05/31/kenshi_sandblasted_swordpunk_where_the_dungeon_master_wants_everyone_dead/ ; https://kenshi.fandom.com/wiki/Faction
- **AWAKEN:** No verified design lesson beyond "the world keeps going". Do not cite Kenshi for mechanism.

### G44. Dwarf Fortress: stories come from actors with motives
- **CLAIM:** In a 2008 Game Developer interview John Harris reports Tarn Adams building from basic elements and rules, writing stories in a notebook with his brother and then picking low-hanging systems to implement. His summary of the method: create actors with motivations and let them go. Players fill gaps with their own narrative; the Boatmurdered tale is the example. **[V]** In a 2026 PC Gamer interview he says he judges a simulation layer by whether, having played it, he can see a story take shape (excerpt). **[P]**
- **SOURCE:** John Harris, Game Developer (Gamasutra), 27 Feb 2008 (https://gamedeveloper.com/design/interview-the-making-of-dwarf-fortress) ; PC Gamer, 2026 (https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/game-development/dwarf-fortress-creator-says-we-have-a-natural-lean-towards-human-made-stories-but-but-were-all-going-to-be-very-sorely-tested-in-the-coming-years-by-ai/, body truncated)
- **AWAKEN:** A story event needs a person with a want and a rule that connects it to the player's base. "A trader who wants a bridge" is better than "a quest that says bring bridge".

# 12. Caves as places of movement and discovery

### G45. Deep Rock Galactic: hand-built rooms, strung like beads, tested with every class
- **CLAIM:** On mission select the game makes a seed that fixes the cave shape, mineral positions and enemies. Designers hand-build rooms from intersecting spheres, half-spheres and planes. Each template has random points that may or may not switch on, so one template yields many rooms. The generator joins rooms with narrow winding tunnels. Mission type can warp, mirror or overlap templates, and biomes add surface noise and debris. Designer Anders Heindorff Frederiksen tests each room with every class, since randomness can change how playable it is. The goal is rooms that invite traversal without being mazes. Numbers: 119 hand-built rooms, 10 biomes, about 150,000 new cave systems a day on Steam, over half a billion since 2018. **[V]** (A mirror of the studio's Steam post; the page itself is undated, tagged 2024.)
- **SOURCE:** GSG_Aaron for Ghost Ship Games (https://devtrackers.gg/deep-rock-galactic/p/8cd5ca9c-below-decks-at-ghost-ship-cave-generation-in-deep-rock-galactic) ; CEO Soren Lundgaard on mixing hand-crafted parts with code: https://gamingbolt.com/deep-rock-galactic-interview-heigh-ho-off-to-plunder-we-go/amp
- **AWAKEN:** The recipe: a modest library of authored rooms, a generator that strings them, and a test with each movement kit (wall run, slide, rope, climb). If a room only works for one move, the generator should know.

### G46. Spelunky: a guaranteed path without tools, extra paths with them
- **CLAIM:** Per Mark Brown's video, based on Derek Yu's book, levels are half designed and half generated. Yu built templates for roles: rooms you drop into, rooms you land in, corridors, and rooms off the critical path. A guaranteed path runs from entrance to exit and can always be finished without ropes or bombs. A separate pass places objects: gems and crates favour walled-in spaces, enemies avoid cramped spaces. Yu admits players will learn repeating landmarks and sense the grid, and argues that playable levels matter more than realism. **[P]** (video transcript not openable, excerpt; the wiki-style summary of the path rule came from a search result.)
- **SOURCE:** Game Maker's Toolkit, "How (and Why) Spelunky Makes its Own Levels" (date not verified; amara transcript: https://amara.org/subtitles/p6MHlmreohsY/en-gb/1/download/How%20and%20Why%20Spelunky%20Makes%20its%20Own%20Levels%20%20Game%20Makers%20Toolkit.en-gb.txt) ; Yu's generator tutorial: https://www.tinysubversions.com/2010/01/tutorial-modding-spelunkys-level-generator/index.html
- **AWAKEN:** A grapple that is required is a lock. A grapple that is optional but pays is a toy. Spelunky settles it with a rule: the main path never needs the tool, and the tool opens treasure and shortcuts.

### G47. Hollow Knight: get lost on purpose, abilities as a light fence
- **CLAIM:** Team Cherry called the map the biggest design challenge. A Metroid-style map that reveals the layout did not suit exploration and an unhelpful one would frustrate, so players buy basic maps from a cartographer and expand them. Abilities lightly keep players in safer areas, but the team avoided steering players directly. Ari Gibson said the aim was disorientation early, with hidden characters, treasures and fights in place of signs. **[V]** (See progression research 22 for the cartographer.)
- **FLUFF RISK:** Deepnest, a cave-like area, was cut down and the lava-filled Boneforest dropped, for schedule reasons.
- **SOURCE:** Game Informer editorial, 15 Oct 2018 (https://gameinformer.com/2018/10/15/the-making-of-hollow-knight)
- **AWAKEN:** Ability gates in caves can be soft (a ledge you can see, a gap you cannot cross yet) and need no sign.

### G48. Sekiro: a grapple rewrites the level
- **CLAIM:** Miyazaki said earlier FromSoftware maps were tall but linked by ladders and stairs, which meant one-way routes, and the grapple and ninja traversal let players move freely through those 3D spaces. The hook serves movement: crossing the map, closing distance and opening combat. Levels were designed heavily around it (Stevivor, via a search excerpt). **[V]** (Game Informer) I could not source a critic who says the grapple made areas skippable; one search summary claimed it and I could not trace it. **[U]**
- **SOURCE:** Game Informer, 21 Aug 2018 (https://www.gameinformer.com/interview/2018/08/21/hidetaka-miyazaki-on-sekiro-shadows-die-twice) ; Stevivor (https://stevivor.com/features/interviews/hidetaka-miyazaki-on-sekiros-level-design-verticality, blocked)
- **AWAKEN:** Adding a rope after the fact is cheating the level. Build caves, villages and towers for it: rooftops, hanging ledges, shafts.

### G49. Elden Ring: caves and catacombs, and the repetition critique
- **CLAIM:** Game Informer (Daniel Tack, 23 Feb 2022, 10/10) says early play is mostly small caves, mines and dungeon delves that grow more complex over time. Some start as two-room layouts and later become teleport puzzles; some end in a boss that feels routine, with a cave troll or magma drake reused. About 100 bosses; the scale of the game makes reuse easier to forgive. Players can skip large areas and still feel they missed plenty after more than 55 hours. **[V]** TrueAchievements (Luke Albigés, Mar 2022) says the underground dungeons are optional side areas with a boss and loot, many follow the same structure, and ending each in a boss wears the appeal. **[P]** Miyazaki (2019) said the open field was added to show the story at scale and give more freedom and variation. **[P]**
- **SOURCE:** https://gameinformer.com/review/elden-ring/absolutely-astonishing-adventure ; https://www.trueachievements.com/n48885/elden-ring-review-xbox (blocked, excerpt) ; Gematsu on the IGN interview: https://gematsu.com/2019/06/elden-ring-is-an-evolution-of-dark-souls-says-director-hidetaka-miyazaki ; producer Kitao on catacombs and legacy dungeons: https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3685838/elden-ring-producer-states-the-game-is-in-the-final-stages-of-development-and-more/
- **AWAKEN:** Small caves sell the sense of constant finds, and they sour when the end is always the same boss. Vary the last room: a rope puzzle, a vista, a trap, a tame animal, not a boss every time.

### G50. Outer Wilds and Noita
- **CLAIM:** Outer Wilds is in progression research 21 (curiosity-only design, no waypoints, a log that organises clues). I found no verified design source for Noita's caves. **[U]**

# 13. Ore that feeds machines

### G51. Satisfactory and Factorio
- **CLAIM:** Satisfactory resource nodes are infinite and come in three purities that multiply output (impure 0.5, normal 1, pure 2), so scarcity comes from distance, power and logistics. Some deposits are hand-mined to unlock research (caterium, sulfur, raw quartz). Uranium sits in guarded places such as crash sites. **[P]** (wiki.gg.) A Factorio forum reply from staff says uranium patches far from the start are intended. **[P]** I found no Wube or Coffee Stain design interview on ore placement. **[U]**
- **SOURCE:** https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Resource_Node ; https://forums.factorio.com/viewtopic.php?p=627378
- **AWAKEN:** Factories make mining matter by giving it a sink. A tier of ore that feeds the Hearth's machines gives mining a long-term demand, and rich rare nodes in guarded caves give a reason to fight.

# 14. Anti-examples and cautions

### G52. Skyrim's Radiant Story: the designer's own cautions
- **CLAIM:** At a 2012 talk to the Vancouver Film School, Bethesda's Bruce Nesmith said the team began by defining 30 player actions and how the world might react (bystanders reacting to a slain dragon, townsfolk fighting over a dropped item). A system that made a vendor accuse the player of theft was cut because it ended with the player killing the NPC. Using generated quests for the main story was rejected as less compelling than hand-written. Some reactions went too far (a whole town gathering when you dropped an item) and needed limits. He said it is easy to go too far. **[V]**
- **SOURCE:** GamesBeat, 27 Jan 2012, on Nesmith's Game Design Expo talk of 21 Jan 2012 (https://gamesbeat.com/bethesdas-nesmith-reflects-on-the-difficult-birth-of-skyrims-radiant-story-system/) ; see progression research 17.
- **AWAKEN:** Systemic reactions need caps, cooldowns and a rule that they cannot punish the player for living in the world.

### G53. Ubisoft-style icon maps
- **CLAIM:** Vikki Blake (NME, 9 Nov 2020) argued that Ubisoft's formula worked well in Assassin's Creed Origins but spread across the company until games felt alike: region liberation, fort captures, fetch quests. Glowing markers motivate but remove discovery and surprise. Repetitive side missions feel like busywork. **[V]** (No developer quotes in the piece.) See progression research 7 and 9 for the icon and tower history.
- **SOURCE:** https://www.nme.com/features/ubisofts-formula-tried-and-true-or-time-to-change-2812353
- **AWAKEN:** Crimson Desert's Re-Blockade (G4) is a modern echo: liberate, hold, repeat. If an activity is "clear the camp", give it a twist or leave it out.

### G54. Fallout 4 settlements
- **CLAIM:** GameBanshee's review calls settlements a fun idea that the engine supports poorly: a building camera that does not zoom out far enough, pieces that do not fit, and a bug where settlers lose happiness while the player is away. The reviewer saw no point in having several. RPG Codex (quoted there) called settlement mode shallow. Rock Paper Shotgun's Jon Morcom wrote a largely positive piece. **[P]**
- **SOURCE:** https://gamebanshee.com/kx66a ; https://gamebanshee.com/kxhkr (both read as search excerpts)
- **AWAKEN:** The failure was a base that decays while you are away and a build tool that fights you. Keep a base stable offline and the tool forgiving.

### G55. Dragon's Dogma 2: travel as the content
- **CLAIM:** Itsuno told IGN that travel is dull only when the game is dull. Fast travel exists but costs a rare item. Events on the road are not scripted: goblins block an ox cart, then a griffin, which tends to attack cows, destroys it. Ox carts are cheap, and that cheapness leads to unplanned outcomes. **[V]** (80.lv, 23 Jan 2024, from an IGN interview.) Push Square and others report players finding overworld encounters repetitive, with fast travel limited to a rare item. **[P]**
- **SOURCE:** Terri Mills, 80.lv (https://80.lv/articles/dragon-s-dogma-2-director-on-why-you-should-fast-travel-less) ; https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2024/03/not-everyone-is-in-love-with-dragons-dogma-2-on-ps5 (blocked, excerpt)
- **AWAKEN:** Systemic roadside stories need a small set of interacting parts (a cart, a blocker, a predator), not a script. They also need variety, or the same goblins ruin the road.

# Principles for AWAKEN

Eighteen rules for living settlements, events that change a place for good, a base that reads the player's building, ore with purpose, caves for movement, and first-time wonder without overload.

1. **Give every settlement a clock the player can read.** Work, gather at a bell, sleep, with the same three beats in every village. *(G30, G2)*
2. **A village is a set of claimed things, not a structure.** Count beds, job blocks and a bell in the player's own build, as vanilla already does. *(G31, G25)*
3. **Arrivals are triggered by a deed, and the rule can be read.** The Merchant comes for 50 silver, a baron for population and wealth. *(G26, G38)*
4. **Show what a room counts and what it lacks, in the room, and cap each category.** One key shows size, style and shortfall, and ten rugs add nothing. *(G35, G18)*
5. **Pay the base now, in a way you can feel.** A visible shop price (75% to 150%), a rested buff, a mood line in speech. *(G27, G18, G13)*
6. **A place changes for good only through a deed the player can name.** Four recruits and some wood grow a town. A bundle restores a hall. A won raid pays a gift. *(G9, G40, G31)*
7. **An event that can undo a place needs real pay and an off switch.** Otherwise it is a chore. *(G4)*
8. **Events announce themselves, scale with the player, run one at a time, and leave something behind.** A message, a count to beat, a new NPC or a gift. *(G28, G31)*
9. **Let the calendar close the town on purpose.** Festival days lock the shops and stop the clock, and players plan around them. *(G39)*
10. **Give ore a visible gate and a reason to carry it home.** A tool power, a base level or a biome sets the gate. The haul turns into something at the Hearth. *(G29, G22, G19)*
11. **Fix gathering with the tool and the pack before adding more ore.** Mark what breaks, cut the swings, add storage and categories. *(G6, G5)*
12. **Side activities must feed something.** Food heals, loot feeds the camp, crops feed a shop. Otherwise they are chores. *(G15, G13, G5)*
13. **Build caves from authored rooms strung together, and test every room with every movement tool.** Wall run, slide, rope, climb. *(G45)*
14. **The route through never needs the rope. The rope buys shortcuts and treasure.** *(G46, G47)*
15. **Add the grapple by building for it.** Hanging ledges, shafts and rooftops, and fill the late areas, because empty space is exposed when the player moves fast. *(G48, G20)*
16. **Let one cave change how the player sees every cliff, and vary the last room.** Not a boss every time. *(G10, G49)*
17. **Open only a few verbs at once.** Wonder survives when new systems arrive one or two at a time. The reference game is faulted for the opposite. *(G7, G2)*
18. **Hand-write the roadside people and let a few systems finish the story.** A cart, a blocker and a predator beat a script. Cap and cool down every reaction. *(G55, G52, G17)*
