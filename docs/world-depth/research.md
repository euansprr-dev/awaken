# Between the Gates: research index

Two research files back this plan, compiled 2026-10-26 by research agents and read in full by the lead designer:

- **`research-games.md`**: 55 entries (G1-G55) on Crimson Desert, BotW and TotK, RDR2, Kingdom Come II, Valheim,
  Enshrouded, Terraria, vanilla Minecraft, MineColonies, Palworld, Dragon Quest Builders 2, Animal Crossing, RimWorld,
  Dwarf Fortress, Stardew Valley, Mount & Blade, Deep Rock Galactic, Spelunky, Hollow Knight, Sekiro, Elden Ring,
  Satisfactory and Factorio, and the anti-examples (Skyrim's Radiant, Ubisoft icon maps, Fallout 4 settlements,
  Dragon's Dogma 2's roads). 18 principles at the end.
- **`research-mods.md`**: 63 entries (M1-M63) on village mods (MineColonies, Millénaire, MCA Reborn, Live Villages,
  Towns and Towers, CTOV, guards, trading, Supplementaries, Farmer's Delight, bounties, roads), Mojang's own villages,
  raids, trade rebalance and the 2026 drops (sulfur caves, 26.3, 26.4's ice caves), mining (Create, Mekanism,
  Tinkers', Silent Gear, prospecting, vein mining, mining as an act, new ores, mining dimensions), caves (Alex's Caves,
  YUNG's, Terralith, Tectonic, Deeper and Darker, Spelunkery), movement mods (ParCool!, grappling hooks, ropes), bases
  that read builds (Villager Comfort, Confluence, Comforts), fishing and taming, and eight packs (Prominence II, BMC4,
  Vault Hunters, RLCraft, Craftoria, Dawncraft, Create: Above and Beyond, Valhelsia). 18 principles at the end.

Each entry carries its source, date and URL, and a tag: **[V]** read on the page, **[P]** partly verified (an excerpt
of a page that blocked fetching, or a fan guide), **[U]** a lead only. Critics are paraphrased; quotes stay under 15
words, at most one per source. Reddit and minecraft.net could not be fetched; where that matters the entry says so.

Guidance and gating research (BotW's pull, Witcher icons, Far Cry's people who tell you, Metroid's locks, Valheim's
no-build rule, Outer Wilds' log) is in `docs/progression/research.md` and is not repeated.
