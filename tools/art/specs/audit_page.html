Sound audit, by ear

Every sound that fell short of the armour and the Forged Pick has been remade from recordings the game already has. Each one here has up to three picks. Pick 1 is what the game plays now. Before is the old sound, so you can hear the change. In its moment plays the sound where the game plays it; Alone plays just the sound. Each press plays a fresh take, as the game would. Press Use this on the one you want, or None of these and say a word.

Every pick is a guess until you have heard it, and the levels are first values. This page plays the recordings from this Mac's own copy of the game; it is not hosted anywhere. Your marks save as you go.
