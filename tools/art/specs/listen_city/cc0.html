CC0 shortlist: what synthesis can't do well

Real recordings licensed Creative Commons 0 (public domain: no credit required, safe in a public repo). Every licence was read on the sound's own Freesound page on 25 Oct 2026. Nothing has been downloaded and nothing will be until you say yes. Then the file goes through our pipeline (trimmed, looped, levelled, mono for places), and its source and licence are written next to it. Nobody has listened to these yet, so each one needs a listen for words, traffic and clipping before it goes in. Two picks per need, best first; the rest are kept in the milestone report.

Left out on purpose: Sonniss GDC bundles and BBC Sound Effects (not CC0); uploads that ask for share-alike or no redistribution (contrary to how we'd ship them); a harbour file that looks like a re-upload of someone else's; and the 1930s-60s studio transfers labelled CC0 by craigsmith, whose right to waive them is unclear.
