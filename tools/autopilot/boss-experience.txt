# The Whole Boss Fight as built (SPEC §68), filmed on the duel stage (/awaken bosses duel stage, SPEC §47.3) in daylight.
# You stand in survival with health enough for the whole film, put back in front of the boss every few seconds (along its
# own facing), and it fights as it does: its own choice of blows, its own aim. A blow played by command (duel play) is
# no measure: it begins between two of the boss's ticks and takes no aim worth the name.
# 1. The ring, slowed to 5 ticks a second, a picture a half tick: clips/ring_struck (the guard never raised),
#    clips/ring_parried (the guard raised in the middle of the band).
# 2. The band against the server: the guard raised on the ring's own clock (arms parry) at the band's early edge, its
#    late edge, its middle, a tick too early and a tick too late, then no guard at all. The log's "[fights] ring:" and
#    "[fights] clock:" lines and the totals are the measure: every guard the ring showed white must come back DEFLECT,
#    and a blow that lands must find the ring at about 0.
# 3. A worn-out blade beside a fresh one in the bag: the crack on its icon.
# 4. The last stand and the finishing blow on the three pilots: the plate's words, a heavy held and let go, the bars,
#    the cut out of your eyes, the white frame, its own ending, the cut back (clips/finish_ringer at half speed, a
#    picture a tick; the Regent and the Colossus as frames), each of those two fought unguarded first for the ring's
#    clock (the Regent's dive is a charge, the Colossus's slam a slam).
