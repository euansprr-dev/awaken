# After Ilse's scene you can move again, and the camp has no dome (Euan, 2026-10-26: "right after the cutscene, you
# can't move"; "a weird dark dome around the camp"). SPEC §27.6, "Control comes back".
# 1. The camp by day, at the fire, looking up into the sky over it: no dome, no facets (the far smoke column fades out
#    within 48 blocks of the fire; vanilla's smoke is the near one).
# 2. Ilse's scene to its end: the ember held out, taken; the scene ends; then walking away (the log says how far).
# 3. The camp at dusk, at the fire, looking up: no dark shell.
# Run: tools/tour-ci.sh tools/autopilot/camp-exit.txt 1500 PACK=perf,world TECTONIC=awakened
world autopilot_campexit survival 20261026
waitworld
window 1440 900
cmd gamerule spawn_mobs false
cmd gamerule awaken:fauna_spawning false
cmd gamerule send_command_feedback false
cmd weather clear 1000000
wait 40
cmd awaken opening skip
wait 160
screen close
cmd awaken powers starter 1
wait 20
screen close
cmd time set 5000
cmd gamerule advance_time false
hud on
camera first
# 1. the camp by day: at the fire, then looking up over it
cmd awaken living camp
wait 240
cmd awaken living first camp
wait 60
shotnow exit_1_camp_day
cmd tp @s ~ ~ ~ ~ -55
wait 20
shotnow exit_1_camp_day_sky
# 2. Ilse's scene to its end, then walk away
cmd awaken living first ilse
wait 60
shotnow exit_2_scene
# she holds the ember out at about 450 ticks in, then walks it over to you
wait 430
shotnow exit_2_offer
wait 80
shotnow exit_2_take_prompt
use
# her last line, then the scene ends (about 180 ticks after your hand closes on it)
wait 260
shotnow exit_2_scene_over
# your own keys again: walk forward for 3 s (the log says where you started and where you got to)
walk 60
wait 70
shotnow exit_2_walked
# 3. the camp at dusk, at the fire, looking up
cmd time set 12800
cmd awaken living first camp
wait 60
shotnow exit_3_camp_dusk
cmd tp @s ~ ~ ~ ~ -55
wait 20
shotnow exit_3_camp_dusk_sky
info
quit
