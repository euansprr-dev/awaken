# Carillon M4 step B3b (animals, the soundscape, the city at night): one measuring tour (shots plus the lantern A/B).
# The same world and settings as B3a's tour (carillon-m4b3a.txt), so the framings pair with b3a_*: the animals at
# noon (pigeons in the bowl and on the square, gulls on the quay, carts and dogs in the Market), the moved laundry lane
# at noon, dusk and night, cats on Bellwatch's roofs with their eyes at night, Choir Square's festoons from dusk, the
# Watch's drawn lanterns, and the lantern A/B: frame times with drawn light (glow) against the old moving light blocks
# (blocks) on the Watch's round (show watch picks a watchman each time), then the light-block writes each made. The log carries "[citizens] animals near you"
# every 30 s and the ambience slot's "status: mixing" lines (the beds really play).
# Run (it measures frame times: the tour slot, alone on the machine):
#   PACK=perf,world,horizon TECTONIC=awakened DH=swift RD=20 SLOT_WHO=M4-B3b \
#   /Volumes/MEDIA/Awaken-carillon/tools/carillon/slot.sh tour tools/autopilot.sh tools/autopilot/carillon-m4b3b.txt 900

shot b3b_noon_canal

wait 200
shot b3b_noon_market_street

wait 240
shot b3b_noon_roofs_cats
cmd awaken city view bellwatch
wait 160
shot b3b_noon_bellwatch

wait 200
shot b3b_noon_tower_flock
