# Carillon M5: the Atlas (SPEC §16.8), one tour per phase (phase 2 adds night lights, cloud shadows and the glance).
# The Choir's bell clears the city, then a walk north out of the High Gate clears a road into the wilds; the table opens over the city by day, orbits, closes in, zooms out to the
# coarser levels, goes to night, plays a bell's ripple over a cleared Bellwatch and the Registry reveal. Short perf
# pairs (closed / open at the same spot) and the '[atlas]' lines carry open time, prepare() cost, mesh and memory.
# Run: PACK=perf,world TECTONIC=awakened RD=16 SLOT_WHO=atlas \
