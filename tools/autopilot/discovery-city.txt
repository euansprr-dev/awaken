# The city is not a discovery (SPEC §26.3b, Euan 2026-10-26): a fresh world, the opening skipped (the Registry done, the
# city is yours), a walk through four districts past the city's animals and citizens, then the K menu: the Journal and
# the Codex's creatures must be empty. Then out through the High Gate into the Commons: the first biome lands there
# (filed to the Journal; a new world's first 5 minutes are quiet, so it is filed, not toasted) and K shows it.
# Run on CI: tools/tour-ci.sh tools/autopilot/discovery-city.txt 1200 PACK=perf,world TECTONIC=awakened
world discoveryCity survival 20261024
waitworld
gui 0
window 1440 900
info
cmd gamerule send_command_feedback false
cmd weather clear 1000000
cmd time set 4000
cmd awaken opening skip
wait 200
cmd awaken powers starter 1
wait 20
screen close
# ---- the city: four districts, a while in each (biomes are checked every 2 s, sightings 4 times a second)
cmd awaken city view square
wait 120
cmd awaken city view market
wait 120
cmd awaken city view garden
wait 120
cmd awaken city view quay
wait 120
shot 01_city_street
screen status
wait 30
# K in the city after the Registry: no Journal, no "New biome", no creatures
shot 02_k_status_in_city
scroll -6
wait 10
shot 03_k_status_in_city_lower
screen codex
wait 30
shot 04_k_codex_in_city
screen close
wait 10
# ---- out through the High Gate into the Commons
cmd awaken city view studio_high_gate
wait 200
shot 05_outside_high_gate
# filed after the director's wait (the world is still in its quiet first minutes)
wait 1000
screen status
wait 30
shot 06_k_status_outside
screen codex
wait 30
shot 07_k_codex_outside
screen close
wait 10
quit
