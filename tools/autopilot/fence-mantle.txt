# Fences, gates and walls (SPEC §17.27), one tour (run with PACK=feel, HUD on). A lane on a flat floor: an oak fence
# vaulted running and hopped walking, a fence gate, a nether brick fence, a cobblestone wall vaulted, a fence on a block
# (a garden wall) climbed over, a long mossy wall climbed onto and walked along its top, a spruce fence over a drop
# climbed onto and held, a fence at a roof's edge hung from and climbed over; one running vault and the garden wall
# again in third person. Then Carillon: the nearest real fence or wall to a
# street, run at. Every move is filmed by the pilot's clip recorder (the pilot's keys and the recorder start together:
# a command reaches the client a few ticks late; the recorder goes first), full size; tools/ascent/clip.py makes each a
