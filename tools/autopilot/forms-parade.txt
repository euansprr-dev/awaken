# The forms (SPEC 21.11): a species at every rung in a row, rung 0 (vanilla) to 5, 7 blocks ahead on a flat floor, from the front and from
# three quarters; the top rung close from the front and three quarters (pig, cow, zombie, skeleton); then the same pig and zombie in the three
# families (green, sun, frost) and at night for the glow. `cmd awaken living formrow <species> [family]` stands them still, facing you; undead
# are shot in a thunderstorm (the sky is dark enough that they do not burn). Look for: each rung visibly more dangerous than the last; every piece attached to the body where it
# belongs and none over a face (tools/art/forms_geom.py checks this when the art is built); the glow at the eyes and in the veins.
