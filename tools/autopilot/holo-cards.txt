# The System's cards in the projected look (docs/modules/holo.md "System cards"): the first-power choice scanning in,
# open and picked; a System toast scanning in, at rest over the sky and over grass, and glitching out; a real gate
# panel (not a toast: no mobs, no choice up) put away with N and glitching out; the choice card scanning in and open;
# a story line; the toast and the choice card at night; then the same flat ("holo style flat") for comparison.
# A "shot" lands about 3 ticks after its line and the next line waits 10 more; "shotnow" shoots this tick and waits 1;
# a "cmd" waits 5. Under the autopilot a picked first power stays on screen 12 ticks. GUI scale 0 (auto).
# Run: tools/autopilot.sh tools/autopilot/holo-cards.txt
world autopilot_holo_cards creative 1234
waitworld
cmd gamerule send_command_feedback false
cmd gamerule spawn_mobs false
cmd gamerule awaken:fauna_spawning false
cmd time set 6000
cmd weather clear
look 20 4
gui 0

wait 200
screen close
cmd fill ~-12 ~ ~-12 ~12 ~3 ~12 air replace minecraft:short_grass
cmd fill ~-12 ~ ~-12 ~12 ~3 ~12 air replace minecraft:tall_grass
cmd kill @e[type=!minecraft:player,distance=..64]
wait 40
