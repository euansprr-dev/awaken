# The holo inventory (docs/modules/holo.md "The inventory", "The calm HUD, tier 1"): E's screen frozen along its
# unfold ("holo at <ms>" is K-menu choreography time; the inventory plays it 2.5x faster, so 450 = 180 ms): the wrist
# rising, the 3D panel growing out of the band and landing on the flat screen's rectangle, the crossfade to the flat
# face (370-450: the landed panel and the flat face must agree to a pixel; "holo log" prints both rectangles), then
# open at rest over sky, grass and night; logs carried out of the hotbar and dropped into the crafting grid (the
# result lit) and the result shift-clicked into the pack, all through the screen's own input path; the recipe book
# opened and closed (the inventory moves, the panel follows); the 100 ms fold; third person (the beams from your
# character's wrist) and what others see; the vanilla style and the flat look (vanilla's own screen, untouched); the
# HUD faded when idle and woken by a hit; then frame times with the inventory open and closed. Survival (creative
# keeps its own screen). GUI scale 0 (auto). Sound is muted under the autopilot: listen by hand (docs/handoff).
# A "shot" lands about 3 ticks after its line and the next line waits 10 more; "shotnow" shoots this tick.
# Run: tools/autopilot.sh tools/autopilot/holo-inventory.txt
