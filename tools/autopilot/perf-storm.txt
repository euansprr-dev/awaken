# The Rolling Storm's frame cost (Euan, 29 Oct: "loses a good 10 fps when it's happening and stutters"; SPEC §21.6
# "Its cost"). One spot and one view, measured with the storm and without it:
#   storm_on   60 s: a storm forced, the camera 70 blocks from its centre on the edge of its rain (local weather, close
#              strikes, flashes), looking up at it, still (so Far Sight may serve its cached horizon);
#   storm_off  30 s: the same camera and view once the storm is calmed away and its weather handed back.
# The storm comes first because only `happen` (random spot) and `near` (the camera relative to it) can place it, and
# both builds have them; the clear sky is measured at the camera the storm left. Each perf line logs avg_fps, p50,
# p95, p99 (the 1% low) and max (the worst frame), and the server tick.
# Sound: tours mute the master volume, and a sound at zero volume is skipped before Sound Physics sees it. `opt volume
# 1` (1%, near silent) lets sounds play so its work is in the numbers; a build without that option (before pack/storm-perf)
# warns and stays muted, so its numbers carry less work than the after run's.
# Run alone on the Mac, before (build/awaken-v1 68d3f77b) and after (pack/storm-perf), Euan's pack without comfort
# (Dynamic FPS would cap a background window):
#   PACK=perf,feel,sound,world TECTONIC=awakened RD=24 LENS=cinematic SLOT_WHO=perf-storm \
#   /Volumes/MEDIA/Awaken-carillon/tools/carillon/slot.sh tour tools/autopilot.sh tools/autopilot/perf-storm.txt 900
# Read: grep "perf storm_" run-autopilot/logs/latest.log
world autopilot_perfstorm creative 20261029
waitworld
gui 0
cmd gamerule send_command_feedback false
cmd gamerule advance_time false
cmd gamerule spawn_mobs false
cmd gamerule awaken:fauna_spawning false
cmd weather clear 1000000
wait 100
screen close
window 1280 720
info
hud off
lens cinematic
opt volume 1
cmd gamemode spectator
cmd awaken living calm
# open land away from the city and its gate pillars, at noon
cmd awaken living goto greenwild_woods
wait 200
cmd tp @s ~400 ~ ~300
wait 300
cmd time set 6000
# ---- the storm: built (12 s), the camera on the edge of its rain, looking up at it
cmd awaken living happen rolling_storm
wait 300
cmd awaken living near 70
wait 200
cmd tp @s ~ ~ ~ ~ -25
wait 300
perf storm_on 60
# ---- the same camera and view, the storm calmed away and its weather handed back
cmd awaken living calm
wait 400
perf storm_off 30
info
quit
