# Room between the blue rope sites (SPEC §17.11 "Room between them"): the census both ways from the seed and the land's
# own biomes, then the seam. A Tether Spire spot that today's rule makes and the new one passes over is made under

# the census of what really stands must find nothing off the grid or cut off, and the Way Up's needle for the climb must
