# Measurement only (the look-steady-60 plan; Euan, 10 Nov: "You can tour on my Dell 4k screen btw for FPS data").
# A copy of his own world, fullscreen on the Dell with his settings (tools/steady60-tour.sh copies his config in):
# the VFX Look as he plays it (vsync on, 60 cap), then uncapped with one ImmersiveVFX feature off at a time, then
# Lens's tiers at the same spot, then flights. Every perf window also writes its frames, in order, to
# run-autopilot/perf-traces/<label>.txt; pixels gpu gives the GPU's world pass.
#   tools/steady60-tour.sh tools/autopilot/steady60-dell.txt
world copied
