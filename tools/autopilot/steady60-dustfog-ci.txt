# Dust and fog as air (Euan, playing, 12 Nov, of the Deep Tide's fog in a wood at night and of a stampede he stood in:
# "it makes everything look very pale and flat instead of actually looking good like a fog or something ... even
# without the shaders it looks bad as well. So I think it's an issue with those textures themselves"). The happenings'
# dust and fog were flat discs of one fixed colour, up to eleven blocks across, drawn twice over. Lens now draws each as

# drawn once and fade as the camera comes into them.
