# Realms' world has the overworld's sky, so the Air and its water (SPEC §22 "Glasswater") are on there, and every Realm
# look gives Lens's light no sun. A pool of one block of water under a wax roof at Brood Burrow's way in (a Hive Realm),
# seen three ways: Lens off (the game's own frame), Cinematic, and Cinematic without the Air's water (the probe chain
# cinematic_comp_water). First an open-air pool, so water outside gates is seen too: across it, down at the bank, and
# under a plank bridge.
