# A power's look over the place's own (Euan, playing, 12 Nov, with two pictures of one deck, the second while holding
# a beam: "is intentional, that everything is way less bright when you're using the power."). A cast's look went
# through Lens's moment channel, which replaces the place's look and switches the player's grade off: his Vivid
# grade was gone for as long as the beam was held. It is now laid over the place's look, under the grade
# (LensClient.accent, SPEC §22 "A power's look").
# A deck at y 232 (over the cloud layer) with a pool, a lawn and a tree beyond it and open sky, the Vivid grade on,
# in first person. Each round: the look in numbers and a shot at rest, then with a beam held two seconds, then after
# it has let go. First as it was (`lens accent off`), then as built. Then Flow, a look written to drain the world,
# both ways, to show that it still does.
