# The Way Up (SPEC 63.8): the Lessons of Rope and Wings' six moves, the ones a tour can reach without mastery.
# The takeoff's (standing outdoors with flight: the keys held at the top with an arrow, the gold figure rising; then
# looking up, the caps on the ring in the sky; the takeoff made and its card), the flare's (a glide down toward the
# ground), the K rows with a line a move, and a try at the sling's (a wall ahead with nothing to swing from).
# Flight is a dev grant here (no rung is handed down, so no lesson is owed).
# Run on CI: tools/tour-ci.sh tools/autopilot/way-up-moves.txt 1500
world autopilot_way_up_moves survival 4245
