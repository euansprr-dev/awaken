# picked (its card: whose, how far, "Change it"); the card to change it; N's new waypoint card. Then the kin plate on a
# wild Lampwing, a Gnawed Mossback, a Burrowbun at home, then named; the ride line on a Frost Stag (climbing on,
# then hurt) and the stag's plate once you're off.
