# "Metal from the deep" (Between the Gates W2, SPEC §28.3): one short tour, shots only (slot.sh light).
# Under the ground at y -30: a staged chamber with an iron lode on its north wall (lanterns: the lode glints), the
# strike's sparks and its last blow, the Tuning Fork's arc, a heart breaking ("the vein gives", the System naming it, the
# light running out through the rock along the vein, its cracks glowing), the heavy strike's charge and its bore, a
