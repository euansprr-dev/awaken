# Between the Gates W5 (SPEC §28.5), the lead's third look: the reshaped sinkhole (radius 10-17, the lip leaning in,
# vine curtains, the stony way in) from its edge, over its lip looking down the shaft, from a ring partway down and from
# its floor; and one grotto in its own light only (no lamp, no night vision): its pool, its curtain from both sides,
# its geode. goto now passes a place the world lacks or has flooded (logged "[delve] goto passes").
# Run: tools/tour-ci.sh tools/autopilot/world-depth-w5-shaft2.txt 900 LENS=cinematic
world autopilot_world_depth_w5s2 creative 52605260
waitworld
gui 0
cmd gamerule send_command_feedback false
cmd gamerule spawn_mobs false
cmd gamerule awaken:fauna_spawning false
cmd weather clear 1000000
cmd awaken opening skip
wait 100

wait 30
shot w5t_03_sinkhole_from_a_ring
# the floor, looking up the cleft at the sky
cmd awaken delve place go cleft view
wait 30
